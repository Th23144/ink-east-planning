## 1. Why the relationship exists（关系为什么存在）

Ink & East（墨与东方）and Spatial Flow（空间流）are independent products/projects（独立产品 / 项目）.

Their relationship is justified not by shared development convenience and not by a content-to-store funnel, but by a real complementary boundary:

- Knowledge / Culture / Provenance / Research（知识 / 文化 / 来源溯源 / 研究）
- ↔
- Object / Material / Craft / Use / Space / Commerce（物件 / 材料 / 工艺 / 使用 / 空间 / 商业）

The potential value is bidirectional:

- Ink & East（墨与东方）can deepen the historical, cultural, source and research context around real-world objects/practices（现实物件 / 实践）;
- Spatial Flow（空间流）can provide a recurring real-world material/practice environment（现实材料 / 实践环境）for some research, object, craft, use and care questions.

Neither side is required for the other to exist.

---

## 2. Ink & East（墨与东方）is a platform with many possible real-world actors

Spatial Flow（空间流）is **not** the only real-world outlet（唯一现实出口）for Ink & East（墨与东方）.

Ink & East（墨与东方）may interact or collaborate with many subjects, including:

- researchers（研究者）;
- museums / archives / libraries（博物馆 / 档案馆 / 图书馆）;
- universities（大学）;
- workshops / makers（工坊 / 制作者）;
- local communities（地方社区）;
- publishers / cultural organizations（出版机构 / 文化组织）;
- brands / companies（品牌 / 企业）;
- Spatial Flow（空间流）.

Therefore the relationship cannot be modeled as:

```text
Ink & East
  ↓
Spatial Flow
  ↓
Real World
```

Spatial Flow（空间流）is one potentially important long-term actor among a broader network.

---

## 3. Spatial Flow（空间流）inside Ink & East（墨与东方）platform context

When Spatial Flow（空间流）speaks or publishes inside Ink & East（墨与东方）platform context, the correct semantic is:

> **Spatial Flow（空间流）is speaking as Spatial Flow（空间流）.**

It is not Ink & East Editorial（墨与东方编辑部）speech.

Conceptually, Spatial Flow（空间流）may participate like another legitimate Person / Organization（个人 / 组织）subject using the platform's applicable publishing/community capabilities.

This does **not** yet lock:
- final onboarding UX（最终入驻体验）;
- advertising product（广告产品）;
- brand-account packaging（品牌账户包装）;
- paid placement（付费展示）;
- exact community distribution mechanics（具体社区分发机制）.

Those examples are explanatory, not implementation commitments.

---

## 4. No hidden platform privilege（无隐藏平台特权）

A deeper relationship with Ink & East（墨与东方）must not automatically give Spatial Flow（空间流）:

- higher organic recommendation ranking（更高自然推荐排序）;
- automatic Issue Inclusion（自动议题收录）;
- automatic Work Recognition（自动作品认可）;
- Contributor Qualification（贡献者资格）;
- source/truth authority（来源 / 真相权威）;
- editorial endorsement（编辑背书）;
- governance power（治理权）.

When Spatial Flow（空间流）publishes through ordinary platform surfaces, its content remains attributable to Spatial Flow（空间流） and is governed by the same applicable product/content rules as comparable actors.

---

## 5. Where the “special relationship” may legitimately live（特殊关系可以合法存在在哪里）

The specialness should primarily exist outside hidden platform privilege, for example in:

- standing strategic cooperation（长期战略合作）;
- recurring research collaboration（持续研究合作）;
- material / craft / object projects（材料 / 工艺 / 物件项目）;
- joint documentation / restoration / experimentation（联合记录 / 修复 / 实验）;
- explicit cross-product relationships between knowledge objects and physical-world objects/projects（知识对象与现实物件 / 项目的显式跨产品关系）;
- licensing / project / commercial agreements（授权 / 项目 / 商业协议）;
- possible ownership / parent-brand / group relationship（可能的所有权 / 母品牌 / 集团关系）, still unresolved.

Thus:

> **Special Relationship（特殊关系） != Platform Privilege（平台特权）**

---

## 6. Q1 conclusion（Q1 结论）

The relationship has sufficient genuine value to justify continued architecture discussion.

But the current result is not “one ecosystem product（一个生态产品）”.

The next question is:

> **What kind of persistent special relationship, if any, should exist between the two independent products when no joint project is currently active?（当没有具体联合项目正在进行时，这两个独立产品之间是否仍应存在一种持续性的特殊关系？如果存在，它究竟是什么？）**
