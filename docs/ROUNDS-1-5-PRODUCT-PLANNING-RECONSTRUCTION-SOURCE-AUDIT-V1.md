# 8. Important distinctions confirmed / 重要区分

The audit confirms the reconstruction must preserve these distinctions:

1. **Reading experience exists** ≠ **Reading Room product exists**.
2. **Community exists** ≠ old isolated forum model.
3. **Membership exists as a subject** ≠ old VIP content package exists.
4. **Recognition destination/function exists** ≠ one permanent monolithic board shape/name.
5. **Profile/identity experience exists** ≠ prestige badges should dominate content surfaces.
6. **Save / Follow / Reading History / private notes exist as primitives/candidates** ≠ they must live in one specific container.
7. **Services appear in historical/future planning** ≠ their old implementation/package is already revalidated.
