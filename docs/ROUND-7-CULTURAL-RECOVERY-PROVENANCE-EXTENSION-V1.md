# Round 7（第七轮）— Cultural Recovery Provenance Extension V1（文化寻回来源溯源扩展 V1）

> **Status（状态）:** TARGETED CROSS-ROUND EXTENSION（定向跨轮扩展）
> **Applies to（适用于）:** Round 7（第七轮）Knowledge Graph & Provenance（知识图谱与来源溯源）
> **Implementation（实现）:** NOT AUTHORIZED（未授权）

## E1 — Recovery Provenance（寻回过程溯源）

Where material, the platform may preserve provenance-bearing events such as:

Discovery（发现）
→ Lead Submission（线索提交）
→ Holder Contact（联系持有人）
→ Temporary Deposit / Loan（临时寄存 / 借用）
→ Documentation（记录）
→ Condition Assessment（状态评估）
→ Digitization（数字化）
→ Restoration / Reconstruction（修复 / 重建）
→ Research / Translation / Annotation（研究 / 翻译 / 注释）
→ Institutional Referral（机构转介）
→ Physical Handoff / Accession（实体移交 / 入藏）
→ Public Release（公开发布）.

Not every case requires every event.

## E2 — Custody and rights axes remain separate（保管与权利维度分离）

The following must remain distinguishable where relevant:

- Owner（所有者）;
- Holder（持有人）;
- Custodian（保管者）;
- Holding Institution（收藏 / 保管机构）;
- Temporary Custody（临时保管）;
- Donor（捐赠者）;
- Depositor（寄存者）;
- Receiving Institution（接收机构）;
- Rights Holder（权利人）;
- Digital Provider（数字提供方）.

No one field such as “source owner（资料所有者）” may collapse these relations.

## E3 — Physical handoff does not erase recovery history（实体移交不抹除寻回历史）

When a physical original（实体原件）moves to a Qualified Institution（合格机构）, permissible Recovery Provenance（寻回溯源）, Recovery Attribution（寻回归因）and transformation history（处理 / 转化历史）remain reconstructable.

## E4 — Donation / Deposit do not imply publication permission（捐赠 / 寄存不等于公开授权）

Donation（捐赠）, Deposit（寄存）, physical custody（实体保管）and public reuse permission（公开再利用授权）are separate facts.

Existing Round 7（第七轮）Rights（权利）, Privacy（隐私）, Cultural Sensitivity（文化敏感性）and Item-level specificity（单件级优先）rules continue to control.

## E5 — Ink & East（墨与东方）is not final legal authority

Ink & East（墨与东方）may document, research, digitize, coordinate and refer material, but does not itself create final legal ownership（法律所有权）, cultural-property classification（文化财产认定）or institutional acceptance（机构接收）by assertion.

Legal / jurisdiction-specific determinations（法律 / 法域具体认定）remain deferred to appropriate professional/institutional processes.
