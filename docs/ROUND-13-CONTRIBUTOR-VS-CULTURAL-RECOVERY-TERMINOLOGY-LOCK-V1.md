# Round 13（第十三轮）— Contributor vs Cultural Recovery Terminology Lock V1
# “贡献者”与“文化寻回”术语分离锁定 V1

> **Status（状态）:** USER-CONFIRMED TERMINOLOGY LOCK（用户确认术语锁定）
> **Implementation（实现）:** NOT AUTHORIZED（未授权）

## 1. Reserved term（保留术语）

**Contributor（贡献者）** is reserved for the existing Round 4（第四轮）qualification identity system.

Canonical architecture（规范架构）:

> Contributor Qualification（贡献者资格） + Expertise Scope（专业范围）

It describes a person whose scoped competence / sustained creation-research-practice capability（范围化能力 / 持续创作、研究、实践能力）has sufficient evidence under the platform qualification process.

Do not reuse Contributor（贡献者）as a generic synonym for “someone who helped”.

## 2. Cultural-recovery terminology（文化寻回术语）

The later cultural-recovery system must avoid generic Contribution / 贡献 vocabulary as its architecture name.

Current working umbrella（当前工作总名）:

> **Open Cultural Recovery（开放文化寻回）**

Use:
- Recovery Participant（寻回参与者）;
- Recovery Role（寻回角色）;
- Recovery Attribution（寻回归因）.

Use specific roles whenever possible:
- Discoverer（发现者）;
- Source Provider（资料提供者）;
- Knowledge Provider（知识提供者）;
- Material Holder（资料持有人）;
- Donor（捐赠者）;
- Depositor（寄存者）;
- Translator（翻译者）;
- Research Collaborator（研究协作者）;
- Digitization Participant（数字化参与者）;
- Institutional Connector（机构联络促成者）.

## 3. Hard anti-confusion rule（硬防混淆规则）

> **Contributor（贡献者） != Recovery Participant（寻回参与者）**

> **Contributor Qualification（贡献者资格） != Recovery Role / Attribution（寻回角色 / 归因）**

A single person may hold both relationships independently, but one never automatically creates the other.
