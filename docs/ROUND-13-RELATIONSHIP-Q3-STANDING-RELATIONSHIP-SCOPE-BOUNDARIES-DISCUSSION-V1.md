## 1. Purpose（目的）

Standing Strategic Relationship（持续战略关系）is now confirmed.

The next task is to define its scope so that “strategic relationship（战略关系）” does not become an unlimited excuse for hidden sharing, preferential treatment or structural dependency.

The relationship must distinguish:

1. what can be persistent（什么可以持续存在）;
2. what must require explicit project-level agreement（什么必须逐项目明确）;
3. what must remain independent by default（什么默认必须独立）.

---

## 2. Candidate standing inclusions（候选持续性内容）

These are discussion candidates（讨论候选）, not locked decisions.

### I1 — Strategic awareness（战略互相了解）
Each side may understand the other's long-term direction, capabilities and relevant upcoming work where appropriate.

### I2 — Collaboration priority / familiarity（合作优先评估 / 熟悉度）
When a relevant project appears, each side may naturally consider whether the other is a suitable collaborator before treating the market as entirely unknown.

This is not exclusivity（排他）.

### I3 — Recurring knowledge ↔ object bridge（持续知识 ↔ 物件桥接）
Where justified, Ink & East（墨与东方）knowledge objects and Spatial Flow（空间流）objects/projects may reference each other through explicit, attributable relationships.

### I4 — Research / material / craft collaboration channel（研究 / 材料 / 工艺合作通道）
The two products may maintain a recurring path for joint research, testing, documentation, restoration, reconstruction or material-practice work.

### I5 — Cooperation history（合作历史）
Prior projects and relationships may remain visible/reconstructable rather than being treated as isolated transactions with no continuity.

### I6 — Conflict / disclosure awareness（利益冲突 / 披露意识）
Because the relationship is persistent, both products may need explicit rules for when that relationship becomes materially relevant to editorial, research or commercial disclosure.

## 3. Candidate project-specific items（候选逐项目决定事项）

These should likely never be assumed from the standing relationship alone:

- ownership of outputs（成果所有权）;
- copyright / license（著作权 / 授权）;
- revenue sharing（收益分成）;
- procurement / supply（采购 / 供应）;
- pricing（定价）;
- exclusivity（独家 / 排他）;
- co-branding（联合品牌）;
- use of each other's trademarks（商标使用）;
- commercial promotion（商业推广）;
- access to non-public research or data（非公开研究 / 数据访问）;
- physical custody（实体保管）;
- publication rights（发布权）.

Each requires explicit agreement（明确协议） where relevant.

## 4. Candidate default independence boundaries（候选默认独立边界）

The following are strong candidates to remain independent by default:

- Editorial Judgment（编辑判断）;
- Work Recognition（作品认可）;
- Recommendation（推荐）;
- Knowledge / Source Authority（知识 / 来源权威）;
- Contributor Qualification（贡献者资格）;
- Governance（治理）;
- Membership（会员）;
- Account（账户）;
- Personal Data（个人数据）;
- Payment / Orders（支付 / 订单）;
- Customer Support Responsibility（客服责任）;
- Brand Voice（品牌声音）;
- Legal Responsibility（法律责任）.

---

## 5. The key principle to test（需要确认的核心原则）

A possible relationship principle is:

> **Persistent cooperation, explicit crossover, independent authority.（合作关系长期存在；跨界行为必须显式；权威与责任保持独立。）**

This would mean:
- the relationship is real and durable;
- cooperation may be deep;
- but any moment where one product affects the other's editorial, commercial, data, rights or user experience boundary must be explicit and attributable.

This principle is locked by `ROUND-13-RELATIONSHIP-Q3-RESOLUTION-SCOPE-BOUNDARIES-V1.md`.
