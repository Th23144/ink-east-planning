# Round 12 Workshop B — Hardening Addendum
# 第十二轮工作坊 B——加固补充

> **Status:** CONTROLLING FOR WORKSHOP B DISCUSSION BOUNDARIES（控制工作坊 B 的讨论边界）  
> **Target:** `INK-EAST-ROUND-12-MEMBERSHIP-READING-ROOM-WORKSHOP-B.md`  
> **Implementation:** NOT AUTHORIZED（未授权实现）

The following safeguards are adopted as **workshop-boundary hardening**, not as acceptance of any candidate Membership benefit.

## R12-BH1 — Published-result rule / 发布结果规则
Member-only live/interactive experiences may exist, but normal editorial/knowledge material later published from them follows the public-content baseline unless a separate rights/privacy/legal state applies.

## R12-BH2 — No artificial quota poverty / 不得人为制造免费额度贫困
A paid capacity increase must correspond to genuine scarcity, abuse resistance, operational cost or differentiated workflow value. Do not deliberately make the free baseline unusable merely to force conversion.

## R12-BH3 — Supporter-marker firewall / 支持者标记防火墙
Supporter status, tenure, visual treatment or member history may describe the commercial/support relationship only. They do not create positive quality evidence for Recommendation（推荐）, Recognition（作品认可）, Contributor Qualification（贡献者资格）, Reviewer Trust（评审信任） or Governance Authority（治理权力）.

## R12-BH4 — Editorial-outcome firewall / 编辑结果防火墙
Priority reply, office hours, salons, roundtables, panels, polls and other member participation benefits create access/opportunity only. They do not guarantee publication, favorable editorial judgment, Recognition, nomination or governance outcome.

## R12-BH5 — Non-member availability floor / 非会员可用性底线
If Membership offers priority/capacity in Ask, events or separately paid services, a legitimate non-member route must remain meaningful. Member priority must not starve non-members indefinitely.

## R12-BH6 — Unified Community identity / 统一社区身份
Any member reading circle or temporary member space remains inside the Round 8 unified Community System（统一社区系统） and uses the same account/identity/governance substrate. Membership does not create a second prestige social graph.

## R12-BH7 — Private-data purpose limitation / 私人数据目的限制
Private notes, reading maps, history and workspace data are not public-profile, Recommendation, commercial-targeting or model-training inputs by default. Any broader use requires separately justified policy/consent and remains subject to Round 9 privacy/lifecycle rules.

## R12-BH8 — Cancellation/data continuity / 取消会员后的数据连续性
Membership expiry/cancellation may remove paid capabilities, but must not silently destroy user-owned private notes, collections or other durable personal data. Exact read/export/migration behavior is deferred to lifecycle design.

## R12-BH9 — Data portability floor / 数据可携带底线
Privacy/legal/basic portability obligations are not premium lock-in tools. Advanced export convenience may be differentiated, but the platform cannot withhold necessary user-data access solely to force Membership retention.

## R12-BH10 — Scarce-resource economics / 稀缺资源经济性
Human-time, service-credit, physical-goods, event-seat and priority-SLA benefits cannot be promised as final Membership entitlements before capacity/cost/economic review.

---

These hardening rules constrain later discussion. They do **not** select B-C1…B-C6 or approve any candidate benefit.
