## 1. Core distribution principle（主动分发核心原则）

> **Adult-themed content may exist; the platform must not push it at users by surprise or grow its general cultural feeds around it.（成人题材可以存在，但平台不得向未主动选择的用户突然推送，也不得让普通文化信息流围绕成人内容增长。）**

Adult-focused sexual/erotic literature and other mature content should **not receive the same ordinary passive-recommendation eligibility** as general literary, historical, scholarly or artistic content merely because its engagement scores are high（成人主题作品不能仅凭点击率高，就获得与一般文学、历史、学术、艺术作品同等的普通被动推荐资格）.

This is a **distribution eligibility boundary（分发资格边界）**, not a statement that adult-themed writers or works are inferior, wrong, immoral, incapable of quality or automatically excluded from any future separate formal Recognition（作品认可） consideration（不是宣告成人作者低劣 / 不道德 / 永远不能获得质量认可）.

**Adult literary publishing now confirmed at category level（成人文学发表原则已确认）:** An adult may ordinarily publish lawful adult-themed fiction/prose, including comparatively direct sexual description（成年用户原则上可以发表合法成人题材文学，包括较为直接的性描写）. This permission is for **publication**, not universal Home / Recommendation exposure（这只代表允许发表，不代表获得普通首页 / 推荐资格）. Applicable reader age, display/preview and precise illegal-content boundaries remain to be specified（年龄访问、展示预览及违法内容边界仍待细化）.

## 2. No surprise for users who have not chosen adult browsing（从未主动选择成人浏览者不得遭遇意外推送）

For a user who has **not explicitly chosen** to browse adult/erotic-focused material:
- Ordinary Home / For You（首页 / 为你推荐）, generalized Explore（探索）, Trending（热门） and mixed recommendation rails must **not inject adult-focused material** as the 20th, 200th or any other surprise item（任何时候不得突然混入成人主题作品）.
- Do not treat a single click, incidental exposure, a search snippet, a followed author's unrelated post, or implicit high click-through as durable affirmative consent to insert adult material（单次点击、偶然曝光、搜索片段、关注作者或高点击率不等于主动授权）.
- Recommendation fallback, cold-start, experimentation, personalization, quality boosting, popularity, Recognition signals or commercial demand must not override the no-surprise boundary（冷启动、兜底、实验、热榜、个性化、优质作品信号、商业需求不得绕过）.
- Content cards, thumbnails, snippets, embeds, notifications and related-item previews should preserve this boundary, not merely the destination page（卡片、缩略图、摘要、嵌入、通知和相关推荐同样不能意外曝光）.

## 3. Repeated voluntary adult reading does not justify takeover of the cultural home（长期主动阅读也不使普通首页成人化）

Even when a user frequently reads adult-themed works:
- Their reading history does **not automatically** rewrite the default mixed cultural homepage into an adult feed（不能仅凭浏览记录把综合文化首页变成成人信息流）.
- Keep adult-focused discovery primarily **intentional and distinct** from general cultural recommendations（成人相关内容发现以用户主动进入的独立情境为主）.
- A dedicated adult-content search/category/subscription-like *discovery view* may be considered later if explicitly chosen, but is **not** hereby authorized as a paid creator subscription product（可另议用户主动进入的分类 / 搜索界面；不等于批准成人付费订阅）.
- Detailed rules for whether and how explicitly opted-in users may encounter adult material in general Home remain **unsettled**; the user has already confirmed it must remain restrained and never crowd out the platform's cultural mission（已明确即使主动读成人内容也必须克制，不得挤占平台文化定位，但具体个性化设计尚未确定）.

## 4. Do not equate nudity with adult-focused entertainment（人体裸体不自动等于色情娱乐）

**User's explicit example（用户举例）:** Michelangelo's **David（米开朗基罗《大卫》雕像）** is meaningful art-history subject matter and should not automatically be restricted as an adult-entertainment work merely for depicting the nude body（《大卫》雕像因艺术史地位及真实语境，不应仅因裸体被归入普通成人娱乐内容限制）.

A faithful, context-rich museum/art-history/sculpture/cultural-education presentation may retain eligibility for **ordinary art/cultural discovery（正常艺术 / 文化内容发现）**, subject to appropriate presentation and applicable age/legal requirements, rather than inheriting restrictions for sexually focused adult material（艺术史展示可按艺术 / 文化语境呈现，仍遵守适当展示与法域年龄要求）.

**Do not invent an 'art' loophole（不能用“艺术”二字绕过边界）:** Judge observable material and framing—what the work actually depicts, focal cropping/thumbnails, language, genre, narrative purpose, source attribution and whether it is being presented as art-history scholarship versus sexually explicit entertainment（考虑实际画面、局部裁切 / 缩略图、描述、体裁、原始来源和整体情境）. A work's self-selected “art（艺术）” tag alone is not a waiver（自行选择艺术标签不自动豁免）。

Artistic importance is **not a mandatory creator credential, prestige tier, museum-membership requirement or universal censor's taste test（艺术价值不是作者身份门槛、名望等级、博物馆资格或主观审美考试）**. This art/content-class boundary should not indirectly force ordinary creators into high-level formal Recognition review（不能倒逼普通创作者接受高层作品认可审核）.

## 5. Compatible architectural boundaries（与既有架构保持一致）
- Round 10 ranking/retrieval systems already distinguish **Eligibility（资格门槛）** from Ranking（排序）, and Recommendation（推荐） from truth/Recognition. Adult-focused exposure restrictions should work as **surface- and intent-specific eligibility/safety limits**, not another popularity weight（成人内容普通推荐限制属于先判断能否展示，而非降低几点推荐分）.
- Round 8 Community（社区） source content and separate presentation/projection allow a user to publish a legitimate work without promising it universal homepage distribution（允许发表不等于保证全站首页曝光）.
- The user's proactive adult search/read decision is not general consent to every unrelated surface（用户主动寻找成人题材不等于所有页面都允许自动推荐）.
- No dedicated adult paid subscriptions, paywalled intimate image business, paid intimate communication or OnlyFans-like commerce（长期排除成人内容专门商业化，与允许内容存在互不冲突）.
- Normal artistic nudes should not be mechanically promoted as pornographic content, nor should overtly erotic media claim universal art-history discovery eligibility（防止错杀经典艺术与色情内容借“艺术”标签进入普通推荐）.
