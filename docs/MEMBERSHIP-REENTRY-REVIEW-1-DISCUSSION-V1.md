---

## 2. New evidence from Round 13（第十三轮新增证据）

### E1 — Platform monetization does not require Membership（平台商业化不依赖会员）

Round 13（第十三轮） now supports:
- advertising（广告）;
- sponsorship（赞助）;
- Paid Amplification（付费加热）;
- Vertical Partner Economy（垂直合作生态）;
- project / institutional monetization（项目 / 机构商业化）;
- future Level 2–3 Commercial Conversion（第二至第三级商业转化） where justified.

None of these requires a recurring consumer Membership（持续消费者会员） product.

### E2 — Spatial Flow relationship does not create a Membership need（空间流关系没有产生会员必要性）

Ink & East ↔ Spatial Flow（墨与东方 ↔ 空间流） now has a Standing Strategic Relationship（持续战略关系）, but:
- accounts remain independent by default;
- personal data remains independent by default;
- no cross-product subscription bundle is confirmed;
- no recurring cross-product member benefit is currently identified.

Therefore the relationship does not currently justify Membership（会员）.

### E3 — No validated recurring first-party service bundle emerged（未出现经验证的持续第一方服务包）

Round 13（第十三轮） did not identify a necessary recurring first-party service catalogue.

Custom Reading（定制解读）and Custom Ebook Studio（定制电子书工作室） remain rejected.

Commission（约稿） remains historical / superseded terminology（历史 / 已取代术语）.

### E4 — Commercial organizations create B2B value, not necessarily consumer subscription value（商业组织产生企业间价值，但不等于消费者订阅价值）

The strongest new commercial evidence is around:
- high-intent vertical audiences（高意图垂直受众）;
- organization/brand participation（机构 / 品牌参与）;
- paid distribution（付费分发）;
- leads / booking / transaction enablement（线索 / 预订 / 交易承载）.

This strengthens the business model without proving a consumer Membership（会员） product.

### E5 — Economic Commitment Signal（经济承诺信号） still does not require Membership（会员）

Round 13（第十三轮） produced no evidence that the existing Economic Commitment Signal（经济承诺信号） must be implemented through recurring Membership（持续会员）.

The anti-abuse/capability mechanism remains independently valid.

---

## 3. Preliminary judgment（初步判断）

Current evidence is **insufficient to justify reopening standalone recurring Membership（持续订阅会员） now**.

The strongest current architecture is:

> public Open Core（开放核心）
> + platform / partner monetization（平台 / 合作方商业化）
> + optional future paid products where real need emerges（真实需求出现时再增加可选收费产品）
> + independent Economic Commitment Signal（独立经济承诺信号）

rather than:

> invent Membership benefits now because the platform needs a subscription product.
