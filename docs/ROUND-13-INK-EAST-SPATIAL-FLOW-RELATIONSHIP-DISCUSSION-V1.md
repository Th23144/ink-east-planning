# 0. What is already fixed（已经确定什么）

The following are not open for reinterpretation in this discussion:

1. Ink & East（墨与东方）and Spatial Flow（空间流）are **not one product（不是同一个产品）**.
2. They are **not one Shared Platform（不是一个共享产品平台）** merely because current development occurs in one repository/context（同一仓库 / 开发上下文）.
3. They may have a future Cooperation Relationship（合作关系）, but that relationship must be explicitly designed.
4. Neither product automatically inherits the other product's:
   - Account（账户）;
   - Identity（身份）;
   - Data（数据）;
   - Search（搜索）;
   - Recommendation（推荐）;
   - Payment（支付）;
   - Membership（会员）;
   - Governance（治理）;
   - editorial / commercial authority（编辑 / 商业权威）.
5. Custom Reading（定制解读）and Custom Ebook Studio（定制电子书工作室）are removed and do not define the relationship.
6. Open Cultural Recovery（开放文化寻回）belongs to Ink & East（墨与东方）architecture and does not itself define the Spatial Flow（空间流）relationship.

# 1. What “relationship” can mean（“关系”可能包含什么）

A complete relationship discussion must separate at least seven layers instead of answering with one vague label.

## Layer A — Mission / Worldview Relationship（使命 / 世界观关系）
Do the two products share:
- only some subject matter（部分主题）;
- a broader cultural / spatial worldview（更广的文化 / 空间世界观）;
- a common parent-brand story（共同母品牌叙事）;
- or merely occasional thematic overlap（偶发主题重叠）?

This layer is about meaning, not infrastructure.

## Layer B — Brand Relationship（品牌关系）
Possible future structures include:
- completely independent brands（完全独立品牌）;
- sister brands（姊妹品牌）;
- one endorsed by the other（品牌背书关系）;
- both under a future parent brand（未来总品牌下的两个品牌）;
- campaign/project-level co-branding only（仅活动 / 项目级联合品牌）.

No option is currently selected.

## Layer C — Product Relationship（产品关系）
Questions:
- Can one product reference the other?
- Can a content experience lead to a commerce experience?
- Can Spatial Flow（空间流）use Ink & East（墨与东方）research/context?
- Can Ink & East（墨与东方）cover objects/material culture without becoming a shopping funnel?
- Which interactions are collaboration and which are contamination?

## Layer D — Commercial Relationship（商业关系）
Possible relationships:
- ordinary referral（普通导流）;
- affiliate / revenue-share（联盟 / 分成）;
- wholesale / supply relationship（批发 / 供应关系）;
- contracted / project-based collaboration（合同制 / 项目制合作）;
- co-developed product（联合开发商品）;
- licensing（授权）;
- sponsorship（赞助）;
- none by default（默认无商业关系）.

These must not be assumed merely because the products are related.

## Layer E — Editorial Independence（编辑独立性）
Ink & East（墨与东方）needs a hard answer to:
- Can Spatial Flow（空间流）pay for placement?
- Can commerce performance influence editorial selection?
- Can products be cited/recommended?
- Must commercial relationships be disclosed?
- Can Ink & East（墨与东方）criticize or decline Spatial Flow（空间流）products?

This layer protects knowledge/editorial integrity.

## Layer F — User / Data Relationship（用户 / 数据关系）
Separate questions:
- same login（同一登录）?
- optional account linking（可选账户关联）?
- no account relationship（完全无账户关系）?
- shared analytics（共享分析）?
- cross-site personalization（跨站个性化）?
- purchase history used by Ink & East（墨与东方是否使用购买历史）?
- reading history used by Spatial Flow（空间流是否使用阅读历史）?

No shared-data assumption is currently authorized.

## Layer G — Technical / Operational Relationship（技术 / 运营关系）
They may:
- share code libraries（共享代码库）;
- share hosting / deployment tooling（共享托管 / 部署工具）;
- share design-system components（共享设计系统组件）;
- share internal admin tooling（共享内部管理工具）;
- remain technically separate（技术完全分离）.

This layer is engineering convenience and does not determine public product identity.

# 2. Relationship is likely asymmetric in some contexts（部分场景关系可能不对称）

A cooperation relationship does not require every link to be symmetrical.

Example:

Ink & East（墨与东方）may create research/context around:
- material culture（物质文化）;
- spatial traditions（空间传统）;
- objects / craft（器物 / 工艺）;
- historical practices（历史实践）.

Spatial Flow（空间流）may independently sell contemporary products inspired by or related to some of those domains.

That does not automatically mean:
- every Ink & East（墨与东方）article links to a product;
- every Spatial Flow（空间流）product is endorsed by Ink & East（墨与东方）;
- commerce owns the editorial narrative;
- editorial research is marketing copy.

---

# 3. Relationship must be explicit at the moment of crossover（跨界时必须显式）

Where one independent product enters the other product's user journey, the user should be able to tell:

- which product they are currently using（当前在哪个产品）;
- whether a link is editorial/contextual or commercial（链接是编辑 / 语境还是商业）;
- whether a recommendation is independent editorial judgment or commercial cooperation（独立编辑判断还是商业合作）;
- which party handles payment / order / support（谁负责支付 / 订单 / 支持）;
- whether data crosses the product boundary（数据是否跨产品边界）.

The relationship should not rely on invisible assumptions.

# 4. Key relationship questions to resolve sequentially（需要按顺序解决的核心问题）

## Q1 — Why should they have a relationship at all?（为什么它们需要存在关系？）
Before designing links, accounts or commercial cooperation, identify the genuine mutual value.

## Q2 — What is the conceptual connection?（概念联系到底是什么？）
Is the connection:
- culture ↔ object（文化 ↔ 物件）;
- knowledge ↔ lived space（知识 ↔ 现实空间）;
- research ↔ contemporary practice（研究 ↔ 当代实践）;
- editorial context ↔ curated goods（编辑语境 ↔ 选物）;
- or something else?

## Q3 — What must remain independent?（什么必须永远独立？）
At minimum candidates include:
- editorial judgment（编辑判断）;
- knowledge/source authority（知识 / 来源权威）;
- commerce transaction（商业交易）;
- Membership（会员）;
- account/data（账户 / 数据）;
- brand voice（品牌声音）.

## Q4 — What kinds of cooperation are actually desirable?（哪些合作值得存在？）
Only after Q1–Q3:
- cross-link（互链）;
- research collaboration（研究合作）;
- co-created content（联合内容）;
- co-created product（联合商品）;
- referral（导流）;
- licensing（授权）;
- physical exhibitions / events（实体展览 / 活动）;
- other forms.

## Q5 — What should the user perceive?（用户应该如何感知两者关系？）
Should users think:
- “two unrelated companies occasionally collaborating（两个无关主体偶尔合作）”;
- “two sister brands（两个姊妹品牌）”;
- “two independent products from one broader cultural ecosystem（一个更大文化生态里的两个独立产品）”;
- another model?

This is a later branding/product decision, not assumed now.
