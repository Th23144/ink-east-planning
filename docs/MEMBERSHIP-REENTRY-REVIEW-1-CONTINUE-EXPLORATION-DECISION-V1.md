## 2. Why the discussion is not finished（为什么讨论还没结束）

The current model still does not answer several core product questions:

- why should support be recurring rather than one-time（为什么支持应持续订阅，而不是一次性支持）;
- what ongoing relationship exists after payment（付款后持续关系到底是什么）;
- what makes Membership（会员） meaningfully different from Donation / Tip / Support（捐赠 / 打赏 / 支持）;
- whether recurring support can remain valuable before ads, partners and mature events exist（在广告、合作方、成熟活动尚未形成前，持续支持是否仍有足够价值）;
- whether any early non-paywall recurring benefit is strong enough without creating artificial scarcity（是否存在不靠知识付费墙、又足够持续的早期价值）.

These must be explored before Review #1（第一次检查） can close.
