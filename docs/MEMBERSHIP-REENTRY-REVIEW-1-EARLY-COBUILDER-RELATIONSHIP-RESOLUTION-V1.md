# 会员重启检查 #1——早期共建者关系结论 V1

## 1. Confirmed core（已确认核心）

Ink & East（墨与东方） recognizes that early platform value is not created only by money.

Therefore:

> **Early Co-builder（早期共建者） is broader than Financial Supporter（资金支持者）.**

Potential early co-building value may come from:
- Early Presence（早期在场）;
- Financial Support（资金支持）;
- Quality Publishing / Knowledge Participation（优质发布 / 知识参与）;
- Network Growth（网络增长）;
- Product Co-building（产品共建）;
- Cultural Recovery / Project Participation（文化寻回 / 项目参与）.

These are independent histories, not one universal score.

## 2. No universal Founder / Co-builder score（不建立统一创始 / 共建分）

The platform should preserve multiple typed historical records rather than collapse them into:
- points（积分）;
- levels（等级）;
- universal prestige（统一声望）;
- Account Weight（账户权重）.

Different future opportunities may consider different relevant histories.

## 3. Special relationship treatment is allowed（允许特殊关系待遇）

Early Co-builder Relationship（早期共建者关系） may legitimately support:
- historical acknowledgment（历史致谢）;
- selected early-access opportunities（部分优先体验机会）;
- product research / feedback invitations（产品研究 / 反馈邀请）;
- project/platform briefings（项目 / 平台简报）;
- anniversary / milestone recognition（周年 / 里程碑纪念）;
- selected event / partner courtesy（部分活动 / 合作生态礼遇）;
- other relationship benefits that are independently justified.

But this relationship does not create:
- Governance Authority（治理权）;
- Editorial Authority（编辑权）;
- Work Recognition（作品认可） advantage;
- Contributor Qualification（贡献者资格）;
- source/truth authority（来源 / 真相权威）;
- hidden Organic Recommendation（自然推荐） priority.

## 4. Economic participation principle（经济参与原则）

Future economic participation should primarily be explored as:

> **actual value-generating activity → reward / compensation（实际价值创造行为 → 奖励 / 报酬）**

rather than:

> **early/supporter status → automatic platform revenue entitlement（元老 / 支持者身份 → 自动获得平台收入分配权）**.

Possible future reward families may include:
- Creator Reward（创作者奖励）;
- Referral Reward（推荐奖励）;
- project compensation（项目报酬）;
- commercial lead reward（商业线索奖励）;
- other contribution-linked compensation（其他与实际价值创造挂钩的报酬）.

These are separate future product/policy subjects.

## 5. Naming principle（命名原则）

“Spiritual Shareholder（精神股东）” may be used as:
- public/community language（公开 / 社区语言）;
- cultural metaphor（文化隐喻）;
- early-stage belonging language（早期归属表达）.

The underlying architecture should use a more precise concept:

> **Early Co-builder Relationship（早期共建者关系）**

This avoids confusing community belonging with legal equity / investment rights（法律股权 / 投资权利）.
