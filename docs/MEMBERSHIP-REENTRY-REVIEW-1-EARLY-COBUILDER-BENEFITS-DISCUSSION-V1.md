## 1. Purpose（目的）

The Early Co-builder Relationship（早期共建者关系） is now confirmed.

The next question is not “who qualifies?” but:

> **What relationship treatment should the platform preserve for people who materially helped build the early platform, without turning that history into hierarchy or authority?（平台应如何对真正参与早期共建的人保留特殊关系待遇，同时不把历史贡献变成等级或权力？）**

---

## 2. Candidate treatment family A — Permanent Historical Recognition（永久历史承认）

Possible forms:
- “early participant / founding-stage participant（早期参与者 / 创始阶段参与者）” history on account/profile where appropriate;
- join-era / milestone history（加入阶段 / 里程碑历史）;
- optional public acknowledgment（可选公开致谢）;
- private archive of the user's early contribution history（个人早期共建历史档案）.

Key rule:
Historical recognition records what happened. It does not create current authority.

---

## 3. Candidate treatment family B — Closer Platform Communication（更近的平台沟通）

Possible forms:
- platform/project briefings（平台 / 项目简报）;
- roadmap/process visibility（路线 / 过程可见性）;
- selected founder-stage retrospectives（部分创始阶段回顾）;
- direct feedback windows（直接反馈窗口）;
- product research invitations（产品研究邀请）.

This is relationship depth, not governance power.

---

## 4. Candidate treatment family C — Priority Access to Limited Opportunities（限量机会优先参与）

Possible forms:
- beta access（测试版优先体验）;
- selected events（部分活动）;
- salons / roundtables（沙龙 / 圆桌）;
- research/project participation（研究 / 项目参与）;
- partner ecosystem courtesy（合作生态礼遇）.

Priority access must remain bounded and must not convert into editorial/Recognition advantage.

---

## 5. Candidate treatment family D — Commemorative / Symbolic Value（纪念 / 象征价值）

Possible forms:
- founding-stage digital badge/mark（创始阶段数字标识）;
- anniversary acknowledgments（周年纪念）;
- physical commemorative objects（实体纪念物）;
- special archival page / history wall（特别档案页 / 历史墙）.

These may create emotional value without creating operational authority.

---

## 6. Candidate treatment family E — Future Economic Opportunity Access（未来经济机会入口）

The platform may later expose eligible Early Co-builders（早期共建者） to:
- creator reward programs（创作者奖励计划）;
- referral programs（推荐计划）;
- paid project opportunities（有偿项目机会）;
- event/research compensation opportunities（活动 / 研究报酬机会）.

Important:
Early Co-builder status may help surface the opportunity, but payment should still be tied to the actual qualifying activity, not the historical label itself.

---

## 7. Anti-hierarchy rule（反等级规则）

Do not convert treatment into:
- “more money = higher founder rank（出钱更多 = 更高创始等级）”;
- “older account = more governance power（账户越老 = 治理权越大）”;
- permanent ranking boost（永久排序加权）;
- universal priority in every product surface（所有产品面统一优先）.

---

## 8. Current decision target（当前决策目标）

The next discussion should determine:
- which treatment families are genuinely desirable;
- which should remain optional/future;
- whether any treatment is missing;
- whether some benefits should apply to all Early Co-builders（早期共建者） while others depend on the specific history/type of contribution（具体共建历史 / 类型）.
