## 1. Why the next question is action scope（为什么下一步是处理范围）

Round 14（第十四轮） has confirmed that enforcement should be scoped rather than globally punitive（处理应尽量限定范围，而不是全局处罚）.

The next question is:

> **When something goes wrong, what exactly should be restricted, for how long, and when is escalation justified?（出现问题时，究竟限制什么、限制多久、什么情况下才应升级处理？）**

A moderation system becomes unfair when it jumps too quickly from one problematic act to whole-account punishment（从一个问题行为直接跳到全账户处罚）.

---

## 2. Proposed action-scope ladder（建议处理范围阶梯）

This is **not** a universal punishment score（不是统一处罚分）.

It is a scope selection model（处理范围选择模型）.

### Scope A — Object-level action（对象级处理）
Target the specific:
- post（帖子）;
- comment（评论）;
- article（文章）;
- upload（上传内容）;
- source/citation entry（来源 / 引用条目）;
- listing or other object（其他具体对象）.

Examples:
- remove one abusive comment（删除一条辱骂评论）;
- correct one false citation（纠正一处错误引用）;
- restrict one fraudulent listing（限制一个欺诈条目）.

Default when the problem is isolated（问题孤立时优先）.

### Scope B — Capability-level action（能力级处理）
Restrict the relevant capability:
- commenting（评论）;
- publishing（发布）;
- messaging（消息）;
- referral/reward participation（推荐 / 奖励参与）;
- organization claiming（组织认领）;
- review/evaluation capability（审查 / 评价能力）.

Use when the problem shows a repeated or capability-specific pattern（当问题表现为重复或特定能力滥用时使用）.

### Scope C — Role / Qualification-level action（角色 / 资格级处理）
Review or remove a specific role/qualification where the issue directly undermines that role.

Examples:
- Contributor Qualification（贡献者资格）;
- Organization Verification（组织验证）;
- reviewer capability（审查者能力）.

The reason must be role-relevant（理由必须与该角色直接相关）.

### Scope D — Account-level action（账户级处理）
Temporary or long-term account suspension（临时或长期账户停用）.

Reserve for:
- severe safety/security abuse（严重安全 / 安全攻击）;
- systemic fraud/manipulation（系统性欺诈 / 操纵）;
- repeated cross-capability abuse（跨多能力持续滥用）;
- evasion after narrower restrictions（规避较窄限制）;
- other cases where narrower scope is clearly insufficient（其他明显无法由更窄处理解决的情况）.

---

## 3. Severity should consider context, not identity prestige（严重程度看上下文，不看身份地位）

Relevant factors may include:
- harm / risk（伤害 / 风险）;
- intent where reasonably knowable（在可合理判断时考虑意图）;
- repetition / pattern（重复 / 模式）;
- scale（规模）;
- reversibility of harm（伤害是否可逆）;
- evasion / manipulation（规避 / 操纵）;
- whether the actor corrected the issue in good faith（是否善意纠正）.

Do not increase or decrease severity because the user is:
- Member（会员）;
- Early Co-builder（早期共建者）;
- Partner（合作方）;
- popular creator（热门创作者）;
- advertiser/commercial customer（广告主 / 商业客户）.

---

## 4. Narrowest effective action principle（最窄有效处理原则）

A candidate governing rule:

> **Use the narrowest action that is sufficient to stop the relevant harm and protect the affected surface.（采用足以停止相关伤害并保护受影响产品面的最窄处理。）**

This does not mean weak enforcement（不等于软弱处理）.

If narrower measures fail, escalation is allowed and may be necessary（较窄措施无效时可以且可能必须升级）.

---

## 5. Time-bounded restrictions should be preferred where appropriate（适当时优先有期限限制）

Temporary restrictions may be preferable when:
- risk is reversible（风险可逆）;
- behavior can reasonably be corrected（行为可合理纠正）;
- permanent action would be disproportionate（永久处理明显过度）.

Long-term/permanent restrictions remain appropriate for severe or structurally incompatible abuse（严重或结构性不兼容的滥用仍可长期 / 永久处理）.

---

## 6. Escalation should preserve reason history（升级处理应保留理由历史）

When action escalates from:
Object（对象） → Capability（能力） → Role（角色） → Account（账户）,

the system should preserve:
- prior relevant actions（此前相关处理）;
- why the narrower action was insufficient（为什么较窄处理不足）;
- what new evidence/pattern justified escalation（什么新证据 / 模式支持升级）.

This prevents mysterious punishment jumps（避免莫名其妙的处罚跳级）.

---

## 7. Current decision set（当前决策题）

1. Should moderation actions explicitly distinguish Object-level（对象级）, Capability-level（能力级）, Role/Qualification-level（角色 / 资格级）, and Account-level（账户级） scope?
2. Should the platform adopt the Narrowest Effective Action（最窄有效处理） principle?
3. Should severity be based on harm, pattern, scale, intent where knowable, reversibility and evasion — not prestige/payment status?
4. Should temporary/time-bounded restrictions be preferred where proportionate, while preserving permanent actions for genuinely severe/systemic cases?
5. Should every major escalation preserve an explainable reason history showing why narrower measures became insufficient?
