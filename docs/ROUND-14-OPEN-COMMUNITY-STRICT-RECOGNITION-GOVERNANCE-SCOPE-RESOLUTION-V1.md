# Round 14（第十四轮）— Open Community / Strict Recognition Governance Scope Resolution V1
# 第十四轮——开放社区与严格作品认可治理适用范围结论 V1

> **Status（状态）:** USER-CONFIRMED / CONTROLLING SCOPE（用户确认 / 控制性适用范围）
> **Implementation（实现）:** NOT AUTHORIZED（未授权）
> **Merge（合并）:** NOT AUTHORIZED（未授权）
> **Applies to（适用）:** Rounds 1–14（第一至十四轮）中普通社区、知识纠错、Candidate（候选）、Work Recognition（作品认可）、原典来源管理、行为治理之间的适用边界
> **Does not settle（不代表决定）:** 18+（成人）内容开放程度、具体成人内容规则、评选报名 / 退出交互、具体事实核验程序、代码实现或历史规则的逐条修订

## 1. Governing intent（控制性意图）
Ink & East（墨与东方） is an **open publishing/discussion platform（开放发表与交流平台）** with **strict, independently bounded high-trust systems（严格且有独立边界的高可信机制）**. Strict Recognition（作品认可） or Canonical Source Fidelity（原典来源忠实） standards **must not leak downward as general compulsory truth verification for all ordinary community content（不得下沉为所有普通社区内容的强制事实核验）**.

Ordinary publishing does not imply the platform endorses its correctness（允许发布不代表平台为内容正确性背书）. User authors retain the ability to express subjective views, fiction, playful narratives, informal anecdotes, criticism, open-ended cultural interpretations and essays（用户可以发表主观观点、虚构故事、非正式经历、评论、开放的文化诠释与随笔） within baseline safety rules（基础安全规则）.

**Scope test（适用判断）:** What is the platform itself explicitly doing here: hosting speech, assessing a work for formal Recognition, preserving/annotating sourced material, or responding to independently consequential harm（平台究竟在承载表达、正式评审认可、维护来源事实，还是处理明确且重大的实际伤害）?

## 2. Distinct governance lanes（四条独立治理路径）

### A. Ordinary Community / User Publication（普通社区 / 用户发表）
- Low-friction ordinary publishing and discussion（普通发表与讨论低门槛）.
- No blanket requirement to prove firsthand travel, every personal recollection, viewpoint or essay（不能普遍要求用户证明亲历旅行、每段回忆、观点或随笔）.
- A critic's disagreement, a report alleging inaccuracy, or a “this is fiction（这是编的）” objection does not automatically initiate scholarly fact-checking, attach public fraud/dispute badges or freeze publication（不同意 / 质疑真实性不自动触发学术核查、公开造假 / 争议标记或停发）.
- Discussion, reply, rebuttal, voluntary correction and author revision are the default response to ordinary disagreements（普通分歧优先讨论、反驳、自愿修订）.
- No formal Work Recognition（作品认可） burden simply because a work becomes popular, an editor links it, or the platform recommends it（不会因为热门、被编辑提及或进入推荐，就自动承担正式评审核验责任）.
- Whether authors may opt in/out of formal Recognition workflow（作者进入 / 退出正式评审的机制） is not decided here（此处未决定）.

### B. Candidate / Formal Recognition Review（候选 / 正式作品认可评审）
- When a **real formal evaluation process** applies, the platform may set **content-type-sensitive** reliability, authenticity, evidence, usefulness, originality and methodological standards（进入真正正式评审时，平台可按作品类型判断真实性、关键证据、实用性、原创性与方法）.
- Verify material claims **when they are relevant to the work's evaluated quality/recognition basis（仅针对影响作品评价 / 认可依据的关键主张）**. No universal proof of every sentence, no ideological viewpoint correctness test（不逐句核验，也不设统一思想标准答案）.
- A travel guide claiming genuine first-hand value may warrant scrutiny of that claim within formal review; an honest desk-research guide or an author's subjective delight is not automatically fraudulent（声称亲历并以此争取评价，可核查关键事实；资料整理、主观好恶不自动算造假）.
- Claims/complaints are not proof; protect against malicious reports, allow proportional reconsideration, reasoned outcomes and meaningful appeal for consequential actions（举报不等于证据，重大处理需适度核查和申诉）.
- Recognition is work-/version-specific; it is not a claim that all the author's other works, opinions or identity are authoritative（认可是作品 / 版本级，不传递成作者 / 其他文章的全局权威）.

### C. Canonical / Source-backed Records（规范 / 原典来源型记录）
- For texts, versions, holdings, provenance, original images and transcriptions, preserve source fidelity, variant witnesses, attribution and corrections（忠实保存古籍、版本、馆藏、来源与转录）.
- Translations, annotations, interpretive essays and ordinary discussion may be closely linked for reading but do not automatically inherit canonical authority（译文、注释、解读、讨论可紧密相连，但不自动继承原典权威）.
- Many interpretations of the same classics may coexist and be debated indefinitely; differences alone are **not** formal dispute/correction cases（千人千解可以长期并存，不因理解不同启动正式争议审理）.
- A voluntarily Recognized（获得作品认可） interpretation remains distinct from canonical source authority（解读获认可仍不等于来源权威）.

### D. Safety / Conduct Baseline（基础安全 / 行为规则）
- Applies across the platform, independently of Candidate/Recognition（适用全站，不依赖候选 / 认可身份）.
- Covers proportionately handled actual safety, rights, privacy and abusive-conduct concerns, e.g. scam/fraud with victims, targeted harassment, impersonation, serious dangerous misinformation where actual risk warrants action, unlawful material, and similarly consequential harm（按影响处理诈骗、骚扰、冒充、严重现实伤害风险、违法内容等）.
- **No categorical rule that mere fictional or embellished ordinary publication is punishable per se（不把普通作品中的虚构 / 夸饰本身一律定为可处罚造假）**.
- Conversely, this openness is **not** a blanket immunity for deliberately harmful or prohibited conduct（开放性也不是违法 / 伤害行为的全面豁免）.
- Actions use the narrowest effective scope, context-based severity, explainable process and appeal where consequential（最窄有效范围、按风险处理、重要决定可解释可申诉）.

## 3. Non-conversion boundaries（不能相互自动转换的边界）
1. `Published（已发布）` != `Verified（事实已核实）` != `Candidate（候选）` != `Recognized（作品认可）` != `Canonical（原典来源）`.
2. `Discussion / disagreement（讨论 / 不同意见）` != `Case / enforcement（审查案件 / 处罚）`.
3. `Recommendation / editorial inclusion / paid amplification（推荐 / 编辑收录 / 付费曝光）` != `Formal Recognition evidence（正式认可证据）`.
4. `Recognition withdrawn（认可撤销）` != `all other work removed / author globally punished（其他作品全部删除 / 全局惩罚）`.
5. `Historical award record（历史授予事实）` != `current award validity（当前认可依据有效性）`; subsequent evidence may invalidate the latter without erasing the former（新证据可使认可失效，但不抹去历史处理记录）.
6. `Interpreted differently（观点不同）` != `Fabricated source / falsely represented verified evidence（伪造来源 / 虚假证据声明）`.

## 4. Repair mandate and precedence（后续修订约束与优先级）
This resolution **supersedes overly broad future interpretations** of earlier Round 14 correction/contested-dispute rules, specifically where those would impose formal evidence requirements on ordinary authors merely due to disagreement or claims of inaccurate personal anecdote（本结论覆盖会因普通观点 / 个人经历质疑而强制审查普通作者的过宽解释）. It does not cancel the five previously confirmed separation/procedural principles when genuinely applicable（不取消适用场景内已确认的权责 / 程序原则）.

It **does not** by itself rewrite the original historical sealed records（不直接重写历史封存记录）. Future surgical correction docs should cite this resolution and the original controlling source.

## 5. Explicitly deferred decisions（明确暂缓 / 待用户决定）

- Exact moderation thresholds, content warning UX（内容提示用户体验）, proof standards for consequential Recognition reviews, user opt-in/out, appeal timing and reviewer staffing（具体阈值、展示、关键证明标准、评选参与机制、申诉时限和人员）.
- Payment processor, advertising and distribution partner eligibility for adult content must be separately checked（成人内容相关支付、广告与分发服务商条件待独立核实）.
