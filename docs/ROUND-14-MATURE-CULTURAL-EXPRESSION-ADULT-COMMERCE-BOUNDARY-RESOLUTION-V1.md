## 1. Core identity（核心定位）
> **Ink & East（墨与东方） is a cultural platform on which lawful adult/mature expression may exist; it is NOT an adult-entertainment platform or adult-content commercial marketplace.（墨与东方是允许合法成熟 / 成人表达存在的文化平台，不是成人娱乐或成人内容商业市场。）**

The user accepts parts of earlier Option B（方案 B） direction but does NOT approve it wholesale（用户部分认可方案 B，并未整体认可细节）. Do not imply that all erotic literature, adult photography, nude imagery or explicit video is automatically permitted from this decision alone（不能据此认定所有情色文学、成人摄影、裸体影像或露骨视频已经批准）。

## 2. Long-term hard non-product direction（长期明确不发展的商业模式）
**Do not build, launch, or enable** product capabilities whose primary purpose is:
- adult/erotic-content-exclusive paid subscriptions or locked adult content collections（专门成人 / 情色内容付费订阅或专属付费内容集合）;
- monetization of paid private/intimate/explicit photos or videos（私密 / 露骨影像付费商业化）;
- paid intimate/adult-only private messaging or personalized erotic content services（付费私密成人互动或个性化情色内容服务）;
- an OnlyFans-like monetization ecosystem for adult performers/content creators（类 OnlyFans 成人创作者商业生态）.

This is a **long-horizon strategic exclusion（长期战略排除）**, not merely “not in initial version（首版暂缓）.” Reconsideration would require an explicit major new product-positioning decision by the user, not a routine growth/advertising experiment or silent feature rollout（重新开放必须由用户明确作出重大的产品定位决定，不能通过常规增长实验或默默上线改变）.

**Scope boundary（边界）:** This prohibition targets *dedicated adult-content commerce*, not automatically the copyright or mere publication of a permitted mature-themed novel, museum-style exhibition, historical study or ordinary lawful artistic work（禁令针对专门成人内容商业模式，不自动否定成熟主题作品的发表或著作权）. Any general commercial policy still must separately comply with platform rules and external service conditions（其他一般商业活动仍须遵守独立政策）。

## 3. No imitation by default（不默认模仿 X 或 Reddit）
X and Reddit are not adopted as adult-content template, rating benchmark or maximum permissiveness target（不以 X / Reddit 的尺度为模板、评分标准或平台最高开放目标）.

This platform may choose stricter **distribution, commercialization and product incentives（分发、商业化与产品激励）** than lawful publication permissiveness（即使允许发表，默认曝光和商业功能可以更克制）.

Preserve the early platform's literary/cultural identity, editorial seriousness and multi-domain knowledge/discussion foundations（维护初期文学 / 文化定位、编辑气质和多领域知识讨论根基）.

## 4. Separate independent policy axes（独立待议轴）
To be decided later, not in this document:
- **What is allowed as expression（允许发表什么）:** non-explicit mature culture, erotic prose, consensual nude art, explicit consensual sexual image/video, etc.;
- **Who may view（谁可看）:** age safeguards, regional restrictions, visibility preferences, default-preview settings;
- **How it is discovered（怎样被发现）:** dedicated category, Search（搜索）, Home / For You（首页 / 为你推荐）, notifications, restrictions;
- **How it is evaluated（如何评价）:** adult thematic label is not inherently poor quality, factual falsehood, moral condemnation or source-authority; formal Recognition（作品认可） eligibility for specific formats remains to be decided;
- **Other unrelated commerce（与成人专门内容商业化无关的商业事项）:** not automatically settled here.

## 5. Unconditional floor（不可被开放定位豁免的底线）
Open expression does not authorize exploitative/abusive content, sexual exploitation of minors, non-consensual intimate imagery, coercive sexual content, material violating applicable laws/rights, etc.（开放表达不豁免未成年人性剥削、未经同意的私密影像、胁迫剥削及其他违法 / 侵权行为）。

This is separate from ideological or aesthetic judgment of mature lawful cultural works（独立于对合法成熟文化作品的意识形态 / 审美判断）。

## 6. Confirmation scope（确认范围）
The user confirmed the “cultural platform that allows adult content to exist” positioning and strongly rejected dedicated adult paid subscriptions/private-media monetization as a future business direction（用户确认允许成人内容存在的文化平台定位，并明确长期排除成人付费订阅 / 私密影像商业化）。

**No blanket adult-content permission level, no final 18+ categories, and no age/visibility scheme have been approved（尚未批准全面成人开放尺度、具体类别或限龄展示方案）.**
