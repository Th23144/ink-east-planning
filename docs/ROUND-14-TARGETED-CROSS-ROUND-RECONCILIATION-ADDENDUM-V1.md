## 0. Do not turn an open community into a truth court（不得把开放社区变成真假审判机关）
Formal Candidate / Recognized Work（候选 / 认可作品） and Canonical Source Fidelity（原典来源忠实） bear stricter responsibility than ordinary Community / User Publishing（普通社区 / 用户发表）.

The ordinary publishing surface does not promise verified factual truth, firsthand proof, scholarly consensus or editorial endorsement（普通用户发布不意味着经过事实 / 亲历 / 学术共识 / 编辑背书核验）.

Fiction, exaggeration, first-person anecdotes, cultural interpretations and different opinions are allowed under ordinary community rules unless independent material rights/safety/abusive-conduct grounds justify intervention（小说、夸饰、个人叙述、文化解读与不同意见在基础规则下开放；涉及独立的重大侵权、安全或行为伤害风险另议）.

### A. RP1: No automatic academic dispute processing（普通诠释不进入正式知识争议程序）
**Clarifies（澄清）:** `ROUND-14-KNOWLEDGE-CORRECTION-SOURCE-DISPUTE-REVISION-LIFECYCLE-RESOLUTION-V1.md`, `ROUND-14-CORRECTION-OWNERSHIP-CONTESTED-DECISIONS-RESOLUTION-V1.md`, `ROUND-14-APPEAL-REVIEW-RESTORATION-RESOLUTION-V1.md`.

Ordinary interpretations of an ancient passage and normal disagreements among readers, whether or not they cite sources, are **not automatically**:
- factual errors（事实错误）;
- formal Dispute（争议） or Correction（纠错） cases;
- mandatory Human Review（人工复核） triggers;
- grounds for a public “disputed” badge（公开争议标签）;
- reasons to reduce quality Recognition（作品认可）.

A structured Dispute / Correction note is reserved for demonstrably material source-record or attribution issues, important platform-owned knowledge/factual commitments, or separately substantiated high-impact review questions—not an automatic verdict on differing philosophical readings（结构化来源纠错 / 争议说明仅适用于确有重要来源事实或平台承担责任的知识记录，及另行证明的重要审核问题，不是思想判胜负）.

Earlier “Good-faith Dispute（善意争议）” phrasing remains useful as a possible description of literature, but **ordinary plural interpretations do not become a moderation case just because they coexist（多元解读并存并非正式审查案件）**.

### B. RP2: Source witness and interpretation are different authority types（原典见证与诠释是不同的权威来源）
**Clarifies（澄清）:** `INK-EAST-CONTENT-KNOWLEDGE-SYSTEM-V1.md` and Round 7 Current Truth（第七轮当前有效规则）.

**Documentary source/witness objects（文献 / 底本对象）:** identifiable source text, each distinct original edition/witness, folio/image, cataloguing, attributable transcription and faithful documentary provenance（原文、各版本底本、影像、编目、转录及证据链）.

**Attributed editorial/derivative objects（署名编辑 / 衍生对象）:** supplied punctuation where not original, modern rendering, translation, interpretive annotations, scholarly essay, learner explanation, reader commentary（后补标点、现代译述、翻译、注释、研究解读、读者讨论）.

They can be **viewed together in one cohesive reading experience（同一阅读界面紧密展示）** while retaining distinct author, edition and evidence attribution（保持作者、底本和证据归属分离）. Different authentic editions may diverge; do not invent one universal “original” combining them（真实版本可以存在异文，不能强行合成唯一原文）.

### C. RP3: Recognition rigor begins with an actual formal review（严格评审只在真实评选时适用）
**Clarifies（澄清）:** Round 3 Recognition Governance Consolidation（第三轮作品认可治理总则） and Round 8 Community（第八轮社区）.

A self-described essay, travel anecdote or ordinary post does NOT enter mandatory verification merely because it mentions a factual-sounding event（提到类似事实的经历，不自动触发正规核验）.

Within a legitimate formal Candidate / Recognition review（候选 / 认可评审）, assess type-relevant quality and honesty around **evaluation-material claimed facts or sources（与评选结果实质相关的重要声明 / 来源）**. Do not demand documentary proof for every sentence or require one “correct” interpretation for philosophical/moral/cultural works（不逐句举证，不要求思想观点唯一）.

Formally recognized interpretation means assessed work quality, **not** canonical source authority or universal truth（认可解读作品质量，不等于原典来源权威或思想唯一真理）.

### D. RP4: Allegation, revalidation and misconduct are separate（举报、认可复核与处罚分离）
**Clarifies（澄清）:** Round 3 Recognition Seal/Consolidation（第三轮认可封存 / 整合）, Round 14 Appeal/Moderation（第十四轮申诉 / 审核）.

- Ordinary unverified accusation（普通未经核实的指控）: not proof and not automatic public stigma（不自动公开污名化）.
- Documentary/new-condition changes（资料 / 现实随时间变化）: may affect timeliness/current usefulness without proving past lies（影响时效不等于历史造假）.
- Independently supported falsification central to formal evaluation（有充分证据证明影响正式评审结果的核心虚假材料）: targeted Candidate / Recognized revalidation or invalidation, with explainable reason and meaningful appeal（针对相关作品评审复核 / 作废，说明理由并可申诉）.
- Independently established abusive conduct（另行证实的违规行为）: separately handled under Safety / Conduct Moderation（安全 / 行为审核） with proportional scope（范围适度）.

Store both historical fact “recognition was awarded（曾授予认可）” and current validity “recognition later invalidated based on materially false evidence（认可因关键证据失实而作废）”; do not perpetuate invalid endorsement or erase historical actions（不维持虚假背书，也不抹除历史）.

**Nothing here compels ordinary writers to produce tickets/passports/proof of travel（不要求普通作者提交票据 / 证件 / 行程证明）**.

### E. RP5: Accurate downstream presentation（当前信息与历史展示同步）
**Clarifies（澄清）:** Round 3 Recognition（第三轮认可）, Round 10 Recommendation（第十轮推荐）, Round 11 Editorial Issue（第十一轮编辑议题）.

Present Search（搜索）, Home/Recommendation（首页 / 推荐）, editor summaries, recognition board/cards and previews using the **current valid** status. Archived editor Issues（历史编辑议题） and historical decisions remain attributable to their actual time/version and can explain later revocation（历史档案可说明后续失效，但不得伪装旧认可目前仍然有效）.

No automatic cross-effect from Recognition reversal to author identity, all other works, source records or ordinary publication rights（认可撤销不自动连坐账户 / 其他作品 / 原典）。
