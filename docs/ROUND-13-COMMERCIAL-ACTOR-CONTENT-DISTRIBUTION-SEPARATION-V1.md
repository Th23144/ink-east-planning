## 1. Core principle（核心原则）

Ink & East（墨与东方） must not force one subject into an artificial choice between:

- Platform User / Organization（平台用户 / 组织）;
- Advertiser / Commercial Customer（广告客户 / 商业客户）.

These are different relationship axes.

The same Organization（组织） may:
- register and maintain its own platform identity/account（注册并维护自己的平台身份 / 账户）;
- publish eligible content in its own name（以自己的名义发布符合要求的内容）;
- participate in Community（社区） and other applicable product surfaces;
- also purchase future advertising / paid distribution products（未来广告 / 付费分发产品）.

Commercial relationship does not erase ordinary platform participation, and ordinary platform participation does not prohibit becoming a commercial customer.

---

## 2. Four-axis separation（四轴分离）

Where relevant, the architecture should distinguish:

1. **Actor / Publisher（主体 / 发布者）**  
   Who is speaking or publishing?

2. **Content Semantics / Content Type（内容语义 / 内容类型）**  
   Is the object a substantive article, community publication, editorial work, advertisement, event object or another type?

3. **Commercial Relationship（商业关系）**  
   Does the actor currently have an advertising, sponsorship, partner, project or other commercial relationship with Ink & East（墨与东方）?

4. **Distribution Treatment（分发处理）**  
   Is this exposure organic（自然）, editorial（编辑）, followed（关注）, search-led（搜索驱动）, paid-boosted（付费加热） or another explicitly represented source?

These axes must not be collapsed into one label.

---

## 3. Brand / Organization authored substantive content（品牌 / 组织发布的实质内容）

A commercial Organization（商业组织） may publish a normal substantive article if the article independently satisfies the platform's applicable content requirements.

Example（仅用于解释模型）:
A travel agency（旅行社） may publish a genuinely useful travel guide（旅游攻略） that fits the platform's cultural/travel domain and provides substantial reader value.

The presence of:
- limited self-reference（有限自我提及）;
- a service mention（服务提及）;
- a commercial callout（商业提示）;
- a link to the publisher's own offering（指向自身服务的链接）;

does not automatically transform the entire Work（作品） into a pure Advertisement（广告） if the primary function remains substantive content.

However:

> **A pure advertisement must not be disguised as an ordinary article merely by adding editorial-looking text.（纯广告不得通过包装成文章的方式伪装成普通内容。）**

Exact promotional-content thresholds and moderation policy remain later Policy / Configuration（策略 / 配置） work.

---

## 4. Paid Amplification（付费加热） as a distribution treatment

The user confirms that an eligible published Work（作品） may later support Paid Amplification（付费加热）.

Paid Amplification（付费加热） means additional paid distribution/exposure of an existing eligible content object.

It does **not**:
- change authorship（作者身份）;
- turn the Work into Ink & East Editorial（墨与东方编辑部） content;
- create Work Recognition（作品认可）;
- create Contributor Qualification（贡献者资格）;
- create source/truth authority（来源 / 真相权威）;
- buy editorial selection（编辑选择）;
- become organic recommendation merely because engagement is high.

Paid exposure must retain explicit distribution provenance（分发来源） and must not masquerade as Organic Recommendation（自然推荐）.

Engagement caused by paid exposure must retain Served Context（投放情境） so it is not silently treated as independent organic quality evidence.

---

## 5. Recognition / evaluation separation（作品认可 / 评选分离）

The user's statement that paid heating “has nothing to do with evaluation（和评选没关系）” is interpreted as:

> **Paid Amplification（付费加热） itself contributes zero positive Work Recognition（作品认可） evidence and creates no privileged evaluation path.**

This does not automatically make every Organization-authored Work（组织发布作品） ineligible for Recognition（作品认可）.

Eligibility for any future/confirmed evaluation path remains controlled by the Work's actual content/object class and applicable Recognition architecture, not by whether the publisher has ever been a commercial customer.

This preserves the existing rule that commercial/Partner status creates neither advantage nor automatic disqualification.

---

## 6. Product consequence（产品后果）

The monetization architecture should therefore treat:

> **Commercial Customer（商业客户） as a relationship/capacity, not a separate caste of platform participant（不是独立的平台用户阶级）.**

And:

> **Paid Amplification（付费加热） as a Distribution Treatment（分发处理）, not a quality / authority / recognition status（不是质量 / 权威 / 认可状态）.**
