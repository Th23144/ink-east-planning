---

## 0. Why this step exists / 为什么先做这一步

Round 12 must no longer begin from the inherited label `Membership / Reading Room`.

The correct sequence is:

```text
Current ordinary product value
→ what real user/product needs are still unmet
→ which unmet needs have scarcity / recurring cost / relationship value
→ whether Membership should own any of them
→ only then define concrete Membership benefits
```

This step deliberately ignores the old 80-item benefit catalogue as a decision source. That catalogue remains candidate material only and may be revisited later **after** the problem space is established.

---

# 1. What the ordinary Project 3 product already provides / 普通用户已经拥有的产品价值

This section distinguishes **hard current baseline** from **confirmed capability whose exact UX/payment boundary remains open**.

## 1.1 Hard current baseline / 已明确的硬基线

### A — Public published content remains readable
Normal published platform content is publicly readable.

Membership is not allowed to become:

- article paywall;
- Issue paywall;
- Archive paywall;
- VIP Library;
- “pay to unlock knowledge”.

### B — The platform remains useful without Membership
Current product journeys explicitly preserve an ordinary public product that remains viable before optional Membership.

Membership therefore cannot be justified by deliberately making the normal product unusable.

### C — Payment cannot purchase authority
Membership/payment cannot purchase:

- Identity/Claim truth;
- Contributor Qualification;
- Expertise;
- Work Recognition;
- Reviewer Trust;
- Governance Authority;
- organic Recommendation boost;
- factual/source authority.

These are architecture boundaries, not benefit-design preferences.

---

Project 3 already has confirmed product surfaces/functions for:

- Home / For You;
- Explore;

# 2. What is *not* currently a valid Membership problem / 当前不能拿来当会员问题的东西

The following are not legitimate reasons to create Membership value:

1. **unlocking normal published knowledge/content** — explicitly rejected;
2. **selling Recognition / authority / qualification / governance** — explicitly prohibited;
3. **selling organic reach/recommendation advantage** — prohibited;
4. **reviving Reading Room because the old plan had one** — existence unresolved;
5. **reviving Reader / Patron tiers because old source fields still exist** — historical only;
6. **reviving Custom Reading / Custom Ebook as automatic member benefits** — their future product roles are not yet revalidated;
7. **creating arbitrary free-user pain** so paid users can remove it — would contradict the “ordinary product remains viable” direction.

# 3. Genuine unmet-need domains that remain plausible / 仍然可能真实存在的未满足需求领域

These are **problem hypotheses**, not accepted Membership roles.

## G1 — Personal continuity & advanced organization / 个人连续性与高级整理

Confirmed capabilities already include Save, Reading History, Private Notes, Saved Passages and related reading.

The unresolved question is not whether these needs exist.

The unresolved question is:

> does normal product use eventually create a deeper organization/continuity problem that basic capability alone does not solve?

Possible unmet needs may include:

- managing a large personal corpus over time;
- returning to long-term reading/research projects;
- organizing notes/passages/works across Topic / Place / Text / Work relations;
- advanced filtering, grouping, export or synchronization;
- continuity across devices/sessions.

This could support a paid utility layer **only if** the free baseline remains genuinely usable and advanced value is not artificial deprivation.

**Current status:** REAL NEED AREA / MEMBERSHIP OWNERSHIP UNDECIDED.

---

## G2 — Supporter relationship / 支持关系

Membership itself is already a valid future paid/support relationship subject.

What is not yet proven is whether users need a durable relationship beyond ordinary product usage.

Possible needs:

- financially support the platform/editorial mission;
- understand what their support sustains;
- feel an ongoing relationship with the publication/platform;
- receive non-authority acknowledgement of support;
- receive closer but bounded communication from the editorial/platform team.

This is fundamentally different from paying for information access.

**Current status:** VALID DESIGN DOMAIN / VALUE STRENGTH UNPROVEN.

---

## G3 — Scarce human attention & limited-capacity experiences / 稀缺人工注意力与限额体验

Project 3 already contains editorial intake, questions, submissions, community participation and future events/experiences.

Some future value may consume genuinely scarce resources:

- editor/researcher time;
- live discussion capacity;
- limited seats;
- personalized response/review capacity;
- event facilitation;
- support/service attention.

Unlike content access, scarcity here can be real.

The product question is:

> should Membership allocate some of this scarce capacity, and if yes, how without buying editorial outcomes, governance or publication preference?

**Current status:** REAL SCARCITY DOMAIN / MEMBERSHIP ROLE UNDECIDED.

---

## G4 — Legitimate anti-abuse friction reduction / 合法的反滥用摩擦降低

Earlier architecture already allows payment to reduce selected zero-cost/Sybil friction where economically justified.

Examples may eventually include:

- selected publishing capacity;
- submission/Ask frequency;
- some account capability friction.

But payment cannot manufacture trust, expertise, qualification, Recognition, ranking or governance standing.

Therefore this can be an **ancillary Membership effect**, but it is structurally weak as the primary reason to pay.

**Current status:** ARCHITECTURALLY ALLOWED ANCILLARY ROLE / NOT A CORE VALUE THESIS YET.

---

## G5 — Service / commerce relationship / 服务与商业关系

Potential value may exist around:

- future scarce services;
- booking priority;
- bounded courtesy;
- Spatial Flow commerce relationship.

However, Round 13 exists specifically to revalidate Services / Monetization / Spatial Flow relationship.

Therefore Round 12 must not steal Round 13's unresolved product decisions.

Membership may eventually interface with services/commerce, but the actual service products and commercial relationship must first be revalidated.

**Current status:** LEGITIMATE INTERFACE DOMAIN / DEFER SUBSTANCE TO ROUND 13.

---

## G6 — Delivery / proactive return value / 主动投递与回访价值

Notification semantics exist, but the full Notifications / Delivery product remains deferred.

Possible unmet needs may include:

- configurable digests;
- meaningful return reminders;
- topic/place/work updates;
- personalized delivery cadence.

This could create recurring utility, but using it as Membership value before the underlying Delivery product is defined risks repeating the Reading Room mistake.

**Current status:** REAL FUTURE PRODUCT DOMAIN / MEMBERSHIP ROLE PREMATURE.

### B — Content unlock
Explicitly rejected.

### C — Recommendation advantage
Explicitly rejected.

### D — Governance power
Explicitly rejected.

# 5. First architecture synthesis / 第一层架构归纳

After removing legacy containers and already-solved/free-baseline needs, the currently plausible Membership problem space reduces to **three primary domains** plus **two secondary interfaces**:

## Primary domain P1 — Advanced recurring utility
Solve a real higher-order personal continuity / organization problem beyond the healthy ordinary product.

## Primary domain P2 — Supporter relationship
Create a durable paid relationship with the platform/publication without turning payment into authority.

## Primary domain P3 — Scarce participation / human attention
Allocate genuinely limited experiences, interaction or service capacity without purchasing outcomes/governance.

## Secondary interface S1 — Contextual anti-abuse/capability friction reduction
Allowed where justified, but not core Membership identity.

## Secondary interface S2 — Services / commerce
Possible later relationship, but substance belongs to Round 13.

Notifications/Delivery remains outside the core until its own product is defined.

# 6. The first genuine Round 12 product decision / 第十二轮第一个真正的产品选择

The question is **not** “which benefits do we pick?”

The first decision is:

> **Should Round 12 define Membership as one primary product role, or as a bounded combination of the three legitimate primary domains above?**

Possible directions for discussion:

### Direction A — Supporter Relationship first
Membership primarily exists because users want to support the platform and maintain a closer relationship.

Utility/participation may exist, but are secondary.

### Direction B — Advanced Utility first
Membership primarily solves advanced personal reading/research continuity and organization.

Support/participation are secondary.

### Direction C — Scarce Participation first
Membership primarily organizes access to genuinely limited editorial/community experiences and human attention.

Support/utility are secondary.

### Direction D — Bounded Hybrid
Membership is intentionally a combined relationship:
- Supporter Relationship as the emotional/business basis;
- Advanced Utility as recurring day-to-day value;
- Scarce Participation as limited high-value differentiation.

This is not the old Workshop B “hybrid recommendation” carried forward as truth. It is re-derived from the current Product Baseline after removing Reading Room and legacy benefit assumptions.

No direction is selected by this document.

---

# 7. User-review checkpoint / 用户确认点

Before defining benefits, the user should decide whether:

1. the three primary domains above correctly describe the unresolved Membership problem space;
2. any domain is missing;
3. any listed domain does not belong in Membership at all;
4. Membership should have one dominant role or intentionally be a bounded hybrid.

Only after that decision should Round 12 proceed to concrete benefit architecture.
