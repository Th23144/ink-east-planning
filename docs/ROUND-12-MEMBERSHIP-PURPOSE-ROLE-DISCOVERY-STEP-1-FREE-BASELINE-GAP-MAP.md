## Why this draft was superseded / 为什么这份草稿被取代

This draft incorrectly framed Round 12 around finding a paid value bundle from three proposed domains:

- Advanced Recurring Utility;
- Supporter Relationship;
- Scarce Participation / Human Attention.

That framing assumed too early that Membership must justify itself by offering additional paid product value.

The user revalidated the earlier Rounds 1–5 rule instead:

> Membership/payment may act as an economic-cost signal that reduces selected zero-cost / Sybil abuse friction and allows some capability restrictions to be relaxed earlier.

The current architecture also keeps the platform core open and prevents payment from becoming trust, authority, Recognition, governance or organic recommendation advantage.

Therefore this draft must not be used as the Round 12 starting model.
