# Membership Re-entry Review #1（会员重启检查 #1）— Formative Stage Boundary Resolution V1
# 会员重启检查 #1——平台形成阶段边界结论 V1

> **Status（状态）:** USER-CONFIRMED / RESOLVED（用户确认 / 已解决）
> **Implementation（实现）:** NOT AUTHORIZED（未授权）
> **Merge（合并）:** NOT AUTHORIZED（未授权）

## 1. Formative stage is milestone-based（形成阶段主要按里程碑判断）

The platform's formative stage（平台形成阶段） should be defined primarily by actual product/community maturity milestones（产品 / 社区成熟里程碑）, not by an arbitrary calendar cutoff alone（而不是单纯按固定日期）.

The exact closing milestone remains deferred（具体关闭里程碑暂缓）, but the governing principle is:

> **“Early（早期）” should reflect the period when users can still materially shape the platform's basic identity, norms and structure（“早期”应对应用户仍能实质影响平台基本身份、规范与结构的阶段）.**

## 2. Early Presence（早期在场） is not the same as Early Co-building（早期共建）

Mere early registration（仅早注册） may be preserved as Early Presence（早期在场） history.

It does not automatically create full Early Co-builder（早期共建者） history.

Therefore:

> **Being early（来得早） != materially co-building（真正共建）.**

## 3. Multiple typed histories remain（继续保留多类型历史）

During the formative stage（形成阶段）, the platform may continue recording multiple independent early co-building histories, including:
- Early Presence（早期在场）;
- Financial Support（资金支持）;
- Quality Publishing / Knowledge Participation（优质发布 / 知识参与）;
- Network Growth（网络增长）;
- Product Co-building（产品共建）;
- Cultural Recovery / Project Participation（文化寻回 / 项目参与）.

These remain separate historical records rather than one binary score/level（单一二元分数 / 等级）.

## 4. The formative stage should close explicitly（形成阶段应明确关闭）

Once Ink & East（墨与东方） reaches a defined product/community maturity milestone（产品 / 社区成熟节点）, the platform should explicitly stop creating new “early-stage（早期阶段）” history.

Existing Early Co-builder（早期共建者） history remains preservable.

The exact milestone is a later Product / Policy（产品 / 策略） decision.

## 5. Later users remain fully eligible for non-historical roles（后来用户仍可完整参与非历史性角色）

Users who join after the formative stage（形成阶段） are not lower-value users.

They remain fully eligible for all applicable future roles/opportunities, including:
- creators（创作者）;
- Contributor（贡献者） where independently qualified;
- Recovery Participant（寻回参与者）;
- supporters / Members（支持者 / 会员）;
- partners / Organizations（合作方 / 组织）;
- project participation（项目参与）;
- other future platform roles.

They simply do not share the same early historical context（只是没有同样的早期历史语境）.
