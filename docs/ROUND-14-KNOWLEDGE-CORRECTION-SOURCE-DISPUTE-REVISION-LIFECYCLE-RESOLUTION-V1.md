## 1. Distinct epistemic / conduct states（区分知识状态与行为违规）
Ink & East（墨与东方） formally distinguishes:
- **Error（错误）**: verifiable factual, citation, attribution, transcription or source mistake（可核实的事实、引用、归属、转录或来源错误）;
- **Uncertainty（不确定）**: evidence is incomplete or source provenance remains unresolved（证据不足或来源未明）;
- **Good-faith Dispute（善意争议）**: more than one interpretation is supported by evidence（不同解释各有来源依据）;
- **Misconduct（违规）**: a separate behavioral finding such as evidenced fabrication, forgery, deception or abuse（另有证据证明的编造、伪造、欺骗或滥用）。

These are not simply one true/false verdict（不是统一的真假判决）. A disagreement or ordinary correction does **not** automatically imply misconduct or poor quality.

## 2. Accessible, evidence-oriented intake（低门槛且以证据为核心的受理）
A substantive correction request should identify the affected claim, content/context and available evidence（明确对应主张、内容 / 上下文和可提供的证据）. Ordinary users may start with lightweight reporting（普通用户可先轻量反馈） without filling a complex research form.

The platform should allow clarification/additional evidence where needed. No automatic finding based solely on a report（不能仅凭举报自动认定事实）.

## 3. Content-class-sensitive correction responsibilities（按内容类别确定纠错责任）
Correction authority, responsibility and visible revision depth depend on the established content classes:
- **Canonical / Source-backed（规范 / 来源型）**: strongest edition/provenance fidelity and material change history（版本 / 来源完整性及重要修改留痕）;
- **Editorial / Teaching（编辑 / 教学）**: accountable editorial corrections and meaningful change notes（编辑责任与重要修改说明）;
- **Contributor（贡献者）** works: involve the author where appropriate; do not conflate work revision with Contributor Qualification（贡献者资格） or Work Recognition（作品认可）;
- **Community / User（社区 / 用户）**: lightweight author corrections and appropriate reporting; do not force all posts into a formal academic editorial workflow（不强迫所有普通帖子进入学术审稿流程）.

This is not a single generic Article（文章） workflow.

## 4. Explainable material changes; lightweight minor edits（重要修订可解释，轻微修改保持轻量）
Material corrections should have an appropriately visible explanation:
- what changed（改了什么）;
- when and why（何时与为何）;
- relevant source/evidence（相关来源 / 证据）;
- responsible editorial/author action where context requires（需要时标记责任方）。

Typographical/minor edits（错字 / 轻微修改） need not produce prominent announcements. Sensitive/private evidence remains access-limited even if a correction note is public（即使公开修订说明，私密证据仍受限）. Internal provenance and public disclosure are separate axes（内部溯源与公开展示相互独立）.

## 5. Disputes may be recorded without removal/punishment（有依据的争议可保留而不删除 / 处罚）
Where multiple source-backed interpretations remain supportable, the platform may publish an annotated Dispute（争议） or Uncertainty（不确定） state rather than coercively picking a single interpretation.

A legitimate disagreement must not automatically delete a work, punish its author, remove Work Recognition（作品认可） or revoke Contributor Qualification（贡献者资格）. Those belong to separate independent processes if independently justified.

## 6. Lifecycle at principle level（原则性流程）
Propose（提出） → Triage（分流） → Evaluate（评估） → Resolve（处理） → Record（留痕） → Revisit（复核）.

This is a product-level responsibility model only. Exact moderation tooling, reviewer staffing, source thresholds, public UI（用户界面） and storage implementation remain deferred（暂缓）.
