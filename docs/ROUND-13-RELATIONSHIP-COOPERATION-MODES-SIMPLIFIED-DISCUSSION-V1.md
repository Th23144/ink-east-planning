## 1. Why simplify（为什么简化）

The previous C1–C8（C1–C8） list was useful for completeness but too granular for the current strategic decision.

The relationship does not need eight separate user decisions.

A cleaner model is three layers.

---

## 2. Layer A — Core Strategic Collaboration（核心战略合作）

This layer describes cooperation that directly expresses the reason the two products maintain a Standing Strategic Relationship（持续战略关系）.

### A1 — Knowledge ↔ Practice（知识 ↔ 实践）
Ink & East（墨与东方） may provide knowledge, history, provenance, cultural context and research.

Spatial Flow（空间流） may provide material, object, craft, use, care, space and real-world practice.

### A2 — Research / Material / Craft Projects（研究 / 材料 / 工艺项目）
The two products may collaborate on:
- research（研究）;
- documentation（记录）;
- material/craft investigation（材料 / 工艺调查）;
- reconstruction / reproduction experiments（复原 / 再制作实验）;
- real-world testing / observation（现实测试 / 观察）.

### A3 — Cultural Recovery Support（文化寻回支持）
Where suitable, Spatial Flow（空间流） may support a specific Open Cultural Recovery（开放文化寻回） case through explicit scoped capabilities such as material, craft, documentation, reproduction or logistics.

Spatial Flow（空间流） is never the default/exclusive recovery operator（默认 / 唯一寻回执行方）.

### A4 — Knowledge ↔ Object / Project Linking（知识 ↔ 物件 / 项目关联）
Where a real relationship exists, both products may create attributable cross-product links.

This is already confirmed as a normal capability.

---

## 3. Layer B — Optional Joint Projects（可选联合项目）

These cooperation types may happen, but they do not define the relationship itself.

Examples:
- co-authored / related publications（联合 / 关联发布）;
- exhibitions / talks / workshops / pop-ups（展览 / 讲座 / 工作坊 / 快闪）;
- jointly developed objects/products（联合开发物件 / 商品）;
- licensing（授权）;
- sourcing / supply（采购 / 供应）;
- project-specific commerce（项目型商业化）.

These remain project-specific and do not need to exist for the Standing Strategic Relationship（持续战略关系） to remain real.

---

## 4. Layer C — Discovery / Referral（发现 / 引导）

Users may move between Ink & East（墨与东方）and Spatial Flow（空间流） where contextually relevant.

This is not treated as the strategic core.

It is a user-journey consequence of real relationships, not the reason the relationship exists.

Therefore:

> **Cross-linking / referral（互链 / 引导） is an outcome, not the foundation.**

---

## 5. Anti-collapse rules（防塌缩规则）

The simplified model does not mean:
- every Ink & East（墨与东方） research project needs Spatial Flow（空间流）;
- every Spatial Flow（空间流） object needs Ink & East（墨与东方） content;
- every collaboration becomes commerce;
- Spatial Flow（空间流） becomes the exclusive physical-world arm（唯一现实执行端）;
- Ink & East（墨与东方） becomes a marketing/content department;
- discovery becomes a universal funnel（统一漏斗）.

---

## 6. Current decision target（当前决策目标）

The current strategic question is now reduced to one simple check:

> **Does this three-layer model correctly describe the relationship: Core Strategic Collaboration（核心战略合作） + Optional Joint Projects（可选联合项目） + Contextual Discovery / Referral（情境化发现 / 引导）?**

If yes, the detailed C1–C8（C1–C8） inventory can remain supporting detail rather than forcing separate decisions.
