## 1. Repair objective（修订目标）
Do **not** replace Ink & East（墨与东方） open community with academic source verification or a permanent platform “truth court（真理法庭）”.

Preserve:
- free ordinary publication and interpretive pluralism（自由普通发表与诠释多元）;
- Recognition（作品认可） as work-level and content-type-sensitive（作品级 / 按类型评价）;
- canonical source fidelity as distinct from interpretive work recognition（原典来源忠实与作品认可分离）;
- proportional safety review for genuine harmful conduct（仅针对真实安全 / 伤害风险按比例审核）;
- consent and user preference concerns for adult content as a separate, undecided policy（成人内容作为独立未决政策）.

## 2. Proposed repair packages（候选修订包）

### RP1 / P0 — Formal interpretation versus dispute separation（开放诠释不自动触发正式争议）
Amend controlling Round 14 knowledge correction / ownership wording using an explicit addendum that normal disagreement is NOT an error/dispute/appeal case. Existing user-approved procedure applies only when relevant to actual source facts, formal Recognition integrity, or proportionate safety.
Affected docs:
- `ROUND-14-KNOWLEDGE-CORRECTION-SOURCE-DISPUTE-REVISION-LIFECYCLE-RESOLUTION-V1.md`
- `ROUND-14-CORRECTION-OWNERSHIP-CONTESTED-DECISIONS-RESOLUTION-V1.md`
- `ROUND-14-APPEAL-REVIEW-RESTORATION-RESOLUTION-V1.md`
Keep original decisions/provenance; do not silently rewrite sealed history（保留已确认决策与历史，附明确适用范围澄清）.

### RP2 / P0 — Source text and derivative commentary differentiation（原典与翻译 / 注释的权威来源区分）
Add a historical clarification to `INK-EAST-CONTENT-KNOWLEDGE-SYSTEM-V1.md` and link to Round 7 current truth. Readers may see texts, editions, translation, commentary and discussion in one reading experience, while their different authorship/provenance remain explicit. No rewriting historical witness（不能改写原始底本）.

### RP3 / P1 — Recognition criteria vs free community publication（候选 / 认可审核不下沉到普通社区）
Clarify Round 3 Recognition rules that content-type evidence checks are invoked by formal evaluation, not merely a published essay with a verifiable-sounding claim. An unrelated report does not automatically trigger formal Recognition review or sanction in ordinary content.
- Normal publication can be fictional, embellished, subjective or imperfect without automatic platform verification.
- Formal Candidate and Recognized works may be checked only for **material review-relevant assertions（重要且与评审相关的声明）**, without demanding proof for every sentence.
- Philosophical interpretations are evaluated for integrity, reasoning and author attribution, not one doctrinally correct interpretation（不要求观点正统统一）.
- Whether authors may participate/decline Formal Nomination（正式提名） remains a separate product decision.

### RP4 / P1 — Recognition evidence reversal and protected process（认可依据作废与权利保障）
Clarify in Round 3 + Round 14 an evidence chain distinct from ordinary social reporting:
`Report / Evidence Intake（举报 / 证据提交）` → `Targeted Assessment（定向核查）` → `Relevant Candidate / Recognition Decision（相关候选 / 认可决定）` → `Appeal / Repair（申诉 / 修复）`.
Differentiate (i) unsubstantiated accusation, (ii) honest change/staleness, (iii) evidenced falsification central to evaluation. Keep historical award fact without presenting an invalidated award as currently valid.
**Do not** impose this workflow on normal works absent actual serious harm or safety-rule grounds.

### RP5 / P1 — Derived surfaces carry current state accurately（历史 / 推荐 / 议题展示准确继承状态）
Where Recognized status is materially reversed, do not let current recommendation cards, editor summaries or search snippets continue claiming the invalid status. Archive keeps truthful historical context rather than silently deleting history. Separate editorial Issue inclusion from award and ordinary recommendation.

### RP6 / P2 — Fix active navigation and avoid false current truth（修复项目索引与当前入口）
Reconcile `PROJECT-3-START-HERE.md`, `PR-53-DETAILED-RULE-REVIEW-INDEX.md`, `ROUND-14-CURRENT-CHECKPOINT-V1.md`; mark outdated discussion pointers historical and show a single active topic. An existing unapproved five-outcome taxonomy remains `REQUIRES REFRAMING`.
Navigation fixes can be routine editorial changes; governance/recognition semantic amendments require user approval（导航清理可为常规文档维护，关键制度语义修改需用户确认）.

## 3. Do not do（明确不做）
- No blanket truth verification on all user posts（不普查普通用户发言真假）.
- No mandatory travel receipt/ID/face verification on ordinary writers（不对普通旅游随笔强制索要票据 / 身份证 / 人脸验证）.
- No forced “dispute / controversy” badge for normal differing interpretations（不因为观点不同强制挂争议标签）.
- No editor or Recognition council declaring one universal philosophical answer（不设唯一正统解释）.
- No adult-content category decision folded into these repairs（成人内容政策单独决定）.
- No changed code, no merge, no public product-policy deployment.
