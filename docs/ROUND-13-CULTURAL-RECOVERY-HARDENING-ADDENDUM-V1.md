# Round 13（第十三轮）— Open Cultural Contribution & Recovery Hardening Addendum V1（开放文化贡献与寻回加固补充 V1）

> **Status（状态）:** CONTROLLING HARDENING ADDENDUM（当前控制性加固补充）
> **Implementation（实现）:** NOT AUTHORIZED（未授权）

## H1 — Intake is not acceptance（接收不等于接纳）
Receiving a lead, file, object description or physical-contact request（线索、文件、物件描述或实体联系请求）does not mean Ink & East（墨与东方）has authenticated, accepted, acquired or agreed to publish the material.

## H2 — Do not create unsafe physical-transfer pressure（不得制造不安全实体转移压力）
The product must not encourage a holder to ship, move, export or surrender a potentially significant physical object（潜在重要实体物件）before appropriate provenance, ownership, legal, safety and receiving-institution conditions are understood.

## H3 — Disputed ownership / provenance pauses transfer（所有权 / 来源争议时暂停转移）
Credible theft, illicit excavation, trafficking, ownership, inheritance, custody or export/import concerns（盗窃、非法发掘、走私、所有权、继承、保管或进出口争议）must be capable of placing the case into restricted review rather than continuing normal handoff/publication.

## H4 — Exact location may be sensitive（精确地点可能敏感）
Exact storage/discovery location（精确保管 / 发现地点）may require restricted visibility where disclosure could create theft, trafficking, privacy, cultural or site-protection risk.

## H5 — Cultural sensitivity can override default openness（文化敏感性可限制默认开放）
Sacred, funerary, community-restricted or otherwise culturally sensitive knowledge/material（神圣、丧葬、社区限制或其他文化敏感知识 / 材料）must not be automatically digitized/published merely because it was submitted.

## H6 — Contributor safety and consent（贡献者安全与同意）
Public attribution（公开署名）must be consent-aware. A discoverer / holder / family / local informant（发现者 / 持有人 / 家族 / 地方知情者）may require pseudonymous, delayed or private attribution where justified.

## H7 — Evidence preservation before transformation（处理前保留证据）
Where material, original received files, source-state documentation and transformation history（原始接收文件、来源状态记录及处理历史）should be preserved sufficiently to distinguish original evidence from OCR, cleanup, reconstruction, translation or editorial synthesis（OCR、清理、重建、翻译或编辑综合）.

## H8 — Restoration must not become fabricated certainty（修复不得变成伪造确定性）
Reconstruction / restoration（重建 / 修复）must distinguish:
- observed original material（可观察原始材料）;
- inferred restoration（推断修复）;
- editorial normalization（编辑规范化）;
- machine-assisted reconstruction（机器辅助重建）.

## H9 — Institutional referral is attributable and non-guaranteed（机构转介可归因且不保证接收）
Referral to a Qualified Institution（合格机构）must record the relevant institution/contact/result where appropriate. Referral does not imply acceptance, endorsement or legal transfer.

## H10 — Platform contribution survives custody handoff（平台贡献不因保管移交消失）
Where lawful and appropriate, the recovery case should preserve Ink & East（墨与东方）and third-party contributions to discovery, documentation, digitization, research, translation and referral even after the physical original moves elsewhere.

## H11 — No automatic commercial claim（不得自动产生商业权利主张）
Contribution, digitization or recovery work does not automatically grant Ink & East（墨与东方）ownership of the physical original, copyright, exclusive publication rights or commercial exploitation rights（实体所有权、著作权、独家发布权或商业利用权）.

## H12 — Public benefit does not erase rights（公共利益不覆盖权利）
The goal of making knowledge available to humanity（向公众开放知识）does not authorize bypassing ownership, privacy, copyright, cultural restrictions, institutional terms or applicable law（所有权、隐私、著作权、文化限制、机构条款或适用法律）.

## H13 — Contribution credit is role-specific（贡献署名按角色）
A contribution record should say what was actually done rather than awarding a generic prestige label（通用荣誉标签）.

## H14 — Recovery importance is not determined by popularity（寻回价值不由热度决定）
Views, likes, sales, donations or social attention（浏览、点赞、销售、捐款或社交热度）must not determine authenticity, preservation priority or cultural significance automatically.

## H15 — Donation（捐赠）is not the umbrella user-facing promise
Donation（捐赠）remains a specific legal/relationship mode. Final public naming for the broader system remains deferred.
