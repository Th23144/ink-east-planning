## 1. Method and core objects
- Be precise about boundaries without freezing context. Distinguish HARD INVARIANT, ADAPTIVE RULE, DEFERRED CALIBRATION, EXAMPLE.
- No global user level, prestige ladder, or universal scalar trust score. Ordinary users keep the simplest path; advanced controls appear only when relevant.
- Keep separate where applicable: Account; Actor; Requester/Initiator; Authorizing Principal(s); Executor; Display Identity; Stable Platform Identity; Knowledge Entity; Authority-bearing Subject; Claim; Relationship; Role; Permission; Grant Authority; authority-mutation permission; Scope; Acting Context; Authorship; Publisher; Operator.
- Knowledge Entity is broad (Person, Organization, Place, Work, Concept, Event/Period, future objects). Authority-bearing Subject is narrower; Person and Organization are current primary kinds. Non-agent graph entities do not receive operational authority merely by being graph entities.
- Organization is the base organizational type; Institution is a subtype. Platform-owned, Official, Editorial, Partner and similar terms are relationship/capacity/provenance attributes, not peer entity types.

## 2. Identity, pseudonymity, knowledge records, representation and attribution
- Stable Platform Identity is durable platform continuity, not proof of a real-world Person. Display Identity is mutable. Real-world Person linkage is optional, claim-driven, sensitive and purpose-limited. Pseudonymous use and multiple legitimate personas are allowed without forced public merge.
- Legitimate name/avatar/handle/pen-name change does not create a new Stable Platform Identity or rewrite historical attribution; sensitive former/legal/deadname data is not exposed merely to prove continuity.
- Creating/editing/curating a Person or Organization knowledge record does not establish identity, representation, authenticity, official status, control or operational authority. Verified linkage/control is separate and provenance-bearing; operational control does not confer unilateral control over neutral/historical knowledge facts.
- Organization authority normally uses individual authenticated accounts, not long-lived shared passwords. Representation is scoped, changeable, suspendable/revocable and time-aware. Invitation normally requires invite + acceptance on the invitee's own account with explicit Scope. Bootstrap administration is platform control, not permanent legal ownership.
- Role/representation/control/authority-relationship mutations retain durable audit/provenance history.
- Actual Actor and public attribution differ. Representative publishing/comments/replies require clear Acting Context; no invisible sticky Organization identity. Ordinary actions default to personal/selected platform identity unless a represented context is deliberately used; irrelevant enterprise controls stay hidden.
- Official Organization speech and personal opinion remain distinguishable. Representation does not create person-specific Reviewer Trust, Governance Authority, Formal Nomination authority or comparable standing.
- Ordinary purchases default to Personal Platform Context. Future Organization commerce requires deliberate Organization commercial context and records actual Operator plus billing/paying and asset-owning subject where applicable. Personal saves/follows/security/preferences remain personal unless deliberately redesigned.
- Author, Publisher and Operator may differ. Multi-author/contribution relations are first-class. Authorship/contribution does not automatically grant edit/publish/delete/moderation/admin rights; Work operating rights are separate scoped permissions. Permission loss does not rewrite historical attribution.

## 3. Claims, verification, qualification and public standing
- Verify specific Claims, not a person's overall truthfulness. Claim types may include real-person, identity consistency, Organization authenticity, affiliation/employment, representation, credential, legal ownership and future scoped claims. Unverified != false.
- Claim != Relationship != Permission. A verified fact may support a later decision but does not automatically create representation or operational authority.
- Claim types have distinct lifecycles; no universal re-verification interval. Dispute/expiry/revocation of one Claim affects that Claim first, not automatically the whole account/identity/qualification/history.
- Claim/attestation provenance records issuer, authorized Actor/context, time and later withdrawal/expiry/dispute where relevant. Organization authenticity does not certify every Organization publication/opinion.
- Contributor Qualification != Identity Verification. Expertise Scope is bounded and non-transferable outside scope. Qualification does not grant Work Recognition; strong ordinary-user work remains eligible. Organization attestation proves scoped facts and cannot bulk-mint qualification. Partner/Official/Platform status does not automatically create Verification, qualification, Recognition evidence, Reviewer/Governance standing or ranking privilege. Verification does not automatically raise Recommendation/Ranking/Recognition/Governance. Membership cannot purchase factual verification, expertise, Reviewer/Governance standing or Work Recognition.
- Public presentation explains what was verified rather than using one ambiguous universal badge. Prevent visual/algorithmic prestige stacking.

## 4. Permission, Role, grant/mutation authority and execution
- Permission is atomic and scoped. Role is a permission bundle, not trust/status. Separate at minimum Content Operations, Member Management, Identity & Representation, Analytics, Commercial & Finance, Security & Audit, Entity Control Authority. Content/member/engineering authority does not imply finance/security/control or other unrelated authority.
- Early UX may expose a small understandable preset Role set while keeping granular underlying permissions; Custom Roles may exist later without becoming a hierarchy.
- Least Privilege by default. Use Permission != Grant/Delegate Permission. Grant, revoke, suspend, restore, deny/restrict, delegate and Scope mutation are separately protected scoped operations. Policy may explicitly couple legitimate powers, never infer them from Role names alone.
- Manage Members/Manage Roles cannot manufacture higher authority. Custom Roles cannot contain permissions the creator cannot grant. Self-elevation needs explicit Grant Authority/current checks. Expanding a shared Role/template is a grant event requiring Role/template mutation authority plus Grant Authority for each added Permission in affected Scope; risk-based bulk safeguards/provenance apply. Narrowing changes future authority without rewriting history.
- A material semantic expansion of an existing Permission, Scope definition, authorization policy or reusable security rule is authority-expansion / bulk-grant-equivalent and requires appropriate governance, impact review, provenance and safeguards. Narrowing changes future authority only. Exact migration/versioning is deferred. **(R40)**
- Protected capabilities are default-deny absent a current valid Permission+Scope source. Ordinary public actions intentionally requiring no special Permission remain ordinary product actions. No routine universal Organization-side super-admin bypass exists by label.
- Multiple authorization sources combine into Effective Permission. Conflicts resolve at Permission+Scope, never Role-name precedence. Applicable security Explicit Deny/restriction overrides ordinary Allow within Scope. Consequential execution rechecks current authority/security; stale page access does not preserve authority at commit.
- Revoking one source removes that source's authority while independent valid grants remain. Revocation affects future capability, not historical authorship/audit/provenance or automatic deletion of existing drafts; current draft access follows current permissions.
- Delegation is non-transitive by default; re-delegation requires explicit authority. Temporary/context-bounded delegation is supported without universal expiry. Emergency Revocation is distinct from normal offboarding.
- Legitimate Joint Action may use multiple independently authorized subjects; cross-entity permission fusion is prohibited.
- Reassigning a protected resource's controlling subject, ownership/control binding, tenant/project membership, authorization-Scope membership or equivalent security boundary is authority-affecting, not ordinary metadata editing; it needs authority for the boundary change plus source/destination re-evaluation. Cosmetic metadata edits remain ordinary. **(R38)**

## 5. Control continuity, recovery and Organization transitions
- Entity Control Authority is platform control; Legal Ownership is a separate Claim. Highest control may be shared. Control Continuity prevents accidental orphaning proportionately, but intentional closure/dissolution/archive/historical-only states may end active control through an explicit lifecycle.
- Ordinary publishing/edit/member management does not imply control transfer/recovery/Organization deletion or other Sensitive Authority.
- High-impact control transfer may require recipient/bilateral confirmation, step-up/re-authentication, contextual risk evaluation and where justified cooling/Protective Hold/independent review. No universal wait, controller count, document count or two-person rule. Control transfer never transfers personal trust/qualification/reviewer/governance/Recognition/behavior history.
- Account Recovery != Organization Control Recovery. Recovering account/email/session does not prove Organization control. Current possession of domain/email/phone/social channel is evidence, not conclusive proof; contested/high-value recovery may use historical control, authenticity, representation, corroboration, notifications, holds, COI checks/human review. No universal checklist/score/wait time.
- Re-registration or later possession of a reused identifier does not restore prior Stable Identity/private data/trust/Claims/qualification/reviewer-governance/Organization authority/security state. Handle/display name/email/phone/URL/domain are mutable/recyclable, not stable identity keys.
- Permissions/scopes/approvals/grants/authority relationships bind to stable internal object identity, not mutable/reusable identifiers.
- Accounts/personas linked to one real-world Person do not automatically share Organization roles/control, staff capacity, qualification use, reviewer/governance capability or comparable standing. Linkage is not a universal permission bridge or automatic Account Recovery factor.
- Knowledge-record reconciliation, real-world merger/succession, Organization split, subject correction and operational-control migration are distinct and never mechanically union controllers/Roles/grants/delegations/sensitive authority. Operational migration is explicit, scoped, provenance-bearing and security/context checked. Organization hierarchy does not automatically inherit permissions.
- Organization authority covers Organization-scoped resources only; it does not automatically expose members' personal messages, unrelated private profile/account/security data or other non-Organization private data.

## 6. Derived authority, pending artifacts, durable decisions, approvals and concurrency
- Derived grants preserve source lineage. Every derived authorization path must trace to at least one valid non-circular authority root appropriate to capability+Scope; circular delegation/reference cannot self-sustain after the valid root disappears. Independent valid roots remain valid; do not blindly cascade-delete independent authority. **(R39)**
- Pending invitations/transfers/delegations/deferred authority changes revalidate relevant source authority, Scope, validity and security context at activation/execution; creation while authorized does not create an irrevocable bearer right.
- A workflow may explicitly define a completed durable decision that can survive a later approver-role change. Durability is never assumed; target/material semantics/current-state/security constraints still govern. Do not make every approval permanent or require former approvers to keep roles forever after a legitimately completed decision.
- Sessions/API tokens/delegated credentials/signed links/cached authorization carry authority but do not accidentally become permanent sources after underlying authority changes; risk-appropriate recheck/invalidation/refresh applies. A genuinely independent durable credential/grant is valid only when explicitly modeled as its own authority source, not because a token/cache happened to persist.
- Where a safeguard requires independence, different account IDs alone are insufficient. Same natural person/effective decision-maker/controlled personas or automation cannot manufacture independence. Distinct authorized people in one Organization may still satisfy dual-control when genuinely independent for that safeguard and not disqualified by relevant COI/common control. No universal real-name requirement.
- Consequential approval/confirmation/acceptance/review/consent binds to the material action actually approved (target, recipient, Scope, authority change/grant, other security-relevant parameters). Material change requires re-evaluation/fresh approval as appropriate; cosmetic change need not restart.
- Consequential authority changes finalize against coherent current state; concurrent/intervening changes cannot combine stale valid decisions into an invalid state. Exact transaction/locking technology is deferred.
- Invitations/approvals/acceptances/transfers and bounded authority artifacts have explicit lifecycle/replay semantics: safe retry/idempotency is allowed, but expired/revoked/consumed/replaced/materially superseded artifacts do not silently regain validity.

## 7. Requester, authorizer, executor and automation
- Requester/Initiator != Authorizing Principal != Executor. A workflow may have one requester, multiple authorized approvers and a distinct executor; approval counts only from an actor/principal authorized for that approval capability+Scope.
- Automation cannot masquerade as human. Preserve automated Actor identity, controller/authorizer, authority source, Scope, expiry/revocation, represented subject and requester/authorizer/executor provenance where applicable. Automation does not inherit human Reviewer Trust/Governance Authority or independent human-judgment standing.
- Broad executor technical capability does not bypass caller/principal authority. User/Organization-triggered work cannot be relabeled as Platform Authority merely because a platform service executes it. Genuine platform-originated moderation/security/legal/recovery/maintenance actions must arise from authorized platform workflow/policy/case and retain authority provenance.

## 8. Staff, policy governance, conflicts and appeals
- Platform Staff capacity is separate from community identity and does not create personal Contributor Qualification, Reviewer Trust, Governance Authority or Work Recognition.
- Internal domains are separated (Editorial, Moderation, Trust & Safety, Support, Identity & Verification, Finance, Security, Engineering & Operations). Need-to-know/Purpose Limitation apply; minimize/mask data; prefer JIT temporary elevation; high-risk elevation may require business justification/independent approval; requester self-approval is not assumed; Break-glass is emergency-only, short-lived and audited.
- Support/debugging must not silently impersonate users. Staff cannot bypass Recognition/ranking/reviewer/qualification/governance systems through universal hidden fields.
- Engineering operations does not imply unrelated business/finance/moderation/verification/user-data/Organization-control authority. High-impact irreversible/bulk staff actions may use proportionate confirmation, narrower temporary Scope, independent/multi-person review. High-privilege actions enter Staff Audit Trail. Offboarding/role change revokes active internal authority/sessions/credentials without erasing history.
- High-sensitivity production/admin capability uses a distinct internal work-security boundary; ordinary community-session compromise must not automatically yield staff production/admin authority.
- Changing authorization policy/Role semantics/grant/deny/Scope/control rules is itself scoped, permissioned and audited. Engineering deployment capability alone does not authorize business/security/governance permission redesign. Material semantic expansion follows the grant-equivalent rule above.
- Conflict of Interest is first-class for consequential Recognition, qualification, identity/representation verification, Organization control/recovery, appeals, staff and relevant commercial/Partner cases. Outcomes may include disclosure, recusal, evidence exclusion, independent review/escalation. Detection remains purpose-limited/privacy-minimized.
- Reviewer identity is sensitive governance data with least-privilege purpose-limited access; editorial curiosity is not valid purpose and reviewer identity must not casually flow into Recommendation/profile/commercial/social systems. Public aggregation may be delayed/coarsened/withheld to prevent deanonymization.
- Appeal, Evidence Reversal and Re-evaluation are distinct. No universal appeal count. Duplicate unchanged submissions may be controlled; materially changed basis may reopen. Filing appeal does not automatically restore risky capability. A materially conflicted original decision-maker is not guaranteed sole final control; adaptive independent route as appropriate.

## 9. Identity abuse and disputes
- Same-name collision alone is not impersonation.
- Deceptive Representation is contextual: name/handle, avatar/likeness, bio, Organization marks/branding, visual presentation, claimed affiliation/authorization and surrounding behavior. Synthetic likeness/cloned voice/digital impersonation are included when deceptive. Clear non-confusing Parody/Fan/Commentary identities may exist.
- Identity Dispute != Username/Handle Dispute. Trademark, Copyright and Impersonation remain separate tracks even when overlapping. Report/dispute != guilt. Reporting ability != evidentiary authority. A Claim dispute first affects the disputed Claim.
- Serious systematic identity/credential/false-representation fraud may escalate after evidence/review. High-risk impersonation may justify proportionate temporary protection where money/official notices/Organization control/account integrity are materially at risk. Challenges are claim-proportional/privacy-minimizing; sensitive government ID is not an ordinary Account Trust shortcut.
- Historical identity/attribution is time-aware and preserves Authorship, Publisher, Operator and Representation-at-the-time, including during disputes.

## 10. Lifecycle, deletion and historical continuity
- Account, Stable Platform Identity, Authority-bearing Subject, Relationship, qualification/content/assets have separate lifecycle semantics. Deactivate/Suspend/Close/Delete are distinct; no single `user.deleted` concept may collapse them.
- Data disposition is category-specific: authentication/security, private personal, public profile, published Works, community activity, private communications, transactions, audit/security records.
- Account closure does not destructive-cascade public Works/citations/history/provenance. Private-data minimization/deletion remains separate. Inactivity != death/fraud/abandonment/entitlement transfer and does not trigger fixed-period deletion of public knowledge.
- Deceased-user handling is separate. Guardian/successor/executor/legal representative does not inherit login identity, posting-as-person, private messages, personal governance/reviewer/contributor standing or unrestricted private account data. Any lawful exception is separately scoped, minimized and audited.
- Minors/guardian relation is reserved without premature age/legal rules; guardian/legal/executor/representative is a Relationship, never identity substitution.
- Person-specific trust/qualification/governance standing is non-transferable. Personal Account/Stable Identity generally non-transferable; Organization control/representation may transfer separately.
- Dissolved/inactive/historical Organizations preserve historical identity/publications/representation/provenance. Rename/merge/split/succession preserve history. Merge is high-risk/audited and does not mechanically sum trust/governance. Split is first-class and supports correction/reassignment when subjects were wrongly conflated. Relationship termination is time-aware.
- Protective Hold may apply to irreversible disputed changes. Account deletion, private-data deletion, Work withdrawal, legal/policy removal, anonymization/hiding, public-knowledge retention and audit/legal retention are separate. Privacy-safe Tombstone may preserve stable reference where lawful. Historical Recognition != current availability/recommendation/policy status.

## 11. Provenance, privacy and contextual execution security
- Consequential decisions preserve privacy-appropriate decision-time provenance sufficient to reconstruct why action was allowed/limited/challenged/denied/escalated: Account, Actor, requester, authorizer(s), executor, Acting Context/subject, authority source/Scope versions, restriction, relevant policy/rule version, security result, timestamp/outcome as material.
- Accountability audit/provenance is not ordinary mutable business content. Ordinary admin/content rights cannot silently erase/rewrite it; corrections are normally additive/versioned/linked. Exceptional handling is separately authorized/audited. Privacy/legal deletion and retention still apply.
- Identity-linkage/anti-abuse correlation is sensitive and purpose-limited. Shared IP/device/timing/behavior/graph evidence may justify caution/investigation but does not automatically establish factual identity/affiliation/public relationship or forced merge.
- Use least-sensitive sufficient verification where feasible. Reviewer identity, COI data, identity linkage and recommendation/behavior telemetry stay purpose-bounded, minimized and retention-limited where relevant.
- Static Permission alone is insufficient for every action. Round 5 remains contextual execution-security engine (Allow/bounded Allow/Step-up/Friction/Protective Hold/Local Restrict/Capability Suspend/Human Review/account-level action where justified). Sensitive Operations Guard protects high-impact identity/control/representation/credential/grant/policy mutations adaptively, not as hidden levels.
