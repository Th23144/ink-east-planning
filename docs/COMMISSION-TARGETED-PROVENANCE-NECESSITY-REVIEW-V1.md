## 1. Provenance finding（来源结论）

Commission（约稿）entered the architecture in two stages:

1. Round 4E（第四轮 E 阶段）accepted editorial commissioning（编辑约稿）as one example of a higher-cost Contributor / Editorial Opportunity（贡献者 / 编辑机会）;
2. Round 11 Workshop B（第十一轮工作坊 B）expanded it into a first-class Editorial Acquisition Route（一级编辑获取路径）with detailed workflow semantics（工作流语义）.

There was no separately recorded user decision stating that Ink & East（墨与东方）must have a large dedicated Commission System（独立约稿系统）.

---

## 2. User redefinition（用户重新定义）

The user clarified that the real product need is much broader than “ask someone to write an article”.

The intended need is:

> when scarce / endangered knowledge, source material or cultural evidence（稀缺 / 濒失知识、资料或文化证据）is missing, Ink & East（墨与东方）should be able to ask the world for help, receive unsolicited leads/materials, verify them, digitize / restore / research them, collaborate with contributors or institutions, and reopen the resulting knowledge to the public.

For physical originals（实体原件）, Ink & East（墨与东方）may serve as a Bridge / Intermediary（桥梁 / 中间人）for documentation, research, digitization and referral, while long-term custody（长期保管）normally belongs with an appropriate qualified institution（合适的合格机构）where lawful and accepted.

---

## 3. Final classification（最终分类）

Commission（约稿）is **not** the umbrella product concept.

Current controlling umbrella concept（当前控制总概念）:

> **Open Cultural Contribution & Recovery（开放文化贡献与寻回）**

Commissioned Article / Editorial Invitation（约稿文章 / 编辑邀请）may remain later as a narrow optional editorial action（狭义可选编辑动作）if needed.

It must not be confused with:

- Donation（捐赠）;
- Deposit / Temporary Custody（寄存 / 临时保管）;
- Source Contribution（资料贡献）;
- Knowledge Contribution（知识贡献）;
- Institutional Referral（机构转介）;
- Research Collaboration（研究合作）.

---

## 4. Round 11（第十一轮）consequence

Round 11（第十一轮）Commission（约稿）semantics require an explicit later amendment so that the old first-class Commission framing（一级约稿框架）does not overstate the product need.

The valid surviving principles include:

- invitation / collaboration does not guarantee publication（邀请 / 合作不保证发布）;
- payment does not buy Recognition / Authority（付款不购买作品认可 / 权威）;
- authorship / provenance must remain attributable（作者身份 / 来源必须可归因）;
- rights / privacy / workflow boundaries remain distinct（权利 / 隐私 / 工作流边界保持分离）.

These principles may be reused inside Open Cultural Contribution & Recovery（开放文化贡献与寻回）where applicable.
