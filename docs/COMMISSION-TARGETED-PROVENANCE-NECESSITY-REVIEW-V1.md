## 5. What Commission currently means in the sealed architecture / 当前封存架构中的含义

Commission（约稿）currently means:

> Ink & East（墨与东方）editorial staff intentionally invites / assigns / collaborates with an author or contributor to create a work for an editorial need.

Typical conceptual example:

```text
Editorial need（编辑需求）
→ invite / commission a contributor（邀请 / 约稿贡献者）
→ brief / scope（简报 / 范围）
→ author creates work（作者创作）
→ editorial review（编辑评估）
→ possible publication / Issue inclusion（可能发布 / 议题收录）
```

Important:

- it is **not** Custom Reading（定制解读）;
- it is **not** Custom Ebook Studio（定制电子书工作室）;
- it is **not** Membership（会员）;
- it is **not automatically paid**;
- it is **not guaranteed publication**;
- it is primarily an Editorial Acquisition（编辑获取）concept in current Round 11 semantics.

## 6. Why it may or may not be needed / 为什么可能需要，也可能不需要

### Possible reason to keep it / 可能保留的理由

An editorial publication may occasionally need a specific missing:

- perspective（视角）;
- expertise（专业能力）;
- translation（翻译）;
- research（研究）;
- format（形式）;
- theme contribution（主题贡献）.

Instead of waiting for unsolicited submissions（自然投稿）, editors may proactively invite a suitable contributor.

This is a normal editorial capability in principle.

### Possible reason to simplify/remove the dedicated architecture / 可能简化或删除独立架构的理由

Project 3（项目三）may not need a large dedicated Commission System（约稿系统）if:

- direct editorial invitation（编辑邀请）is rare;
- it can be represented as an ordinary collaboration / editorial task（合作 / 编辑任务）;
- paid commissioning（付费约稿）is not part of the actual Ink & East（墨与东方）operating model;
- the detailed workflow adds architecture complexity without a real product need.

So the question is not:

> “Is commissioning a real-world thing?”

It is:

> **Does Ink & East（墨与东方）need Commission（约稿）as a first-class product/workflow concept, or is a lighter editorial invitation/collaboration model enough?**
