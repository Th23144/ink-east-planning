## 1. Confirmed three-layer cooperation model（已确认三层合作模型）

The long-term relationship uses three layers:

### Layer A — Core Strategic Collaboration（核心战略合作）
This is the part that explains why the Standing Strategic Relationship（持续战略关系） exists.

It includes:
- Knowledge ↔ Practice（知识 ↔ 实践）;
- Research / Material / Craft Projects（研究 / 材料 / 工艺项目）;
- appropriate Cultural Recovery Support（适当的文化寻回支持）;
- Knowledge ↔ Object / Project Linking（知识 ↔ 物件 / 项目关联）.

### Layer B — Optional Joint Projects（可选联合项目）
These may happen but do not define the relationship itself.

Examples include:
- related / joint publications（关联 / 联合发布）;
- exhibitions / talks / workshops / pop-ups（展览 / 讲座 / 工作坊 / 快闪）;
- jointly developed objects/products（联合开发物件 / 商品）;
- licensing（授权）;
- sourcing / supply（采购 / 供应）;
- project-specific commerce（项目型商业化）.

### Layer C — Contextual Discovery / Referral（情境化发现 / 引导）
Users may move between Ink & East（墨与东方）and Spatial Flow（空间流） where the crossover is genuinely relevant.

This is a consequence of real relationship/context, not the foundation of the relationship.

> **Cross-linking / referral（互链 / 引导） is an outcome, not the foundation.**

## 3. Anti-collapse rules（防塌缩规则）

The confirmed model does not mean:
- every Ink & East（墨与东方） research project needs Spatial Flow（空间流）;
- every Spatial Flow（空间流） object needs Ink & East（墨与东方） content;
- every collaboration becomes commerce;
- Spatial Flow（空间流） becomes the exclusive physical-world executor（唯一现实执行端）;
- Ink & East（墨与东方） becomes a marketing/content department;
- all user journeys become a funnel toward Spatial Flow（空间流）.
