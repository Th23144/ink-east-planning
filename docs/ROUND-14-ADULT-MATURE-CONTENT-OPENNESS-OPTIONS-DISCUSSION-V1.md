## 1. Avoid one misleading 18+ switch（不要用一个“18+开关”掩盖不同内容）
Decide independently by **purpose, medium, explicitness, audience and distribution（目的、媒介、露骨程度、受众、分发方式）**. Artistic/historical/educational nudity, adult discussion, erotic writing, explicit images/video, sexual-service solicitation and non-consensual/abusive imagery are not the same.

Mature themes may include adult language, sex education, historical texts, reproductive health, queer studies, non-erotic nude fine art, violence in cultural documentation, and adult fictional writing（成熟主题可含粗口、性教育、历史原文、健康、艺术裸体、成人文学等）.

## 2. Three package choices for consideration（三种候选开放范围）
**A — Contextual mature topics（主题性成熟内容）**:
Allow mature historical, educational, artistic, scientific and ordinary personal discussion; contextual non-erotic nudity where lawful and appropriately presented. Prohibit sexual-gratification-targeted explicit pornographic imagery/video and erotica solely for arousal. Avoid categorical censorship of sex-related study.

**B — Adult expressive publishing with strict viewer control（更开放的成人表达，但严格由读者选择）**:
Everything in A plus potentially adult erotic literary expression and some consensual adult artistic nudity, subject to explicit categories, adult-only access where required, user opt-in filters, no unwanted exposure, rights/consent checks and no automatic mainstream recommendation. Exact imagery/video explicitness remains separately decided. This creates serious app-distribution/merchant risk to resolve first.

**C — Explicit adult media hosted（明确开放露骨成人媒体）**:
Everything in B plus intentionally sexual-gratification-centered consensual adult explicit image/video uploads. Requires a separate substantial governance/compliance and infrastructure plan; cannot be conflated with ordinary cultural publishing. High payment/app-store/distribution constraints.

The user should **not** be pressured to choose A/B/C solely as a one-size-fits-all policy; granular choices for (1) education/arts, (2) adult nude art, (3) explicit erotic text, (4) explicit adult imagery/video, (5) monetization and recommendation may be more precise（可分别决定艺术教育、艺术裸体、成人情色文字、露骨图片 / 视频、商业化与推荐）.

## 3. Protection is not ideological review（保护用户选择，不做思想审查）
Whatever the breadth:
- Protect minors and individuals from non-consensual intimate material, sexual exploitation, abuse, coercion or illegal content（严格保护未成年人、禁止未经同意的私密影像、剥削及违法内容）.
- Age/visibility settings and refusal-to-see preferences are product functionality, not Recognition（作品认可） or author prestige（限龄 / 不展示偏好属于产品功能，与作者等级无关）.
- An adult category label must not mean a work is low-quality, false or academically unrecognizable（成人内容标签不等于低质量 / 假 / 不可认可）.
- Distinguish speech allowed to exist vs content recommended to all vs external payment/app distribution（允许存在、默认推荐、第三方支付 / 上架相互独立）.
- Practical rules depend on the serving jurisdictions, product age audience, hosting/service partners, and web-versus-native distribution（需按所在法域、受众、服务商及网页 / 原生应用分别核实）.

## 4. Official external constraints reviewed（已查官方约束，2026-10）
- X official Adult Content Policy（成人内容规则） permits labeled consensually made adult content, restricts high-visibility placement and underage access: https://help.x.com/en/rules-and-policies/adult-content
- Apple App Review Guidelines（应用审核指南） 1.1.4 / 1.2 restrict overtly pornographic apps and UGC platforms primarily used for porn; narrow allowance for incidental, default-hidden web-generated NSFW: https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/
- Google Play User Generated Content（用户生成内容） and Inappropriate Content（不适宜内容） rules restrict explicit sexual material, default-hide incidental adult material and protect minors: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9876937 ; https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9878810
- Stripe Restricted Businesses / FAQs（受限业务 / 常见问题） state that sexual-gratification-focused pornography and other adult material/services, including some erotic literature, are not supported for sale/offering through Stripe's supported-business scope: https://stripe.com/legal/restricted-businesses ; https://support.stripe.com/questions/prohibited-and-restricted-businesses-list-faqs

**These are service conditions and design constraints, not automatically a legal ban on all 18+ speech（这些是第三方服务限制，不是对所有18+言论的法律禁令）**. Check the specific product, region, jurisdiction and service contracts before rollout.

## 5. Recommended order（推荐决策顺序）
1. Decide content categories and media separately（分别确认内容类别 / 媒介）.
2. Decide adult-reader agency: default-hidden category, filter, explicit consent to see and no surprise exposure（成年读者可主动控制，默认不突然曝光）.
3. Decide minor access and geographic obligations based on actual product markets（根据实际市场决定未成年人访问与法域义务）.
4. Decide whether adult-tagged content has access to public Home, Search, Recommendation, Candidate, Recognition, commercial promotion（成人内容是否进入首页、搜索、推荐、候选 / 认可 / 付费加热）.
5. Validate payments, hosting and mobile app distribution before implementation（实现前核实支付、托管和应用市场约束）.

Culture-platform positioning and long-term dedicated adult-content monetization exclusion are confirmed; exact mature-content categories, permitted explicitness, discovery and age rules are still undecided（文化平台定位和长期排除专门成人商业模式已经确认；具体成人表达尺度、发现及限龄政策尚未确认）.
