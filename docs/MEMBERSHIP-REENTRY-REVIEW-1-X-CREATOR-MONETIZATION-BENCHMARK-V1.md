# 会员重启检查 #1——X（社交平台）创作者商业化基准 V1

> **Status（状态）:** EXTERNAL BENCHMARK / NON-CONTROLLING（外部基准 / 非控制性）
> **Checked（检查日期）:** 2026-10-04
> **Implementation（实现）:** NOT AUTHORIZED（未授权）

## 1. Why this benchmark matters（为什么研究这一模式）

The user's screenshot shows X Premium（X 高级订阅） advertising “Get paid to post（发帖可获得收入）”.

This must not be confused with:
- equity ownership（股权所有权）;
- profit/dividend rights（利润 / 分红权）;
- supporter investment returns（支持者投资回报）.

It is a creator monetization / platform reward model（创作者商业化 / 平台奖励模型）.

## 2. Current X model as of 2026-10-04（截至 2026-10-04 的 X 当前模式）

X's prior Creator Revenue Sharing（创作者收入分成） program stopped accepting new enrollments on 2026-08-07 and was retired on 2026-09-07.

It has been replaced by Original Content Rewards（原创内容奖励）.

Current Original Content Rewards（原创内容奖励）:
- rewards eligible creators for original, high-quality content（奖励符合资格的原创高质量内容）;
- payout is based on Qualified Impressions（合格展示）;
- Qualified Impressions（合格展示） currently means unique Home Timeline（首页时间线） impressions from Premium（高级订阅） users where at least 50% of the post is visible;
- paid/promoted/artificial/fraudulent impressions are excluded;
- payouts are issued every two weeks subject to program requirements.

Current published eligibility includes:
- 18+;
- supported country;
- account in good standing;
- Personal or Business Account（个人或商业账户）;
- active Premium / Premium+ / Premium Business（高级订阅 / 高级订阅增强版 / 商业高级订阅）;
- at least 500,000 qualifying Home Timeline impressions from verified users in the last 90 days;
- at least 500 verified followers;
- active original-content posting.

Exact program economics are controlled by X and may change.

## 3. Why this is legally/product-wise different from “supporter gets platform ad revenue”（为什么这与“支持者分平台广告收入”不同）

The economic cause is:

> **creator produces eligible content → content generates qualified platform attention → platform pays creator under a creator-reward program.**

Not:

> **user/supporter pays or joins early → acquires a right to share future platform revenue.**

Therefore it is much closer to Creator Compensation（创作者报酬） / Content Incentive（内容激励） than investment return（投资回报）.

## 4. X Creator Subscriptions（X 创作者订阅） is another separate model

X also supports Creator Subscriptions（创作者订阅）:
- followers pay a creator a monthly subscription;
- the creator provides bonus content / closer access;
- current X documentation states creators may receive up to about 97% of gross subscription revenue, subject to third-party/app-store fees and other applicable adjustments.

This is:
> **fan → creator recurring purchase/support（粉丝 → 创作者持续购买 / 支持）**

and is different from Original Content Rewards（原创内容奖励）.

## 5. Product lesson for Ink & East（墨与东方）

The strongest transferable idea is:

> **A platform can economically reward users for producing measurable value without turning early supporters into investors.**

Potential future Ink & East（墨与东方） models could separately include:
- Creator Revenue / Content Reward（创作者收入 / 内容奖励）;
- Referral Reward（推荐奖励）;
- project-specific compensation（项目型报酬）;
- qualified commercial lead reward（合格商业线索奖励）;
- other contribution-linked compensation（其他与实际行为挂钩的报酬）.

These should be based on actual value-generating behavior, not on historical supporter payment.

This keeps community belonging, supporter history and economic compensation as separate axes.
