# 会员重启检查 #1——会员关系与早期共建者关系分离结论 V1

## 1. Separate relationship axes（分离关系轴）

Membership / Supporter Relationship（会员 / 支持者关系） and Early Co-builder Relationship（早期共建者关系） are formally separate.

### Early Co-builder Relationship（早期共建者关系）
A historical relationship answering:

> What did this person materially do during the formative stage of Ink & East（墨与东方）?（这个人在平台形成阶段实际做过什么？）

### Membership / Supporter Relationship（会员 / 支持者关系）
A current/ongoing relationship answering:

> What support / Membership relationship does this user currently maintain with Ink & East（墨与东方）?（这个用户当前与平台维持什么支持 / 会员关系？）

## 2. Historical relationship survives payment changes（历史关系不随付款变化消失）

A genuine Early Co-builder（早期共建者） history remains valid even if:
- the user later stops Membership/payment（会员 / 付款）;
- pricing or Membership packaging changes（定价 / 会员包装变化）;
- the user never paid at all.

Historical co-building is not revoked by later commercial status.

## 3. Membership cannot purchase early history（会员不能购买早期历史）

Paying for Membership（会员） alone is never sufficient to create Early Co-builder（早期共建者） status/history.

A later user may become a strong current Supporter / Member（支持者 / 会员） without retroactively becoming an early co-builder.

## 4. Later Members are not lesser Members（后来加入的会员不是低一等会员）

A user who joins Membership（会员） later should not be treated as lower-status merely because they were not present in the platform's formative stage.

Membership value is current relationship value, not a founding-age hierarchy.

## 5. “Spiritual Shareholder（精神股东）” stays outside Membership tiers（“精神股东”不进入会员档位）

“Spiritual Shareholder（精神股东）” remains a possible public/community metaphor for early co-building/belonging.

It must not become:
- a Membership tier（会员档位）;
- a price package（价格套餐）;
- a status purchasable by payment amount（按付款金额购买的身份）.

Underlying architecture remains:
> Early Co-builder Relationship（早期共建者关系）.
