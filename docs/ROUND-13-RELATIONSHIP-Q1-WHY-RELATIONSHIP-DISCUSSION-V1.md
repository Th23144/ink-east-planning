## 0. Why Q1 comes first（为什么先问这一题）

Before discussing:
- Brand Relationship（品牌关系）;
- Cross-link（互链）;
- Commercial Cooperation（商业合作）;
- Account / Data（账户 / 数据）;
- Technical Sharing（技术共享）;

the architecture must first prove that a relationship creates genuine value.

Otherwise the correct relationship may simply be:
> independent products with occasional ad-hoc cooperation（独立产品 + 偶发临时合作）.

The relationship must not exist merely because both products are currently developed by the same team.

## 1. Five possible value sources to test（需要验证的五类可能价值来源）

These are hypotheses（假设）, not Current Truth（当前有效真相）.

### H1 — Subject / worldview overlap（主题 / 世界观重叠）
Possibility:
Ink & East（墨与东方）and Spatial Flow（空间流）may touch some overlapping cultural, material, spatial or experiential subjects（文化、物质、空间或体验主题）.

Question:
> Is this overlap deep enough to create a durable relationship, or is it merely occasional thematic coincidence（偶发主题巧合）?

### H2 — Knowledge ↔ physical-world complementarity（知识 ↔ 现实物质世界互补）
Possibility:
Ink & East（墨与东方）may explain history, meaning, provenance and context（历史、意义、来源与语境）;
Spatial Flow（空间流）may operate in objects, materials, products and lived space（物件、材料、商品与现实空间）.

Question:
> Does one product make the other materially better, or can each achieve its mission equally well alone?

### H3 — Audience overlap（用户群重叠）
Possibility:
Some users interested in Ink & East（墨与东方）may also be interested in Spatial Flow（空间流）, and vice versa.

Question:
> Is that overlap meaningful enough to justify a designed relationship, or should users discover the other product only incidentally?

### H4 — Resource / capability complementarity（资源 / 能力互补）
Possibility:
The two independent products may possess different resources, such as:
- research / cultural context（研究 / 文化语境）;
- physical materials / suppliers / makers（实体材料 / 供应商 / 制作者）;
- photography / documentation（摄影 / 记录）;
- real-world objects / spaces（现实物件 / 空间）;
- distribution / commercial capabilities（分发 / 商业能力）.

Question:
> Are there collaborations that neither product can perform as well alone?

### H5 — Strategic / business complementarity（战略 / 商业互补）
Possibility:
A relationship may create:
- better acquisition（更好的获客）;
- broader brand ecosystem（更广的品牌生态）;
- research-to-product or product-to-research opportunities（研究到产品 / 产品到研究机会）;
- joint projects（联合项目）;
- resilience through multiple revenue/value channels（多收入 / 价值渠道带来的韧性）.

Question:
> Is this a genuine strategic advantage, or would formalizing the relationship create more confusion than value?

---

## 2. Important negative test（重要反向测试）

For every proposed relationship benefit, ask:

> **If the other product disappeared tomorrow, what specific capability / experience / value would this product lose?（如果另一个产品明天消失，这个产品具体会失去什么能力 / 体验 / 价值？）**

If the answer is “almost nothing（几乎没有）”, the relationship should remain light.

If the answer is concrete and recurring（具体且持续）, the relationship may deserve formal product / brand / commercial architecture（产品 / 品牌 / 商业架构）.

---

## 3. What Q1 does NOT decide（Q1 不决定什么）

Q1 does not yet decide:

- whether they share a parent brand（是否共享母品牌）;
- whether they link to each other（是否互链）;
- whether they share users/accounts（是否共享用户 / 账户）;
- whether they share data（是否共享数据）;
- whether there is revenue sharing（是否分成）;
- whether Ink & East（墨与东方）may recommend Spatial Flow（空间流）products;
- whether they share technical infrastructure（是否共享技术基础设施）.

Those belong to later relationship layers only after genuine mutual value is established.
