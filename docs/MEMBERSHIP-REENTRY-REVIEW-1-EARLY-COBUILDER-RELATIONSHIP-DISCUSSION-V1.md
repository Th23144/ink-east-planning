## 1. Starting principle（起点原则）

The user confirms an important direction:

> **An early/founding user does not create value only by paying money.（元老用户的价值不只来自付费。）**

Early Ink & East（墨与东方） should recognize that several different behaviors may materially help the platform survive, improve and grow.

The discussion therefore shifts from “who paid?” to:

> **Who materially helped build the early platform, and how should that history be remembered without creating a permanent caste?（谁真正帮助平台完成了早期共建，以及平台该如何记住这段历史而不制造永久阶级？）**

---

## 2. Candidate sources of early co-building value（早期共建价值的候选来源）

These are independent evidence types, not one universal score.

### A — Early Presence（早期在场）
- registered/active during the formative period（在平台形成期注册 / 活跃）;
- remained meaningfully engaged（持续真实参与）.

Early presence alone may justify historical acknowledgment, but not high privilege.

### B — Financial Support（资金支持）
- voluntarily supported Ink & East（墨与东方） financially;
- helped sustain public-interest/cultural work（帮助维持公共价值 / 文化工作）.

Financial support should be acknowledged, but amount/frequency must not become authority.

### C — Quality Publishing / Knowledge Participation（优质发布 / 知识参与）
- published genuinely useful work（发布真正有价值的作品）;
- helped establish healthy early content/community norms（帮助形成健康的早期内容 / 社区规范）.

This remains separate from Contributor Qualification（贡献者资格） and Work Recognition（作品认可）.

### D — Network Growth（网络增长）
- invited/referred real users（带来真实用户）;
- helped viable communities/topic clusters emerge（帮助形成有效社区 / 主题群）;
- created durable connections between people/organizations（促成真实持久的人 / 组织连接）.

This should be measured carefully to avoid spam/referral farming.

### E — Product Co-building（产品共建）
- beta testing（测试）;
- high-quality bug reports（高质量问题反馈）;
- product research participation（产品研究参与）;
- repeated actionable feedback（持续可执行反馈）.

### F — Cultural Recovery / Project Participation（文化寻回 / 项目参与）
- legitimate Recovery Participation（寻回参与）;
- documentation / digitization / research assistance（记录 / 数字化 / 研究协助）;
- project-specific help（项目型协助）.

This retains the existing Recovery Participant / Role / Attribution（寻回参与者 / 角色 / 归因） semantics and must not be relabeled as Contributor（贡献者）.

---

## 3. Do not collapse these into one score（不要压成一个总分）

A universal “Founder Score / Co-builder Score（创始分 / 共建分）” would recreate the exact status/weight architecture the project has repeatedly rejected.

Instead, the platform may preserve typed historical records such as:
- early participant（早期参与）;
- supporter history（支持历史）;
- referral/growth contribution（增长 / 推荐贡献）;
- product research participation（产品研究参与）;
- cultural-recovery attribution（文化寻回归因）;
- publication history（发布历史）.

Different future opportunities may use different relevant histories.

---

## 4. Potential special treatment（可能的特殊待遇）

Special treatment should be legitimate relationship value, not authority transfer.

Possible directions:
- founding-stage historical acknowledgment（创始阶段历史致谢）;
- selected early-access opportunities（部分优先体验机会）;
- product research / feedback invitations（产品研究 / 反馈邀请）;
- platform/project briefings（平台 / 项目简报）;
- anniversary / milestone recognition（周年 / 里程碑纪念）;
- selected event / partner courtesy（部分活动 / 合作生态礼遇）;
- future economic rewards only when tied to actual value-generating activity（未来经济奖励仅与实际价值创造行为挂钩）.

Not allowed by default:
- governance votes with binding authority（有约束力治理投票）;
- editorial authority（编辑权）;
- Work Recognition（作品认可） advantage;
- Contributor Qualification（贡献者资格）;
- source/truth authority（来源 / 真相权威）;
- hidden organic ranking priority（隐藏自然排序优先）.

---

## 5. “Spiritual Shareholder（精神股东）” as culture, not legal ownership

The phrase may work as an informal cultural metaphor for:
- belonging（归属）;
- shared building history（共同建设历史）;
- “I was here when this was small”（我在平台还很小时就在这里）.

But the product should use a more precise underlying architecture than the slogan itself.

Possible internal concept:
> **Early Co-builder Relationship（早期共建者关系）**

Public naming remains open.

---

## 6. Economic participation without investment rights（不依赖投资权的经济参与）

X（社交平台） provides a useful reference:
eligible creators can earn through Original Content Rewards（原创内容奖励） based on qualified content attention.

Ink & East（墨与东方） could later explore economic participation tied to:
- original useful content（原创有用内容）;
- referral/growth outcomes（推荐 / 增长结果）;
- project work（项目工作）;
- commercial lead generation（商业线索产生）;
- other measurable value creation（其他可测量价值创造）.

This would be compensation/reward for actual activity, not a return merely for being an early supporter.

---

## 7. Current decision questions（当前决策题）

1. Should “early co-builder（早期共建者）” be broader than “financial supporter（资金支持者）”?  
2. Should early co-building history be preserved through multiple typed records rather than one points/level system（积分 / 等级系统）?  
3. Should the platform allow special historical/relationship treatment without granting governance/editorial/Recognition authority（治理 / 编辑 / 认可权）?  
4. Should future economic participation be explored primarily as activity-linked rewards/compensation（与实际行为挂钩的奖励 / 报酬）, rather than returns for supporter status itself?  
5. Is “Spiritual Shareholder（精神股东）” better treated as public/community language while Early Co-builder Relationship（早期共建者关系） remains the underlying architecture?
