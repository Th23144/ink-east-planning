# 3. Round 3 parity / 第三轮完整性

The safe layer preserves the material Round 3 architecture:

- Normal → Candidate → Recognized as the small public quality-state model;
- nomination eligibility/exposure are workflow/distribution states, not prestige tiers;
- semi-blind Formal Nomination（正式推举） suppresses unnecessary prestige/social proof where feasible;
- Candidate Review（候选评审） restores provenance needed for serious verification;
- Integrity Gate + Shared Quality Core + Content-Type Rubric remain conceptually distinct;
- review is structured, not one star/percentage/average score;
- Reviewer Trust（评审信任） is separate from Account Trust / Contributor Qualification;
- Reviewer Quality cannot be majority-agreement self-validation;
- reviewer identity is privacy-sensitive and public aggregation must resist deanonymization;
- clear cases may transition under policy while exceptional/contested cases enter Human Governance（人工治理）;
- editorial taste/viewpoint disagreement alone is not a valid quality override;
- incentives do not pay raw review/action counts;
- contaminated evidence is repaired at the finest justified granularity;
- Not Recognized（未获认可） ends the current Candidate lifecycle without creating a permanent public negative badge;
- Evidence Insufficient（证据不足） may pause/dormant the case rather than become a negative verdict;
- same-work substantive revision uses Delta Review / Revalidation（差异评审 / 重新验证）;
- replacement-level rewrite restarts the normal Recognition journey;
- Founding Reader（创始读者） is cold-start strategy only, not a permanent privileged caste.

The final three lifecycle gaps R3-C1 / R3-C2 / R3-C3 were specifically restored during this parity pass.

**Round 3 result: PASS.**

# 5. Round 5 parity / 第五轮完整性

Preserved:

- no universal Account Weight / Trust Score;
- multi-dimensional Account Trust evidence;
- Account Trust and Reviewer Trust permanently separate;
- capabilities unlock individually;
- internal/farmable scoring mechanics remain non-public;
- account age requires sustained normal use;
- raw action counts are not the growth mechanism;
- popularity is not Account Trust;

- consequential capability loss gets bounded understandable transparency;
- serious/consequential restrictions can support review/reconsideration;

## Round 5D–5E

Preserved:

- controls exist for abuse-prone actions, not as a gamified new-user ladder;
- sensitive identity proof is not the highest/default Account Trust shortcut;
- low-intrusion anti-abuse challenges are preferred where sufficient;
- a challenge proves only the narrow uncertainty it addresses;
- Membership may reduce some economic/Sybil friction but cannot manufacture historical behavior, qualification, Recognition, Reviewer Trust, Governance Authority or organic reach;
- capability Risk Profiles（风险画像） are multidimensional;
- Publishing Capability != Recommendation Eligibility != Recommendation Ranking;
- no global level/tier system for trust/governance.

**Result: PASS.**

## Round 5F — Sensitive Operations Guard

Preserved:

- local intervention + cross-cutting Protective Containment（防护性控制） when credible account-integrity/evasion/takeover risk requires it;
- guard is not a punishment tier;
- identity/control/trust-claim/authority/evidence/high-impact-exit mutations may be sensitive;
- low-confidence anomaly does not justify blanket freeze;
- secure recovery remains available;
- allow / step-up / hold / local restriction / broader action are contextual outcomes;
- consequential user-facing status may be coarse while detection internals remain hidden.

**Result: PASS.**

## Round 5G — Multi-account / Sybil / coordinated abuse

Preserved:

- multiple accounts != abuse;
- Deceptive Independence（欺骗性独立伪装） is the core integrity problem;
- IP/device/VPN/shared network is weak context, not proof;
- legitimate coordination != coordinated abuse;
- Signal Neutralization（信号中和） before unnecessary punishment where appropriate;
- Evidence Count != Independent Evidence;
- reports/nominations/reviews from coordinated actors are not mechanically independent;
- restriction-evasion may be handled across related accounts when evidence supports it;
- bounded Cluster Containment（关联账号簇控制） may apply under credible active attack;
- Membership/qualification/Organization identity is not an independence whitelist;
- false linkage supports review + Evidence Reversal;
- linkage graphs/thresholds remain non-public/non-farmable.

**Result: PASS.**

## Round 5H

Preserved:

- Account Compromise（账号被盗） != Owner Abuse（用户本人滥用）;
- sensitive mutation classes remain auditable;
- local anomalies do not automatically freeze unrelated operations;
- high-trust relationship changes preserve history;
- public deletion and required internal audit retention remain distinct;
