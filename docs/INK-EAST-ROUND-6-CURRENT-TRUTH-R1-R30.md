## Core model

Account, Actor, Display Identity, Stable Platform Identity, Knowledge Entity, Authority-bearing Subject, Claim, Relationship, Role, Permission, Grant Authority, Scope, Acting Context, Authorship, Publisher, Operator, Requesting Principal and Executor are separate concepts. Role is a permission bundle, not a trust/status level. Permission is atomic and scoped. Identity, factual verification, Contributor Qualification, Work Recognition, Account Trust, Reviewer Trust, Governance Authority and Membership are separate systems.

Ordinary users keep a simple path. Advanced Organization, delegation, staff, recovery and high-risk controls appear only where relevant. Architecture must be precise about boundaries without making contextual rules rigid. No global user levels or universal prestige score.

## Accepted revalidation rules

**R1.** Knowledge Entity is the broad graph concept; Authority-bearing Subject is the narrower authorization concept. Person and Organization are current primary authority-bearing subjects. Place, Work, Concept and similar graph objects do not gain operational authority merely because they are entities.

**R2.** Stable Platform Identity is durable platform continuity, not proof of a verified real-world Person. Ordinary pseudonymous participation does not require real-world identity resolution.

**R3.** Claim != Relationship != Permission. Verified facts may inform later decisions but never silently create representation or operational authority.

**R4.** Consequential decisions retain privacy-appropriate decision-time provenance sufficient to reconstruct why an action was allowed, limited, challenged, denied or escalated at that time.

**R5.** Re-registration or reuse of email, phone, handle, username, domain or similar identifier does not resurrect the former identity, data, trust, qualification, governance standing or Organization authority.

**R6.** In Organization recovery, present control of a domain/email/phone/social channel is evidence but not conclusive proof of entitlement.

**R7.** Automated actors retain controller/authorizer, grant source, Scope and revocation provenance. Automation does not inherit human reviewer/governance standing.

**R8.** Consequential appeals require a conflict-controlled independent route where appropriate; no universal fixed reviewer count is imposed.

**R9.** Use Permission and Grant/Delegate Permission are distinct. Manage Members/Manage Roles cannot manufacture higher authority. Custom Roles cannot include permissions the creator is not authorized to grant.

**R10.** Accountability audit/provenance records are not ordinary mutable business content. Corrections are normally additive/versioned; exceptional handling is separately authorized and audited.

**R11.** Personal Platform Context may use the authenticated Actor + Stable Platform Identity without resolving a real-world Person. Acting for an Organization still requires relevant representation and authority.

**R12.** A knowledge record describing an Organization does not create operational Organization control. Knowledge-layer facts and operational authority remain separate.

**R13.** Multiple accounts/personas associated with one real-world Person do not automatically share Organization roles, staff capacity, qualifications, reviewer/governance capability or comparable standing.

**R14.** Knowledge reconciliation, real-world merger/succession, Organization split, subject correction and operational-control migration are different events. None automatically unions controllers, grants or sensitive authority.

**R15.** Creating/editing a Person or Organization knowledge record does not prove identity, representation, official status or operational authority. Any verified linkage is separate and provenance-bearing.

**R16.** Expanding a shared Role/template is itself a grant event to affected holders. The change requires authority to grant the added Permission within affected Scope and retains audit/provenance.

**R17.** Pending invitations, transfers, delegations and deferred authority changes revalidate source authority, Scope, validity and current security context when they activate or execute.

**R18.** Protected capabilities require a current valid authorization source granting the required Permission for required Scope/context. Absence of a valid grant is not implicit Allow. Ordinary public actions that intentionally need no special Permission remain ordinary product actions.

**R19.** Independent approval is an integrity property, not merely different account IDs. Alternate accounts or automation under the same effective decision-maker cannot manufacture independence.

**R20.** Sessions, tokens, signed links and cached authorization decisions carry authority but do not become permanent authority sources after underlying authority changes.

**R21.** Independence is contextual. Distinct authorized people in the same Organization may satisfy a legitimate independent-review or dual-control requirement when they are genuinely independent for that decision and not disqualified by relevant conflict/common control.

**R22.** Approval/confirmation/acceptance for a consequential operation binds to the material semantics actually approved: target, recipient, Scope, authority changed/granted and other security-relevant parameters. Material change requires re-evaluation where appropriate.

**R23.** Executor capability is separate from principal authority. A platform service acting for a user or Organization cannot use broader technical capability to bypass the principal's authorization or Scope. Platform-originated actions use explicit platform authority and provenance.

**R24.** Where a service/worker executes for another principal, consequential provenance preserves both the requesting/authorizing principal and the actual executor where applicable.

**R25.** Permissions, scopes, approvals, grants and authority relationships bind to stable internal object identity rather than only mutable/reusable visible identifiers.

**R26.** Derived grants preserve enough source lineage to evaluate downstream authority if an upstream source expires, is revoked, narrowed, corrected or found invalid. Do not blindly cascade-delete all downstream authority when an independent valid source exists.

**R27.** Consequential authority/control changes finalize against a coherent current state. Concurrent or intervening changes cannot combine stale individually-valid decisions into an invalid resulting state. Exact transaction technology is deferred.

**R28.** Invitations, approvals, acceptances, transfers and comparable bounded authority artifacts have explicit lifecycle semantics that prevent unintended repeated use while allowing safe retry/idempotent processing.

**R29.** Guardian, successor, executor, estate/legal representative or similar status does not automatically grant the represented person's login identity, private messages or unrestricted private-data access. Any exception is separately scoped, minimized and auditable.

**R30.** Changing authorization policy, sensitive role semantics, grant rules or control rules is itself a scoped, permissioned and auditable high-sensitivity operation. Engineering/deployment capability alone does not grant authority to redefine business/security/Organization-control permissions.

## Original Round 6 rules preserved

Organization is the base organization type; Institution is a subtype. Platform-owned/Official/Editorial/Partner describe relationships or capacities, not peer subject types. Organization hierarchy does not automatically grant authority. Entity Control Authority is separate from Legal Ownership. Highest control may be shared. Control Continuity prevents accidental orphaning but does not forbid intentional closure/dissolution.

Representation is scoped, changeable, revocable and time-aware. Organization invitation normally uses the invitee's own account plus explicit Scope. Actual Actor and public attribution are distinct. Public publishing/comments/replies must use clear Acting Context where personal vs Organization identity matters. Personal actions stay personal. Author, Publisher and Operator may differ. Multi-author/contributor relationships are first-class.

Effective Permission is computed from valid sources at Permission + Scope level, not by highest Role label. Applicable security Deny/restriction overrides ordinary Allow within its Scope. Consequential execution rechecks current authority/security state. Delegation is non-transitive by default. Legitimate Joint Action is allowed, but cross-subject permission fusion is prohibited.

Staff authority is separate from community identity and person-specific governance standing. Internal responsibility domains, Need-to-know, Purpose Limitation, temporary elevation, separation of duties, audited break-glass and dedicated staff audit apply according to risk. Ordinary community-session compromise must not automatically yield production/admin authority.

Verification is claim-specific. Unverified != false. Organization authenticity does not certify every publication. Contributor Qualification, Expertise Scope, Partner status, Recognition, Recommendation, Governance and Membership remain separate. Membership cannot buy factual verification or governance standing.

Display Identity and Stable Platform Identity are separate. Legitimate name changes do not create a new identity or rewrite history. Same-name collision alone is not deceptive representation. Identity/handle/trademark/copyright disputes remain distinct tracks where relevant. Reports are not guilt. Historical attribution remains time-aware.

Appeal, Evidence Reversal and Re-evaluation are distinct. No universal fixed appeal count. Filing an appeal does not automatically restore risky capability.

Account, Stable Platform Identity, Authority-bearing Subject and Relationship lifecycles are separate. Deactivate, Suspend, Close and Delete are distinct. Account closure does not automatically erase public Works, citations, Organization history or provenance, while private-data minimization/deletion remains separately governed. Inactivity does not imply death/fraud/abandonment. Personal trust/qualification/governance standing is non-transferable. Organization rename/merge/split/succession preserve provenance rather than silently rewriting history.

Account deletion, private-data deletion, Work withdrawal, legal/policy removal, profile anonymization, public-knowledge retention and audit/legal retention are separate operations. Historical Recognition and current availability/recommendation/policy state are separate.

Conflict of Interest is first-class across consequential governance/verification/recovery decisions. Reviewer identity and identity-linkage data are sensitive, least-privilege and purpose-limited. Anti-abuse correlation does not automatically establish a public factual identity or relationship.

Public presentation explains what was verified and avoids prestige/status stacking. Profile context does not automatically determine work standing.

## Hard implementation boundaries

No valid grant for a protected capability means no authority. Use authority is not grant authority. Stable internal IDs carry authority references, not mutable handles. Principal authority cannot be bypassed by executor power. Approval binds to what was materially approved. Pending authority is rechecked at activation. Derived authorization artifacts do not outlive invalid underlying authority by accident. Consequential authority changes use coherent current state. Bounded authority artifacts cannot be replayed unintentionally. Authorization-policy mutation is itself governed authority. Accountability records cannot be silently rewritten through ordinary admin permissions.
