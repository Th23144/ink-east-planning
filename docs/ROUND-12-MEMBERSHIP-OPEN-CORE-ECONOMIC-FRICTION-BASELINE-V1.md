## 0. Why this baseline exists / 为什么需要这份基线

The previous Round 12 restart draft incorrectly assumed that Membership must first justify itself through a paid bundle of advanced utility, supporter relationship and scarce participation.

That assumption is not supported by the Rounds 1–5 product logic.

The revalidated source-backed rule is narrower:

> **Membership / payment may act as an economic-cost signal that reduces selected zero-cost / Sybil abuse friction for new or low-trust accounts, allowing some capability restrictions to be relaxed earlier.**

This does **not** mean:

> paid account = trusted account.

It also does not mean:

> Membership must manufacture exclusive product value by withholding ordinary platform capabilities from non-paying users.

The correct Round 12 restart therefore begins from **Open Core + Contextual Economic Friction**, not from a benefit catalogue.

---

# 1. Source-backed rule / 已有来源支持的规则

Rounds 1–5 established:

- a newly registered account may participate in Community but may initially face stricter publishing limits because its intent/behavior is not yet known;
- possible initial controls include shorter maximum length, lower publishing frequency/rate limits, additional moderation checks and limited access to richer long-form publishing tools;
- established accounts may gain broader publishing capability through accumulated legitimate account history/trust signals;
- paid Membership/VIP status may provide expanded publishing capability;
- payment may reduce selected zero-cost / Sybil friction;
- payment does not create professional qualification, Work Recognition, Reviewer Trust, Governance Authority, factual/source authority or organic recommendation privilege.

Later architecture replaced any universal Account Weight / Trust Score with:

> **Account × Capability × Context（账户 × 能力 × 情境）**

Therefore Membership/payment cannot operate as one global “weight”.

---

# 2. Controlling Round 12 principles / 第十二轮控制原则

## R12-OF1 — Open Core / 核心产品开放

Ordinary users must be able to use the platform's normal core product loops without Membership.

Membership must not be justified by deliberately withholding ordinary platform capabilities merely to create something to sell.

This includes the already-confirmed public/non-paywall rule for ordinary published content.

Exact capacity, safety and abuse controls may still differ by context; “open” does not mean “unlimited”.

---

## R12-OF2 — Payment may be an economic-friction signal / 付费可以成为经济摩擦信号

Payment may be considered as evidence that an account is no longer costless to create or replace.

Where a restriction exists specifically to resist:

- disposable accounts;
- bulk account farming;
- Sybil behavior;
- zero-cost spam/abuse;

payment may justify earlier relaxation of **that specific restriction**.

This is a risk-control mechanism, not a prestige benefit.

---

## R12-OF3 — Payment != Trust / 付费不等于信任

Membership/payment must not directly create:

- Behavioral Trust;
- Contributor Qualification;
- Expertise;
- factual verification;
- Work Recognition;
- Reviewer Trust;
- Formal Nomination authority;
- Governance Authority;
- organic Recommendation advantage;
- epistemic/source authority.

A paid account that behaves badly remains subject to moderation/restriction.

---

## R12-OF4 — Contextual capability, not global weight / 是情境能力，不是全局权重

Membership must not create one global account level or universal numerical weight.

Any effect of payment is evaluated at:

> **Account × Capability × Context**

Example:

- payment may be relevant to a new account's long-form publishing capacity;
- the same payment is irrelevant to whether that user is a qualified historian, trusted reviewer or governance participant.

---

## R12-OF5 — Only restrictions that payment actually mitigates may be relaxed / 只能放宽付费真正能缓解的限制

Payment does not automatically bypass a restriction merely because the restriction exists.

A restriction based on:

- legal/rights obligations;
- content safety;
- serious moderation history;
- expertise requirements;
- source/provenance requirements;
- human-review requirements;
- physical/technical resource limits unrelated to Sybil cost;

requires its own justification.

“Member” is not a universal bypass flag.

---

## R12-OF6 — Free legitimate path must remain for the confirmed publishing family / 已确认的发布能力必须保留非付费合法路径

For the publishing-capability family already established in Rounds 1–5:

- a legitimate non-paying account can gain broader publishing capability through account history / behavior / other future approved signals;
- paid Membership may allow selected anti-abuse restrictions to be relaxed earlier;
- paid status must not become the only path to ordinary durable/long-form community publishing.

Therefore:

> **paying may reduce waiting/friction; it does not purchase exclusive ownership of the capability.**

---

## R12-OF7 — The only currently source-confirmed acceleration family is publishing capacity / 当前唯一有明确来源支持的加速领域是发布容量

Current source-backed examples include:

- richer/longer community publishing;
- maximum-length relaxation;
- publishing-frequency/rate-limit relaxation where the limit exists for anti-abuse reasons.

Exact thresholds are not decided.

The following are **not** automatically confirmed as Membership accelerations:

- Recognition voting;
- Contributor application/qualification;
- Identity verification;
- governance/moderation authority;
- recommendation reach;
- editorial acceptance;
- Ask/Letter priority;
- service priority;
- private workspace capability;
- storage/export/AI limits.

Each would require independent product/risk justification.

---

## R12-OF8 — Do not invent Premium features to justify Membership / 不为了证明会员有价值而制造高级功能

Round 12 must not start by asking:

> “What can we remove from free users and sell back?”

Nor should it invent member-only knowledge, arbitrary member-only workspace, member-only ordinary community participation, paid authority, or paid distribution advantage simply because a subscription needs a feature list.

If future product features create real marginal cost, scarce resources or optional enhancements, those may be evaluated later on their own merits. They are not required to prove that Membership deserves to exist.

# 3. Critical architectural separation / 一个必须拆开的架构概念

## Membership product != economic-friction mechanism
## 会员产品 ≠ 经济摩擦机制

Rounds 1–5 used VIP/Membership as the available paid state when discussing new-account friction.

Round 12 must not collapse these into one concept.

There are two distinct layers:

### Layer A — Platform risk/capability control

Question:

> Can a payment event or current paid relationship provide useful economic-friction evidence for one specific capability?

This belongs to anti-abuse / capability policy.

### Layer B — Membership as a commercial/product relationship

Question:

> Does Project 3 need a recurring Membership product at all, and if so, what legitimate role does that product have without closing the core platform?

This remains unresolved.

A future Membership may supply an economic-friction signal to Layer A, but **Layer A does not by itself define why Membership exists as a consumer product**.

This distinction prevents:

- “member = trusted”;
- subscription lapse automatically erasing earned standing;
- a commercial plan becoming the platform's universal permission system;
- pressure to create artificial paywalls simply to make a subscription marketable.

# 4. Current capability classification / 当前能力分类

## Class O — Open Core / 开放核心

Normal platform participation and product use remain available without Membership, subject to proportionate abuse/safety/resource controls.

Examples include the ordinary forms of:

- public reading;
- Search / Explore / Following / Topic / Place discovery;
- Save / Bookmark;
- Follow;
- basic private reading utilities already confirmed by the product;
- ordinary Community participation;
- ordinary publishing path;
- Profile / identity use;
- normal Question / Answer and editorial-intake paths where those products exist.

This class must not be converted into “member-only” merely to monetize.

## Class F — Economic-friction acceleration / 经济摩擦加速

Currently source-confirmed:

- selected new/low-trust **publishing capacity** restrictions.

Membership/payment may be relevant here only when the restriction is specifically about zero-cost/Sybil risk.

## Class A — Authority / standing / recognition / governance / 权威、地位、认可、治理

Payment has no automatic positive effect.

## Class C — Genuine marginal-cost / scarce-resource products / 真实边际成本或稀缺资源产品

Not automatically Membership.

If these emerge later, they may be separately priced, rate-limited, usage-priced, bundled, partly subsidized, or free.

That decision belongs to the product owning the cost/resource, not to a rule saying “Membership needs benefits”.

## Class D — Deferred / unresolved / 暂缓与未决

Examples:

- Reading Room existence;
- exact Membership consumer role;
- plan count;
- price/billing cadence;
- cosmetics;
- advanced convenience features;
- service relationship;
- any broader capability-acceleration set beyond the confirmed publishing family.

---

# 5. What Round 12 should ask next / 第十二轮下一步真正要问什么

The next question is no longer:

> “Which premium benefits should Membership include?”

It is:

> **Given that Project 3's core product remains open, and payment's only currently confirmed platform role is a contextual economic-friction signal for selected anti-abuse limits, does Project 3 actually need Membership to be a distinct recurring product relationship? If yes, what additional role—if any—can be justified without creating artificial deprivation?**

This question must be answered before benefit catalogue, tiers, price, billing cadence, Reading Room, member-only surfaces, or service bundles.
