# 2. Round 13 problem blocks / 第十三轮问题块

Round 13 should proceed in this order:

### Block 1 — Monetization role inventory / 商业化角色盘点
Identify which current/future product areas legitimately involve money:

- Spatial Flow commerce;
- services;
- commissions/custom work if revalidated;
- events/experiences if they later exist;
- creator/editorial commercial relationships where legitimate;
- future sponsorship/advertising only if separately authorized;
- other real-cost transactions.

Do not assume all monetization belongs to one model.

### Block 2 — Ink & East ↔ Spatial Flow relationship / 两者关系
Determine whether the relationship is:

- one platform / multiple product domains;
- editorial-to-commerce adjacency;
- shared account/infrastructure but distinct product surfaces;
- cross-reference/referral;
- another bounded relationship.

Do not let ecommerce redefine editorial truth, Recognition or recommendation.

### Block 3 — Legacy service revalidation / 旧服务重新验证
For each legacy service such as Custom Reading / Custom Ebook:

1. what user problem was it meant to solve;
2. does that problem still exist in current Project 3;
3. is a service actually the right solution;
4. should it be human-delivered, productized, automated, hybrid or removed;
5. how does it relate to Ink & East, Spatial Flow or neither;
6. what business/operational cost makes it viable.

Only after passing this gate may old service concepts return.

### Block 4 — Commercial boundary / 商业边界
Define what payment may purchase without corrupting:

- authority;
- source truth;
- Work Recognition;
- editorial selection integrity;
- organic Recommendation;
- Contributor Qualification;
- governance.

### Block 5 — Economics later / 经济模型最后
Only after products/services are validated:

- pricing model;
- one-time vs recurring;
- margin/capacity;
- refund/cancellation;
- delivery expectations;
- service level;
- cross-sell/bundle logic.
