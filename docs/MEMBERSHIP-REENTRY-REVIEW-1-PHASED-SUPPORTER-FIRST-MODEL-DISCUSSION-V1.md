## 1. Reframed question（重新定义问题）

The current evidence suggests that Membership（会员） may not need to launch as a mature Premium（高级付费） bundle.

A different model may fit Ink & East（墨与东方） better:

> **Supporter-first at the beginning, benefit-rich only when real platform maturity creates real recurring value.（前期以支持者关系为核心；只有当平台成熟后真实持续价值出现时，再逐步增加权益。）**

This avoids inventing fake utility simply to justify subscription revenue.

---

## 2. Phase 1 — Supporter Membership（第一阶段——支持型会员）

Potential early role（可能的前期角色）:
- explicitly support Ink & East（墨与东方） and its mission;
- receive recurring supporter/editorial communication（持续支持者 / 编辑沟通）;
- receive transparent updates on what support sustains（了解支持资金维持了什么）;
- maintain a supporter relationship/history（支持关系 / 支持历史）;
- optional low-prestige supporter marker（可选、低身份感的支持者标识） if later desired;
- Economic Commitment Signal（经济承诺信号） may operate where independently justified.

What is **not** required:
- a large benefit catalogue;
- paywalled normal content;
- Reading Room（阅读室）;
- fake premium utilities;
- a large partner discount network;
- heavy event operations.

This can be commercially modest but conceptually honest.

---

## 3. Phase 2 — Community / Experience Value（第二阶段——社区 / 体验价值）

Only when real community/editorial formats exist:
- reading salons（阅读沙龙）;
- roundtables（圆桌）;
- limited-capacity discussions（限额讨论）;
- product/research participation（产品 / 研究参与）;
- selected event priority/courtesy（部分活动优先 / 礼遇）.

This phase should emerge from actual operating formats, not be promised in advance.

---

## 4. Phase 3 — Mature Platform Convenience（第三阶段——成熟平台便利价值）

Only when platform economics create real recurring value:
- ad-free / reduced-ad experience（无广告 / 减少广告）;
- genuinely costly advanced storage/sync/offline/delivery options（真实有成本的高级存储 / 同步 / 离线 / 投递能力）;
- advanced personal utility（高级个人工具） if usage proves demand.

No artificial degradation of the free product is allowed.

---

## 5. Phase 4 — Partner Ecosystem Value（第四阶段——合作生态价值）

Only when Vertical Partner Economy（垂直合作生态） is sufficiently developed:
- selected partner event courtesy（合作活动礼遇）;
- bounded partner discounts（有限合作方优惠）;
- selected early-access opportunities（部分体验提前访问）;
- other non-editorial commercial benefits（其他非编辑型商业权益）.

This phase depends on real partner density and quality.

---

## 6. Product implication（产品含义）

Membership（会员） does not need one immutable value proposition forever.

Its value thesis may evolve while preserving the same hard boundaries:

- Open Core（开放核心） remains;
- payment does not buy authority / Recognition / governance / organic reach（付款不购买权威 / 认可 / 治理 / 自然触达）;
- new benefits require real need/cost/value evidence（新权益必须有真实需求 / 成本 / 价值证据）;
- old benefits may be removed or changed through normal Rule Evolvability（规则可演进） rather than being treated as permanent entitlements without review.

---

## 7. Immediate decision question（当前决策题）

Should Review #1（第一次检查） adopt this product direction:

> **Membership（会员） deserves continued active exploration, with an early Supporter-first（支持者优先） role and maturity-based benefit expansion, rather than being fully deferred until a mature Premium bundle already exists?**

This would still **not** decide:
- final product name;
- price;
- monthly/annual billing;
- public badge;
- exact benefits;
- Reading Room（阅读室）;
- launch timing.
