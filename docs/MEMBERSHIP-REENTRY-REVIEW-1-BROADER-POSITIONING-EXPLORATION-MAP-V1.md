## 1. Purpose（目的）

Membership Value Discovery（会员价值发现） has produced enough useful direction that the subject should remain open.

The goal is not to force a final Membership（会员） definition yet.

The goal is to discover whether one or more durable value theses exist beyond a simple benefit bundle.

---

## 2. Direction A — Supporter / Mission Relationship（支持者 / 使命关系）

Core idea（核心想法）:
Users may support Ink & East（墨与东方） because they value the platform's cultural/public-interest mission, especially in the early stage when commercialization is limited.

Possible relationship value:
- visible continuity of support（支持连续性记录）;
- transparent project/research/recovery updates（项目 / 研究 / 寻回透明更新）;
- clear explanation of what support sustains（支持维持了什么）;
- optional supporter acknowledgment（可选支持者致谢） without authority/prestige implications.

Open question（开放问题）:
Can mission relationship itself remain compelling enough over time without being reduced to “donation only（仅捐赠）”?

---

## 3. Direction B — Access to Process, Not Access to Knowledge（访问过程，而不是购买知识）

Core idea:
Keep finished/public knowledge open, but let supporters/members see more of the **process（过程）** around producing it.

Possible examples:
- research diaries（研究日志）;
- source-recovery progress（资料寻回进展）;
- digitization / restoration progress（数字化 / 修复进展）;
- upcoming editorial/project plans（后续编辑 / 项目计划）;
- behind-the-scenes methodology（幕后方法）;
- work-in-progress project notes（进行中项目记录） where rights/sensitivity allow.

This may create recurring relationship value without paywalling the final public knowledge.

Boundary:
Process access must not expose sensitive sources, private data, rights-restricted material or create editorial/governance privilege.

---

## 4. Direction C — Cultural Stewardship / Project Sustaining（文化守护 / 项目持续支持）

Core idea:
Membership（会员） may connect support to visible public-interest cultural work.

Possible examples:
- support Open Cultural Recovery（开放文化寻回） operations;
- support digitization / documentation（数字化 / 记录）;
- support translations / annotations / preservation work（翻译 / 注释 / 保存工作）;
- support specific editorial/research initiatives（特定编辑 / 研究项目）.

Important:
This is not governance. A supporter may understand what their support helps sustain without buying control over truth, selection, Recognition（作品认可） or institutional decisions.

Open question:
Could this become a distinctive Ink & East（墨与东方） Membership（会员） identity rather than a generic subscription?

---

## 5. Direction D — Participation / Belonging（参与 / 归属）

Core idea:
Membership（会员） may create a closer relationship to the platform/community without creating status hierarchy.

Possible future value:
- salons（沙龙）;
- roundtables（圆桌）;
- project briefings（项目简报）;
- selected feedback sessions（部分反馈会议）;
- beta/research participation（测试 / 研究参与）;
- event invitations（活动邀请）.

This direction requires real recurring formats before launch promises are made.

---

## 6. Direction E — Advanced Personal Knowledge Utility（高级个人知识工具）

Core idea:
Members may later receive legitimately advanced personal knowledge-work capabilities.

Possible examples:
- advanced reading maps（高级阅读地图）;
- advanced collections（高级集合）;
- richer annotation organization（更强批注整理）;
- advanced revisit/history tools（高级回访 / 历史工具）;
- advanced digest / delivery controls（高级摘要 / 投递控制）.

Boundary:
Basic reading/account utility must remain good. Do not degrade free users to manufacture value.

This direction remains evidence-dependent.

---

## 7. Direction F — Mature Platform Convenience（成熟平台便利）

Possible later value:
- Ad-free / Reduced Ads（无广告 / 减少广告）;
- higher-cost storage/sync/offline options（更高成本存储 / 同步 / 离线能力）;
- stronger delivery convenience（更强投递便利）;
- bounded priority support（有限优先支持）.

This direction is maturity-dependent and may be weak early.

---

## 8. Direction G — Partner Ecosystem Value（合作生态价值）

Possible later value:
- selected partner benefits（部分合作方权益）;
- event courtesy（活动礼遇）;
- selected discounts（有限折扣）;
- special cultural/travel/education experiences（文化 / 旅游 / 教育特殊体验）.

This can become meaningful only when Vertical Partner Economy（垂直合作生态） has sufficient density and quality.

---

## 9. Direction H — Paid Capability Products That Should Maybe NOT Be Membership（也许不应归入会员的付费能力产品）

A critical architecture possibility:

> **Not every paid product needs to be called Membership（会员）.**

Some future paid relationships may be cleaner as separate products:
- advanced creator tools（高级创作者工具）;
- Organization tools（组织工具）;
- commercial analytics（商业分析）;
- advertising / Paid Amplification（广告 / 付费加热）;
- transaction / lead tools（交易 / 线索工具）;
- storage/resource upgrades（存储 / 资源升级）.

This prevents Membership（会员） from becoming a catch-all paid bucket.

---

## 10. Current exploration question（当前探索问题）

The review should now test two things in parallel:

1. Which directions above could form a distinctive and durable Membership（会员） identity?
2. Which paid capabilities should remain separate products rather than being stuffed into Membership（会员）?

No final positioning is selected by this document.
