# Round 4（第四轮）— Cultural Contribution Role Amendment V1（文化贡献角色修订 V1）

> **Status（状态）:** TARGETED CROSS-ROUND AMENDMENT（定向跨轮修订）
> **Applies to（适用于）:** Round 4（第四轮）Contributor / Identity（贡献者 / 身份）architecture
> **Source（来源）:** Round 13（第十三轮）Open Cultural Contribution & Recovery（开放文化贡献与寻回）
> **Implementation（实现）:** NOT AUTHORIZED（未授权）

## Amendment 1（修订 1）— Contribution Role（贡献角色） != Contributor Qualification（贡献者资格）

A person or organization may make a valuable cultural contribution without holding Contributor Qualification（贡献者资格）.

Contribution Role（贡献角色）may include:
- Discoverer（发现者）;
- Source Provider（资料提供者）;
- Knowledge Contributor（知识贡献者）;
- Donor（捐赠者）;
- Depositor（寄存者）;
- Translator（翻译者）;
- Transcriber（转录者）;
- Researcher（研究者）;
- Digitization Contributor（数字化贡献者）;
- Restoration / Reconstruction Contributor（修复 / 重建贡献者）;
- Institutional Connector（机构联络促成者）.

Contribution Attribution（贡献署名）records actual contribution. It does not automatically grant:
- Contributor Qualification（贡献者资格）;
- Expertise Scope（专业范围）;
- Work Recognition（作品认可）;
- Reviewer Trust（评审信任）;
- Governance Authority（治理权）;
- source authenticity（资料真实性）.

## Amendment 2（修订 2）— Contributor opportunities are broader than Commission（约稿）

Round 4（第四轮）examples of editorial commissioning（编辑约稿）must be read as one narrow possible collaboration form.

The broader current direction includes:
- source discovery（资料发现）;
- field/local knowledge contribution（田野 / 地方知识贡献）;
- research collaboration（研究合作）;
- translation / transcription（翻译 / 转录）;
- digitization / preservation collaboration（数字化 / 保存合作）;
- cultural recovery projects（文化寻回项目）.

## Amendment 3（修订 3）— Qualification remains evidence-based

Participation in a recovery case（寻回案件）may later become evidence relevant to a scoped Contributor Qualification（贡献者资格）review if the contribution genuinely demonstrates competence.

But:
> **one contribution != automatic qualification（一次贡献不等于自动获得资格）**.

All existing Round 4（第四轮）identity / qualification / authority separation rules remain intact.
