# Round 4（第四轮）— Recovery Participation & Attribution Amendment V2（寻回参与与归因修订 V2）

> **Status（状态）:** USER-CORRECTED TARGETED CROSS-ROUND AMENDMENT（用户纠正后的定向跨轮修订）
> **Applies to（适用于）:** Round 4（第四轮）Contributor / Identity（贡献者 / 身份）architecture
> **Source（来源）:** Round 13（第十三轮）Open Cultural Contribution & Recovery（开放文化贡献与寻回）
> **Implementation（实现）:** NOT AUTHORIZED（未授权）

## 1. Contributor（贡献者）term is reserved（术语保留）

Round 4（第四轮）already gave **Contributor（贡献者）** a specific product meaning:

> a person who passes scoped Contributor Qualification（范围化贡献者资格）through evidence of competence / sustained contribution capability（能力 / 持续创作贡献证据）, and may receive stronger publishing tools / profile semantics / bounded launch support（更强发布工具 / 个人资料语义 / 有界启动分发支持）.

It is **not** a generic word for every person who helps Ink & East（墨与东方）.

Therefore Open Cultural Contribution & Recovery（开放文化贡献与寻回）must not rename every discoverer / holder / source provider / translator / helper as a platform Contributor（贡献者）.

## 2. Recovery Participation Role / Attribution（寻回参与角色 / 归因）is separate

People participating in a Recovery Case（寻回案件）may have roles such as:

- Discoverer（发现者）;
- Source Provider（资料提供者）;
- Knowledge Provider（知识提供者）;
- Material Holder（资料持有人）;
- Donor（捐赠者）;
- Depositor（寄存者）;
- Photographer / Imager（摄影 / 影像记录者）;
- Digitization Participant（数字化参与者）;
- Transcriber（转录者）;
- Collator（校勘者）;
- Translator（翻译者）;
- Research Collaborator（研究协作者）;
- Restorer / Reconstruction Participant（修复 / 重建参与者）;
- Institutional Connector（机构联络促成者）.

These are **Recovery Participation / Attribution（寻回参与 / 归因）roles**, not Contributor（贡献者）identity classes.

## 3. Hard separation（硬分离）

> **Recovery Participation / Attribution（寻回参与 / 归因） != Contributor Qualification（贡献者资格）**

A person may make a historically important recovery contribution without ever becoming a Contributor（贡献者）.

Likewise, a qualified Contributor（贡献者）does not make every submitted source authentic, lawful, important or publication-ready.

A recovery participation record may later become one piece of evidence in a Contributor Qualification（贡献者资格）review **only if** it genuinely demonstrates scoped competence; it never grants qualification automatically.

## 4. Existing Round 4 meaning survives（第四轮原义保留）

Round 4（第四轮）Contributor（贡献者）continues to mean the evidence-based, scoped creator / researcher / practitioner / field-local-knowledge / platform-native qualification system（基于证据、范围化的创作者 / 研究者 / 实践者 / 田野地方知识 / 平台原生资格体系）.

This amendment does not broaden Contributor（贡献者）into a generic participation label.
