## 4. Sealed architectural boundaries / 已封存架构边界

Round 11 now controls, among other things:

- Issue（议题） as a first-class editorial curation object, not the whole content domain;
- Issue Inclusion（议题收录） as a typed relation that preserves underlying object identity;
- multi-class curation without flattening provenance/authority/content type;
- editorial selection separate from Work Recognition（作品认可）, Account Trust（账户信任）, Contributor Qualification（贡献者资格） and governance authority;
- Submission（投稿）, Commission（约稿）, Selection（选入）, Publication（发布） and Inclusion（收录） as distinct semantics;
- version-bound editorial decisions and published Issue snapshots;
- explicit amendment/correction/withdrawal/archive history rather than silent rewriting;
- Latest-valid-first current representation with historical traceability;
- canonical composition/order separate from personalized/supplemental/commercial modules;
- cover/lead/hero prominence separate from authority/Recognition;
- rights/privacy/legal/access changes capable of restricting visible material without falsifying permissible history;
- planning, accountability, readiness and publication operations separated from public content truth;
- AI / automation as assistive/attributable rather than unaccountable editorial authority;
- multi-dimensional measurement without universal editorial-quality score;
- editorial performance, commercial conversion and engagement separate from authority/Recognition;
- rule/policy/workflow/configuration evolvability without retroactive historical rewriting.
