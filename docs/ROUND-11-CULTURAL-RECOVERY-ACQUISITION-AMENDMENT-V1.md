# Round 11（第十一轮）— Cultural Recovery Acquisition Amendment V2（文化寻回内容获取修订 V2）

> **Status（状态）:** USER-CORRECTED TARGETED CROSS-ROUND AMENDMENT（用户纠正后的定向跨轮修订）
> **Applies to（适用于）:** Round 11（第十一轮）Editorial Workflow & Acquisition（编辑工作流与内容获取）
> **Implementation（实现）:** NOT AUTHORIZED（未授权）

## A1 — Commission（约稿）is removed from current architecture（从当前架构移除）

The earlier Commission（约稿）label is now treated as **historical / superseded terminology（历史 / 已取代术语）**.

It must not remain as:
- an umbrella concept（总概念）;
- a narrowed current route（收窄后的当前路径）;
- a first-class Editorial Acquisition object（一级编辑获取对象）.

If Ink & East（墨与东方）directly contacts a known researcher / holder / institution / specialist（研究者 / 持有人 / 机构 / 专家）for help, that action belongs to the broader:
- Public / Directed Request（公开 / 定向求助）;
- Research Collaboration（研究合作）;
- Source Recovery Collaboration（资料寻回合作）;
- Editorial Collaboration（编辑合作）;

depending on the actual purpose.

No separate Commission（约稿）product concept is required.

## A2 — Current acquisition / recovery routes（当前获取 / 寻回路径）

Current architecture may include:

- Discovery（发现）;
- Submission（投稿）;
- Open Call / Public Request（公开征集 / 公开求助）;
- Directed Request（定向求助）;
- Open Cultural Recovery（开放文化寻回）;
- Reader Letter / Question（读者来信 / 提问）;
- Research / Editorial / Institutional Collaboration（研究 / 编辑 / 机构合作）.

## A3 — Recovery Intake（寻回接收） != Article Submission（文章投稿）

Lead（线索）, Knowledge Provision（知识提供）, Digital Material Provision（数字资料提供）, Deposit（寄存）, Donation（捐赠）and Institutional Referral（机构转介）must not be forced into one Article Submission（文章投稿）workflow.

They may create a Recovery Case（寻回案件）whose outputs later become eligible knowledge/editorial objects.

## A4 — Existing Round 11 safeguards that survive（保留的第十一轮边界）

The following remain valid as general editorial/collaboration safeguards:

- invitation / collaboration does not guarantee publication（邀请 / 合作不保证发布）;
- payment does not buy Recognition / Authority（付款不购买作品认可 / 权威）;
- authorship and material roles remain attributable（作者身份与实质角色保持可归因）;
- rights / privacy / publication-readiness checks remain independent（权利 / 隐私 / 可发布状态检查保持独立）;
- Issue Inclusion（议题收录）is a curation relation, not ownership or custody.

## A5 — Recovered objects may later enter Issue（议题）

Recovered source material（寻回资料）, translations（翻译）, research works（研究作品）or recovery dossiers（寻回档案专题）may later become Issue（议题）candidates.

The Recovery Case（寻回案件）itself does not automatically become a published Work（作品）or Issue（议题）content.
