# Round 11（第十一轮）— Cultural Recovery Acquisition Amendment V1（文化寻回内容获取修订 V1）

> **Status（状态）:** TARGETED CROSS-ROUND AMENDMENT（定向跨轮修订）
> **Applies to（适用于）:** Round 11（第十一轮）Editorial Workflow & Acquisition（编辑工作流与内容获取）
> **Implementation（实现）:** NOT AUTHORIZED（未授权）

## A1 — Commission（约稿）is no longer the umbrella acquisition concept

The prior first-class Commission（约稿）framing is narrowed.

Current broader acquisition / recovery routes include:

- Discovery（发现）;
- Submission（投稿）;
- Open Call（公开征集）;
- Open Cultural Contribution & Recovery（开放文化贡献与寻回）;
- Reader Letter / Question（读者来信 / 提问）;
- Collaboration（合作）;
- optional Editorial Invitation / Commissioned Article（可选编辑邀请 / 约稿文章）.

## A2 — Recovery Intake（寻回接收） != Article Submission（文章投稿）

Lead（线索）, Knowledge Contribution（知识贡献）, Digital Material Contribution（数字资料贡献）, Deposit（寄存）, Donation（捐赠）and Institutional Referral（机构转介）must not be forced into one Article Submission（文章投稿）workflow.

They may create a Recovery Case（寻回案件）whose outputs later become eligible knowledge/editorial objects.

## A3 — Narrow Commission survives only where it is actually useful

Editorial Invitation / Commissioned Article（编辑邀请 / 约稿文章）may remain where:
- editors know the missing content;
- editors know whom they want to invite;
- authorship / scope / editorial relationship are explicit.

It does not cover cultural-source recovery.

## A4 — Existing Round 11 safeguards survive

The following remain valid:
- invitation/collaboration != guaranteed publication（邀请 / 合作不等于保证发布）;
- payment != Recognition / Authority（付款不等于作品认可 / 权威）;
- authorship and material roles remain attributable（作者身份与实质贡献角色保持可归因）;
- rights/privacy/publication-readiness checks remain independent（权利 / 隐私 / 可发布状态检查保持独立）;
- Issue Inclusion（议题收录）is a curation relation, not ownership or custody.

## A5 — Recovered objects may enter Issue（议题）through later editorial selection

Recovered source material（寻回资料）, translations（翻译）, research works（研究作品）or recovery dossiers（寻回档案专题）may later become Issue（议题）candidates.

The Recovery Case（寻回案件）itself does not automatically become a published Work（作品）or Issue（议题）content.
