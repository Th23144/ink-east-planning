## 0. Decision / 决定

B-C1…B-C6 are **not ready for user decision**.

They were surfaced before the underlying products—Reading Room（阅读室）, Participation（参与）, Supporter Relationship（支持者关系） and Service Relationship（服务关系）—had been concretely defined.

Therefore:

- B-C1…B-C6 remain **UNRESOLVED / NOT SELECTED（未解决 / 未选择）**;
- assistant recommendations attached to those choices have **zero controlling effect**;
- no package, tier, price or benefit bundle may be inferred from them;
- these choices should be answered **after** concrete product capabilities are defined and evaluated.

