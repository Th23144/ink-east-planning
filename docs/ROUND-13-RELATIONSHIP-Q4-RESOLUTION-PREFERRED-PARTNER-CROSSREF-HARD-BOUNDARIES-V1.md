## 1. Preferred Strategic Partner（优先战略合作伙伴）— CONFIRMED（已确认）

Spatial Flow（空间流）may be treated by Ink & East（墨与东方）as a **Preferred Strategic Partner（优先战略合作伙伴）** for highly relevant opportunities.

Meaning（含义）:
- when a project clearly matches Spatial Flow（空间流）'s capabilities, it is normal to consider Spatial Flow（空间流）early;
- accumulated familiarity and prior cooperation may legitimately reduce discovery/re-onboarding friction;
- this does not guarantee selection;
- this does not create exclusivity（排他）;
- Ink & East（墨与东方）remains free to work with other brands, institutions, workshops, researchers or organizations.

> **Preferred consideration（优先评估） != guaranteed appointment（保证选用） != exclusivity（排他）.**

## 2. Cross-product reference（跨产品引用）— CONFIRMED AS NORMAL CAPABILITY（确认为正常能力）

Knowledge ↔ Object / Project（知识 ↔ 物件 / 项目）relationships may be created when the relationship is real, useful and attributable.

A formal joint project（正式联合项目）is not required merely to create a valid cross-reference.

Requirements（要求）:
- the relationship must actually exist（关系真实存在）;
- the reference must have user/context value（对用户 / 语境有价值）;
- provenance / relationship type（来源溯源 / 关系类型）should remain explicit where material;
- a relationship link does not automatically imply endorsement, authority, quality or commercial preference（关联不自动等于背书、权威、质量或商业偏好）.

This permits long-term semantic connection without merging the two products or databases.

---

## 3. Personal Data（个人数据）— HARD DEFAULT INDEPENDENCE（硬性默认独立）

Personal Data（个人数据） remains independent across Ink & East（墨与东方）and Spatial Flow（空间流） by default.

No silent cross-product reuse of:
- reading history（阅读历史）;
- purchase history（购买历史）;
- personal profile data（个人资料数据）;
- behavioral personalization data（行为个性化数据）.

Any future cross-product personalization using such data requires an explicitly designed, bounded user Opt-in（用户明确选择加入） model and appropriate privacy/purpose controls.

> **No silent cross-product personalization（禁止静默跨产品个性化）.**

This decision does not by itself decide whether future optional Account Linking（可选账户关联） exists.

---

## 4. Hard independent-authority boundaries（硬性独立权威边界）— CONFIRMED（已确认）

The following may **not** transfer because of ownership, partnership, commercial relationship or strategic proximity:

### H1 — Editorial Judgment（编辑判断）
Spatial Flow（空间流） cannot direct or inherit Ink & East（墨与东方） editorial conclusions.

### H2 — Knowledge / Source Authority（知识 / 来源权威）
Strategic/commercial proximity cannot manufacture source reliability, factual authority or canonical standing.

### H3 — Work Recognition（作品认可）
Spatial Flow（空间流） affiliation or relationship cannot create, transfer or guarantee Work Recognition（作品认可）.

### H4 — Governance（治理）
Strategic/commercial relationship cannot transfer Reviewer Trust（评审信任）, governance decision power or comparable standing.

These are **Hard Boundaries（硬边界）**, not merely default-independent settings.

Existing separation of Contributor Qualification（贡献者资格）, Recommendation（推荐）and other trust/capability systems remains in force; no hidden privilege is created by the strategic relationship.

## 5. Public visibility of the standing relationship（持续战略关系的公开可见性）— DEFERRED（暂缓）

The user has **not** decided whether the Standing Strategic Relationship（持续战略关系） itself should be permanently visible at a general public/brand level.

This question is not currently necessary for the relationship architecture and is therefore deferred.

Important distinction（重要区分）:

- material disclosure（重大关系披露） in a specific editorial/project/commercial context remains a separate integrity question and is not automatically deferred.

Public relationship wording should be revisited only when Brand Architecture / Public Relationship / Legal Structure（品牌架构 / 对外关系 / 法律结构） makes it necessary.
