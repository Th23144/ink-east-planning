# 墨与东方 ↔ 空间流当前关系基线 V1

> **Implementation（实现）:** NOT AUTHORIZED（未授权）
> **Merge（合并）:** NOT AUTHORIZED（未授权）

---

## 1. Identity of the two products（两个产品的身份）

- Ink & East（墨与东方）and Spatial Flow（空间流）are independent products/projects（独立产品 / 项目）.
- Project 3（项目三）is a shared development/planning context（共同开发 / 规划上下文）, not one unified user-facing platform（统一面向用户平台）.
- Spatial Flow（空间流）is not Ink & East（墨与东方）'s exclusive physical-world outlet（唯一现实出口）.

---

## 2. Strategic relationship（战略关系）

The two products maintain a:

> **Standing Strategic Relationship（持续战略关系）**

and Spatial Flow（空间流） may be treated as a:

> **Preferred Strategic Partner（优先战略合作伙伴）**

for highly relevant opportunities.

This is non-exclusive（非排他） and does not guarantee selection（不保证选用）.

---

## 3. Platform participation（平台参与）

When Spatial Flow（空间流） participates/publishes inside Ink & East（墨与东方）:

> **Spatial Flow（空间流） speaks as Spatial Flow（空间流）.**

Its content is not Ink & East Editorial（墨与东方编辑部） speech merely because it appears on the platform.

Spatial Flow（空间流） receives no automatic hidden platform privilege（隐藏平台特权） in:
- organic recommendation（自然推荐）;
- Work Recognition（作品认可）;
- Issue Inclusion（议题收录）;
- Contributor Qualification（贡献者资格）;
- source/truth authority（来源 / 真相权威）;
- governance power（治理权）.

---

## 4. Relationship operating principle（关系运行原则）

> **Persistent cooperation, explicit crossover, independent authority.（合作长期存在；跨界行为显式；权威与责任保持独立。）**

Deep cooperation may exist without collapsing editorial identity, attribution, authority or platform neutrality（编辑身份、归因、权威或平台中立性）.

---

## 5. Cross-product connection（跨产品连接）

Knowledge ↔ Object / Project（知识 ↔ 物件 / 项目） relationships may be created as a normal attributable capability when the relationship is real and useful.

A formal joint project is not required for every valid cross-reference.

Cross-reference does not automatically mean endorsement, authority, quality or commercial preference（背书、权威、质量或商业偏好）.

---

## 6. Data and authority boundaries（数据与权威边界）

Personal Data（个人数据） is independent by default.

Any future cross-product personalization using reading history, purchase history, profile or behavioral data requires explicitly designed user Opt-in（用户明确选择加入）.

Hard Boundaries（硬边界） include:
- Editorial Judgment（编辑判断）;
- Knowledge / Source Authority（知识 / 来源权威）;
- Work Recognition（作品认可）;
- Governance（治理）.

Strategic/commercial proximity cannot transfer these.

---

## 7. Cooperation model（合作模型）

The confirmed relationship has three layers:

1. Core Strategic Collaboration（核心战略合作）
   - Knowledge ↔ Practice（知识 ↔ 实践）;
   - research / material / craft projects（研究 / 材料 / 工艺项目）;
   - appropriate Open Cultural Recovery（开放文化寻回） support;
   - Knowledge ↔ Object / Project（知识 ↔ 物件 / 项目） linking.

2. Optional Joint Projects（可选联合项目）
   - publications, exhibitions, events, products, licensing, sourcing/supply, project commerce（发布、展览、活动、商品、授权、采购 / 供应、项目商业化）.

3. Contextual Discovery / Referral（情境化发现 / 引导）
   - relevant crossover may lead users between products;
   - referral is an outcome, not the foundation.

---

## 8. Deferred axes（暂缓轴）

Not currently decided:
- permanent public relationship wording（长期公开关系措辞）;
- final Brand Architecture（品牌架构）;
- legal / ownership / group structure（法律 / 所有权 / 集团结构）;
- optional Account Linking（可选账户关联）;
- technical infrastructure sharing（技术基础设施共享）;
- detailed public disclosure presentation（具体公开披露呈现）.

These are intentionally deferred and do not make the strategic relationship unresolved.
