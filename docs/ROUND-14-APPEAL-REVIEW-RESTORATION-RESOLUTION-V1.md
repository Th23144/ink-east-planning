## 1. Appeal intensity scales with impact（申诉强度随处理影响变化）

Ink & East（墨与东方） should not force every action into one identical appeal process.

### Low-impact actions（低影响处理）
Examples:
- spam hiding（垃圾内容隐藏）;
- minor rate limits（轻微速率限制）;
- low-stakes operational restrictions（低风险操作限制）.

Use lightweight review where appropriate（适当时采用轻量复核）.

### Medium-impact actions（中影响处理）
Examples:
- temporary comment/reply restrictions（临时评论 / 回复限制）;
- content removal（内容删除）;
- temporary capability suspension（特定能力临时停用）.

Provide a formal appeal path（提供正式申诉路径）.

### High-impact actions（高影响处理）
Examples:
- long/permanent account suspension（长期 / 永久账户停用）;
- Contributor Qualification（贡献者资格） removal;
- Organization Verification（组织验证） removal;
- Recognition（认可） withdrawal;
- major archival/source-status removal（重大档案 / 来源状态撤销）.

Require stronger process protections（需要更强程序保障）.

## 2. Original decision-maker cannot be the sole consequential appeal reviewer（原决定者不能成为高影响申诉的唯一复核者）

For consequential appeals:

> **The original decision-maker or automated path should not be the sole authority reviewing its own high-impact decision.（原决定者或自动处理路径，不应成为自己高影响决定的唯一复核者。）**

Possible mechanisms include:
- a different human reviewer（不同人工复核者）;
- human review after automated action（自动处理后转人工复核）;
- an independent second-stage review（独立第二阶段复核）.

Exact staffing and operational design remain deferred（具体人员配置与运营设计暂缓）.

## 3. Defined high-impact cases require Human Review（特定高影响案件必须人工复核）

Human Review（人工复核） should be mandatory for defined high-impact cases, including where appropriate:
- permanent/long-term account suspension（永久 / 长期账户停用）;
- Contributor Qualification（贡献者资格） removal;
- Organization Verification（组织验证） removal;
- Recognition（认可） withdrawal;
- disputed important/source-sensitive cultural records（有争议的重要 / 来源敏感文化记录）;
- cases where automation cannot reliably interpret context（自动机制无法可靠理解上下文的案件）.

Routine obvious spam/abuse does not require the same process intensity（普通明显垃圾 / 滥用不需要同等强度流程）.

## 4. Repeated appeals require new basis after full review（完整复核后重复申诉需要新依据）

After one complete appeal/review cycle, the platform may decline repetitive appeals that provide no material new basis.

A closed case may be reopened when there is:
- Material New Evidence（实质性新证据）;
- changed circumstances（情况变化）;
- material procedural error（实质程序错误）;
- other independently justified grounds（其他独立成立的新依据）.

This prevents infinite relitigation while preserving correction of genuine mistakes（防止无限重复重审，同时保留纠正真实错误的能力）.

## 5. Successful appeals require real restoration（申诉成功必须真正恢复）

If an appeal succeeds, the platform should restore the affected state where applicable:
- content visibility（内容可见性）;
- capability access（能力访问）;
- qualification status（资格状态）;
- Recognition（认可） status;
- profile / organization verification（个人资料 / 组织验证）;
- related metadata（相关元数据）.

The record may preserve:
- original action（原处理）;
- appeal/review result（申诉 / 复核结果）;
- restoration date（恢复日期）;
- reason for reversal（推翻原因）.

The platform should not silently erase its own mistake by default（平台不应默认悄悄抹掉自己的错误）.

## 6. Status does not buy procedural favoritism（身份不能购买程序优待）

Member（会员）, Early Co-builder（早期共建者）, Partner（合作方）, Organization（组织）, Contributor（贡献者） or commercial status do not buy a better appeal outcome.

Process may differ because the affected capability/role differs（流程可以因受影响能力 / 角色不同而不同）, not because one identity is socially superior（不能因为某种身份更“高级”）.
