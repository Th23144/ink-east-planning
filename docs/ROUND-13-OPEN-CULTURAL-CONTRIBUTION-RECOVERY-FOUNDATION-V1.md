## 1. Core idea（核心概念）

Ink & East（墨与东方）does not merely wait for ordinary Article Submission（文章投稿）or commission people to write content（约稿写作）.

Its deeper role may include discovering, receiving, verifying, preserving, digitizing, restoring, researching and reopening scarce or endangered cultural knowledge that may otherwise remain inaccessible or disappear.

The system is bidirectional（双向）:

```text
Platform → World（平台 → 世界）
Public Request / Open Call（公开求助 / 征集）
+
World → Platform（世界 → 平台）
Lead / Knowledge / Material Contribution（线索 / 知识 / 资料贡献）
```

The common objective is:

```text
Discover（发现）
→ Verify（验证）
→ Preserve（保存）
→ Digitize（数字化）
→ Restore / Reconstruct（修复 / 重建）
→ Research / Translate / Annotate（研究 / 翻译 / 注释）
→ Publish Openly（开放发布）
→ Preserve Provenance（保留来源溯源）
```

---

## 2. Physical originals（实体原件）的 special rule（特殊规则）

When a contribution involves a physical original（实体原件）or other potentially culturally significant object（潜在具有文化价值的实体载物）, Ink & East（墨与东方）should **not** position itself as the permanent final repository（永久最终保管机构）by default.

Its role is better understood as:

> **Discovery / Documentation / Research / Digitization / Referral Intermediary（发现 / 记录 / 研究 / 数字化 / 转介中间人）**

Conceptual flow（概念流程）:

```text
Owner / Discoverer（持有人 / 发现者）
→ Ink & East（墨与东方）
→ initial documentation / provenance / rights / condition review
   （初步记录 / 来源 / 权利 / 状态核查）
→ digitization / photography / transcription / research where appropriate
   （必要时数字化 / 摄影 / 转录 / 研究）
→ identify suitable qualified authority / institution
   （寻找合适的合格权威机构）
→ lawful referral / handoff / accession if accepted
   （在合法且机构接受的前提下转介 / 移交 / 入藏）
→ public scholarly / cultural access where permitted
   （在允许范围内向公众开放研究 / 文化访问）
```

Ink & East（墨与东方）therefore acts as a **Bridge / Intermediary（桥梁 / 中间人）**, not a default Museum / Archive（博物馆 / 档案馆）.

---

## 3. Critical legal / product qualification（关键法律 / 产品限定）

The architecture must not assume that every old physical object is legally a Cultural Relic / Cultural Property（文物 / 文化财产）.

Exact legal classification, ownership, custody, export/import, transfer and accession rules（法律分类、所有权、保管、进出口、转移、入藏规则）depend on jurisdiction（法域）, object type（物件类型）, provenance（来源）, rights（权利）and the receiving institution（接收机构）.

Therefore the product rule is:

> **Ink & East（墨与东方）may help identify, document and route potentially significant material, but does not itself make the final legal/cultural-property classification or promise that an official institution must accept the object.**

Where a qualified institution（合格机构）is appropriate and willing to accept the original, long-term custody（长期保管）should normally move to that institution rather than remain with Ink & East（墨与东方）.

## 4. What Ink & East（墨与东方）contributes even when the original is handed onward

The fact that the original object（原件）ultimately goes to a museum, archive, library, university collection or another qualified institution（博物馆 / 档案馆 / 图书馆 / 大学收藏 / 其他合格机构）does **not** erase Ink & East（墨与东方）'s contribution.

Ink & East（墨与东方）may contribute:

- Discovery Coordination（发现协调）;
- Contact Bridging（联系搭桥）;
- Intake Documentation（接收记录）;
- Provenance Research（来源研究）;
- Rights / Permission Documentation（权利 / 授权记录）;
- Condition Documentation（状态记录）;
- Digitization（数字化）;
- Photography / Imaging（摄影 / 影像记录）;
- Transcription（转录）;
- Textual Reconstruction（文本重建）;
- Translation（翻译）;
- Annotation（注释）;
- Research（研究）;
- Contextualization（语境整理）;
- Cataloging / Metadata（编目 / 元数据）;
- Public Presentation（公共展示）;
- Open Publication（开放发布）;
- Referral / Institutional Handoff（转介 / 机构移交）;
- Contributor / Discoverer Attribution（贡献者 / 发现者署名）;
- permanent Provenance Chain（永久来源链）.

Thus the durable contribution may be:

> **the recovery process, evidence chain, digital surrogate, research, interpretation and public-access layer（寻回过程、证据链、数字替代物、研究、解释与公共访问层）**

even when the physical original（实体原件）does not remain with Ink & East（墨与东方）.

## 5. Contribution modes（贡献方式）

The broader system may distinguish:

1. **Lead（线索）** — “I know where this may exist.”
2. **Knowledge Contribution（知识贡献）** — oral/local/specialist knowledge（口述 / 地方 / 专业知识）.
3. **Digital Material Contribution（数字资料贡献）** — scans, photographs, audio, documents（扫描、照片、音频、文件）.
4. **Deposit / Temporary Custody（寄存 / 临时保管）** — material is temporarily provided for documentation/digitization/research（临时提供用于记录 / 数字化 / 研究）.
5. **Donation（捐赠）** — actual transfer of ownership / rights where legally valid（在法律允许时真正转移所有权 / 权利）.
6. **Institutional Referral（机构转介）** — Ink & East（墨与东方）helps connect the holder with an appropriate qualified institution（帮助持有人连接合适的合格机构）.
7. **Research Collaboration（研究合作）** — contributors / institutions participate in verification, reconstruction, translation or scholarship（参与验证、重建、翻译或研究）.

Donation（捐赠）is therefore one subtype（子类型）, not the name for the entire system.

## 6. Historical Commission（约稿）terminology correction（历史“约稿”术语纠正）

The previous Commission（约稿）label is too narrow for the user-confirmed product need.

The current core need is not:

> “we need an article, so we pay/invite someone to write it.”

It is:

> **“we have a knowledge/source gap, or someone in the world has material we do not yet know about; the platform needs a structured way to ask, receive, verify, recover, collaborate and reopen it.”**

Therefore:

- Commission（约稿）must no longer be used as a current product-architecture term（当前产品架构术语）;
- old Round 11（第十一轮）Commission semantics require amendment;
- if Ink & East（墨与东方）directly contacts a known person or institution, describe the actual action as Directed Request（定向求助）, Research Collaboration（研究合作）, Source Recovery Collaboration（资料寻回合作）or Editorial Collaboration（编辑合作）;
- historical Commission（约稿）records remain Decision Provenance（决策溯源）only.

## 7. Relationship to Contributor（贡献者）and Recovery Participants（寻回参与者）

Round 4（第四轮）already defines Contributor（贡献者）as a specific qualified identity based on scoped evidence of competence / sustained contribution capability（基于范围化能力 / 持续创作贡献证据的资格身份）.

Therefore the cultural-recovery system must **not** call every person who helps a Contributor（贡献者）.

Instead, Recovery Participants（寻回参与者）may be attributed by their actual role, such as:

- Discoverer（发现者）;
- Source Provider（资料提供者）;
- Knowledge Provider（知识提供者）;
- Material Holder（资料持有人）;
- Donor（捐赠者）;
- Depositor（寄存者）;
- Translator（翻译者）;
- Research Collaborator（研究协作者）;
- Preservation / Digitization Participant（保存 / 数字化参与者）;
- Institutional Connector（机构联络促成者）.

A Recovery Participation / Attribution record（寻回参与 / 归因记录）does not automatically create Contributor Qualification（贡献者资格）.
