## 1. Core terminology correction（核心术语纠正）

Ink & East（墨与东方）and Spatial Flow（空间流）having overlapping subjects, projects or commercial relevance does **not** itself mean a Conflict of Interest（利益冲突） exists.

The five user-confirmed rules below are better understood as:

- Editorial Independence（编辑独立性）;
- Role Separation（角色分离）;
- Attribution Integrity（归因完整性）;
- Commercial / Editorial Boundary（商业 / 编辑边界）;
- Platform Neutrality（平台中立性）.

A genuine case-specific Conflict of Interest（具体利益冲突） remains possible as a general governance concept under existing platform rules, but the long-term Ink & East ↔ Spatial Flow（墨与东方 ↔ 空间流） relationship is **not itself defined as a conflict relationship**.

---

## 2. Confirmed hard editorial rules（已确认编辑硬规则）

### E1 — Independent editorial/research judgment（独立编辑 / 研究判断）
Ink & East（墨与东方） may independently cover, research, compare, cite or discuss subjects that overlap Spatial Flow（空间流） commercial interests.

Spatial Flow（空间流） does not receive veto power over Ink & East（墨与东方） editorial conclusions.

### E2 — Real criticism and non-selection remain possible（保留真实批评与不选用能力）
Ink & East（墨与东方） may:
- decline to feature Spatial Flow（空间流）;
- criticize a Spatial Flow（空间流） product/practice where editorially justified;
- conclude that another maker / brand / source（制作者 / 品牌 / 来源） is more relevant or better supported for the editorial purpose;
- publish mixed or unfavorable conclusions without requiring Spatial Flow（空间流） approval.

### E3 — Commercial support cannot purchase editorial outcomes（商业支持不能购买编辑结果）
Future sponsorship（赞助）, advertising（广告）, project funding（项目资助）, affiliate/referral arrangements（联盟 / 导流安排） or other commercial relationships cannot purchase:
- favorable editorial conclusions（正面编辑结论）;
- Issue Inclusion（议题收录）;
- Work Recognition（作品认可）;
- source authority（来源权威）;
- organic ranking（自然排序）.

### E4 — Proportionate integrity handling where a real case arises（真实情况出现时按比例处理完整性问题）
If a future concrete case produces a material integrity issue（重大完整性问题）, the platform may use proportionate measures such as:
- disclosure（披露）;
- recusal（回避）;
- independent review（独立复核）;
- role separation（角色分离）.

No automatic assumption is made that every overlap is a conflict.

### E5 — Spatial Flow self-publishing remains self-publishing（空间流自行发布仍是自行发布）
When Spatial Flow（空间流） publishes its own content on Ink & East（墨与东方） platform surfaces:
- Spatial Flow（空间流） is the publisher/speaker;
- the content does not become Ink & East Editorial（墨与东方编辑部） speech;
- platform publication does not itself create editorial endorsement（编辑背书）.

---

## 3. Relationship-level conclusion（关系层结论）

The governing relationship principle is therefore:

> **Deep cooperation may exist without collapsing editorial identity, authority, attribution or platform neutrality.（可以深度合作，但不合并编辑身份、权威、归因与平台中立性。）**

This is an editorial-integrity boundary（编辑完整性边界）, not a statement that Ink & East（墨与东方）and Spatial Flow（空间流） are structurally in conflict.
