## 1. Four governance domains are formally separated（四类治理问题正式分离）

Ink & East（墨与东方） formally separates:

### A — Safety / Conduct Moderation（安全 / 行为审核）
Handles rule-breaking, abuse, harassment, fraud, spam, manipulation and safety-related behavior（处理违规、滥用、骚扰、欺诈、垃圾内容、操纵与安全相关行为）.

### B — Factual / Source Correction（事实 / 来源纠错）
Handles factual, provenance, citation, source and update problems（处理事实、来源溯源、引用、来源与更新问题）.

### C — Quality / Recognition Review（质量 / 认可审查）
Handles whether a work continues to meet independent quality / Recognition（认可） standards（处理作品是否继续符合独立质量 / 认可标准）.

### D — Identity / Qualification / Capability Review（身份 / 资格 / 能力审查）
Handles whether a person or organization still meets requirements for a specific verified identity, qualification or capability（处理个人或组织是否仍满足特定已验证身份、资格或能力要求）.

These domains may interact, but one does not automatically imply the others.

## 2. Correction is normal knowledge maintenance（纠错属于正常知识维护）

Ordinary factual/source correction and revision should be treated as normal knowledge maintenance rather than punishment（普通事实 / 来源纠错与修订属于正常知识维护，而不是处罚）.

Therefore:
> **Correction（纠错） != Punishment（惩罚）.**

Honest mistakes, source updates and revisions do not automatically create misconduct findings（诚实错误、来源更新和修订不自动构成违规认定）.

Malicious fabrication, repeated deception or abuse may separately trigger Conduct Moderation（行为审核） where justified.

## 3. Important public knowledge should preserve explainable revision history（重要公共知识应保留可解释修订历史）

Where appropriate, important public knowledge/editorial records should preserve:
- what changed（改了什么）;
- when it changed（何时修改）;
- why it changed（为什么修改）;
- source/evidence basis（来源 / 证据依据）;
- who/what initiated the change（由谁 / 什么机制发起修改）.

The degree of visible revision history should depend on content class and responsibility（公开修订历史的深度应依据内容类型与责任程度决定）.

## 4. High-impact decisions require stronger due process（高影响决定需要更强程序保障）

The higher the impact of a platform action, the stronger the need for:
- explicit reason（明确理由）;
- evidence/provenance（证据 / 来源记录）;
- appeal path（申诉路径）;
- review independence where appropriate（必要时独立复核）;
- reversibility/restoration where feasible（可行时支持撤销 / 恢复）.

Examples include:
- account suspension（账户停用）;
- Contributor Qualification（贡献者资格） removal;
- Organization Verification（组织验证） removal;
- Recognition（认可） withdrawal;
- major content removal（重大内容删除）.

## 5. Enforcement should be scoped rather than globally punitive（处理应尽量限定范围，而不是全局处罚）

Where possible, action should target the relevant:
- content（内容）;
- behavior（行为）;
- capability（能力）;
- role（角色）;
- context（上下文）.

Avoid:
- one Global Trust Score（全局信任分）;
- one universal strike ladder（统一违规阶梯）;
- paid/Membership（付费 / 会员） status affecting moderation outcomes;
- Early Co-builder（早期共建者） history shielding enforcement;
- popularity shielding correction/moderation.

Account-level enforcement（账户级处理） remains available when severity or cross-domain abuse genuinely justifies it.

## 6. Governing principle（控制性原则）

> **Ink & East（墨与东方） should not judge whether a person is globally “good” or “bad”. It should identify what happened in the relevant context and correct, restrict or review the affected surface proportionately.（Ink & East（墨与东方）不判断一个人整体上“好”或“坏”，而是在具体上下文里判断发生了什么，并按比例纠正、限制或复核受影响的部分。）**
