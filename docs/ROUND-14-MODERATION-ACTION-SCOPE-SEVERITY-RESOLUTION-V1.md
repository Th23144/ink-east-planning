## 1. Four independent scopes（四种处理范围）
Moderation / Enforcement（审核 / 处置） must distinguish:
- **Object-level（对象级）:** 具体帖子、评论、内容、上传对象等；
- **Capability-level（能力级）:** 评论、发布、消息、推荐 / 奖励参与等相关能力；
- **Role / Qualification-level（角色 / 资格级）:** 与问题直接相关且经独立审查的特定角色或资格；
- **Account-level（账户级）:** 必要时的账户级限制、停用。

These are choices of affected scope, **not one mandatory ordered punishment ladder（并非必须逐级走完的统一处罚阶梯）**. Serious imminent harm（严重紧迫危害） may justify immediate account-level action where narrower measures are insufficient.

## 2. Narrowest Effective Action（最窄有效处理）
> **Use the narrowest action sufficient to stop relevant harm and protect the affected surface（采用足以停止相关伤害、保护受影响产品面的最窄处理）.**

This is proportionality, **not automatic leniency（不等于一味从轻）**. A role/qualification review must meet its own role-relevant standards; it does not follow automatically from a post removal.

## 3. Context-based severity（按上下文衡量严重程度）
Consider:
- harm / risk（伤害 / 风险）;
- repetition / pattern（重复 / 模式）;
- scale（规模）;
- intent where reasonably knowable（可合理判断时的意图）;
- reversibility of harm（伤害可逆性）;
- evasion / manipulation（规避 / 操纵）;
- genuine correction/cooperation where relevant（相关情况下的善意纠正 / 配合）.

Do not calibrate severity by Membership（会员）, Early Co-builder（早期共建者）, Partner（合作方）, advertiser（广告主） or popularity（热度） status.

## 4. Duration proportionate to risk（限制期限与风险匹配）
Prefer proportionate time-bounded restrictions（适当时优先有期限的限制） where risk is reversible and correction is feasible.

Long-term/permanent restrictions（长期 / 永久限制） remain available for severe, persistent or systemic abuse（严重、持续或系统性滥用）. Immediate high-severity intervention is possible where necessary; do not require a harmful preliminary step merely to satisfy a nominal ladder.

## 5. Explainable escalation（升级处理须可解释）
When an action expands from one affected scope to another, record:
- relevant prior action or evidence（相关既往处理或证据）;
- new pattern/risk justifying the change（支持扩大处理的新模式 / 风险）;
- why narrower effective measures were insufficient（为什么较窄有效措施不足）;
- the affected scope and duration（受影响范围与期限）;
- review/appeal path under the confirmed appeal framework（根据已确认申诉框架提供复核 / 申诉路径）.

## 6. Separation of correction from punishment（纠错与惩罚分离）
Factual / Source Correction（事实 / 来源纠错） is ordinary knowledge maintenance unless separate evidence supports misconduct. Correcting a citation is **not** itself a moderation sanction; the Scope model applies to enforcement, not to every editorial revision.
