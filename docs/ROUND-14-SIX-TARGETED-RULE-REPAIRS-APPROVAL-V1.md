1. **RP1** — Normal interpretations/disagreements do not require a formal knowledge dispute case, adjudication or warning label; only material source facts, formal Recognition（作品认可） integrity and separate baseline safety use their own appropriate procedures（不同解读不进入默认争议审理）.

3. **RP3** — Formal Candidate / Recognition（候选 / 认可） quality and evidence review does not apply automatically to ordinary posts; normal essays, stories and informal writing are not universally fact-checked. Evaluate only claims truly material to the formal review outcome（普通发表不自动核验，正式评审才按作品关键依据核查）.
4. **RP4** — In appropriate formal evaluation cases, verified material invalidity may justify specific Candidate/Recognition revalidation, with evidence intake, independent/proportionate decision, appeal and accurate history. No automatic punishment for mere allegation（正式认可受关键证据与复核约束，举报不等于事实）.
