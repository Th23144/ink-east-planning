## 1. Final seal audit result

The accepted Round 4 decisions form a coherent architecture. No unresolved contradiction blocks closure.

The final system preserves these separations:

- Entity Type vs qualification/relationship;
- Public Display Identity vs private Identity Verification;
- Credential / Institutional Authority verification vs Contributor Qualification;
- Contributor Qualification vs Expertise Scope;
- Contributor Qualification vs Work Recognition;
- Account Trust / capability vs professional qualification;
- Reviewer Trust / governance capability vs Contributor identity;
- VIP/payment vs professional evidence and governance authority;
- organic Contributor launch support vs sponsored/commercial distribution;
- platform Official speech vs employee/personal Contributor speech.

## 2. New-account / VIP closure

Round 4 does not adopt either a blanket VIP requirement or unrestricted zero-history application access.

The sealed interpretation is:

- ordinary zero-history accounts may face Account Trust / anti-abuse capability gates before expensive application flows;
- VIP can legitimately add economic friction against mass-created accounts and can accelerate/unlock some account capabilities that ordinary users otherwise gain through time/normal use;
- VIP is never Contributor evidence and cannot buy Contributor Qualification, Reviewer Trust, Work Recognition, or governance power;
- strong external evidence, Platform Invitation, or legitimate Institution/Partner onboarding can let a genuine newcomer enter Contributor review without waiting for ordinary account-age accumulation;
- this exception bypasses a time/access gate, not the actual qualification review and not the absence of platform history.

Exact Account Trust mechanics are deferred to Round 5; exact Membership benefit packaging is deferred to the Membership round.

---

## 3. Contributor Qualification closure

One Verified Contributor framework supports multiple legitimate evidence paths rather than prestige tiers.

Qualification is:

- evidence-combination based;
- content/domain Scope aware;
- capable of partial Scope approval/narrowing;
- available to academic, institutional, independent, practitioner, field/local-knowledge, and platform-native applicants;
- deliberately not reduced to degree/title/fame/follower count;
- reviewable with low-friction forms that avoid making every possible supporting field mandatory.

---

## 4. Identity / privacy closure

Public real-name disclosure is not required for Verified Contributor standing.

Pen names may remain public while necessary claims are verified privately.

Verification follows a claim-proportional and privacy-minimizing principle:

> **Verify the claim with the least sensitive sufficient evidence; do not collect high-sensitivity identity material merely because the platform can.**

Government ID is not a universal default requirement. Sensitive-data handling must be explainable before submission, and raw verification material should be minimized/separated from long-lived verification results where feasible.

Detailed security, legal, regional, vendor, and retention design remains deferred.

---

## 5. Integrity enforcement closure

Contributor trust-layer fraud is a major governance matter.

Impersonation, fabricated credentials, false institutional representation, systematic plagiarism/fabrication, deliberate misuse of Contributor status, and comparable serious integrity abuse may trigger immediate temporary suspension pending review and, if confirmed, Scope/Contributor revocation, removal of high-trust capabilities, long-term/permanent re-verification disqualification, and account-level action where warranted.

Confirmed serious trust abuse cannot be satisfied by a trivial mute or lightweight punishment alone.

Consequential action must remain reasoned, evidenced, and appealable. Ordinary quality disagreement, one weak work, good-faith error, or legitimate scholarly disagreement is not automatically a Contributor-integrity violation.

---

## 6. Contributor value / distribution closure

Contributor identity must have meaningful product value without becoming automatic epistemic or ranking authority.

Contributors may receive stronger publishing capabilities, professional tools, richer profiles, editorial/collaboration access, and **bounded real launch support** for new works.

Contributor Launch Support means:

- genuine qualified exposure, not merely eligibility to enter a test pool;
- work-level and bounded support;
- adaptive support rather than one universal fixed impression guarantee;
- evidence-driven continuation/decay after the launch phase;
- stronger cold-start assistance may be appropriate for low-traffic/new Contributors;
- no permanent high-rank entitlement;
- no automatic Recognition;
- no hidden stacking of Contributor/Recognition/Editorial signals into unlimited boost;
- no purchase of organic Contributor support through VIP/payment;
- commercial promotion, if later offered, must be separate and disclosed.

Exact distribution budgets and mixer implementation are deferred to Discovery / Recommendation.

---

## 7. Public semantics closure

The public system should use few clear top-level identity classes, visible Expertise Scope, explainable verification semantics, and minimal prestige inflation.

Canonical presentation principle:

> **Profile explains identity; Content is judged as work.**

A Contributor badge must not imply that every work is correct, Recognized, platform-endorsed, or within every possible domain.

---

## 8. Institution / Partner / Editorial closure

The sealed entity model is:

```text
Entity Type
├─ Person
│  └─ may qualify as Verified Contributor
├─ Institution
│  └─ may have verified representatives / Partner relationship
└─ Platform-owned Entity
   └─ may act as Editorial / Official
```

Institution/Partner status is not a higher Contributor tier. Organization authenticity and representative authority are separately verified. Institutional publishing should preserve individual authorship where applicable. Partner status is not blanket endorsement. Official platform speech is distinct from employee/personal speech. Institution/Partner/Official works remain subject to the same work-level Recognition framework.
