## 1. Purpose（目的）

Q3（第三问） established the three buckets.

Q4（第四问） now asks a more practical question:

> **What can happen normally because the Standing Strategic Relationship（持续战略关系）exists, what requires explicit project-level consent/agreement（明确的逐项目同意 / 协议）, and what should never transfer automatically even under deep cooperation?**

This is still Product Architecture（产品架构）, not contract drafting or implementation.

---

## 2. Bucket A — Default standing behaviors（默认持续行为）

Candidate behaviors that may occur without negotiating a new relationship from zero every time:

### A1 — Strategic visibility（战略可见性）
Each side may maintain a reasonable understanding of the other's public direction, capabilities and relevant plans.

This does not imply access to confidential information（保密信息）.

### A2 — Collaboration discovery channel（合作发现通道）
Relevant teams/actors may have a standing way to identify potential joint opportunities.

This is not a right to demand participation.

### A3 — Mutual suitability check（相互适配优先评估）
When a project clearly touches the other side's strengths, it is normal to ask first whether the other side is suitable.

This is not exclusivity（排他） and does not require selecting the other side.

### A4 — Cooperation history continuity（合作历史连续性）
Prior collaborations, roles and outcomes may remain attributable and discoverable where appropriate.

### A5 — Explicit cross-reference capability（显式跨产品引用能力）
A valid Ink & East（墨与东方）knowledge object may reference a Spatial Flow（空间流）object/project, and vice versa, where the relationship is real and useful.

The link must not imply endorsement beyond the stated relationship.

### A6 — Standing conflict/disclosure awareness（持续利益冲突 / 披露意识）
Because the relationship is ongoing, Ink & East（墨与东方）should be able to recognize when Spatial Flow（空间流）has a material commercial or project interest relevant to editorial/research treatment.

## 3. Bucket B — Explicit project-level agreement required（必须逐项目明确）

The following should require explicit agreement whenever they matter:

### B1 — Joint project scope（联合项目范围）
Who does what, what the project is, and what counts as completion.

### B2 — Rights / ownership（权利 / 所有权）
Copyright（著作权）, license（授权）, physical ownership（实体所有权）, dataset/document ownership（数据集 / 文档所有权） and derivative rights（衍生权利）.

### B3 — Confidential / non-public access（保密 / 非公开访问）
Access to unpublished research, private material, supplier information, user data, internal analytics or other restricted information.

### B4 — Commercial terms（商业条款）
Pricing（定价）, procurement（采购）, supply（供应）, revenue sharing（收益分成）, commission fee / service fee（佣金 / 服务费）, exclusivity（独家 / 排他） and sponsorship（赞助）.

### B5 — Public representation（对外表达）
Co-branding（联合品牌）, logo use（标志使用）, naming, press copy, public attribution and claims about the relationship.

### B6 — Distribution / promotion（分发 / 推广）
Any preferential commercial placement, paid promotion or campaign coordination must be explicit and appropriately labeled.

### B7 — Publication / release（发布 / 公开）
Who may publish, when, in what form, and under which identity/attribution.

---

## 4. Bucket C — Non-transferable by relationship alone（关系本身不可转移）

The following should remain independently governed unless a separately designed system explicitly allows bounded interoperability（有限互操作）:

### C1 — Editorial authority（编辑权威）
Spatial Flow（空间流）cannot direct Ink & East（墨与东方）editorial conclusions merely because of the strategic relationship.

### C2 — Knowledge / source authority（知识 / 来源权威）
Commercial or strategic proximity does not make Spatial Flow（空间流）a source authority.

### C3 — Recognition / governance standing（作品认可 / 治理地位）
No transfer of Work Recognition（作品认可）, Reviewer Trust（评审信任）, Contributor Qualification（贡献者资格） or Governance Authority（治理权）.

### C4 — Organic recommendation privilege（自然推荐特权）
No hidden ranking boost merely from the relationship.

### C5 — Account / identity（账户 / 身份）
No automatic account merger or identity equivalence.

### C6 — Personal data（个人数据）
No silent cross-product reuse of reading history, purchase history or personal profile data.

### C7 — Transaction responsibility（交易责任）
Payment, order, refund, fulfillment and support responsibility remain attributable to the actual product/service handling the transaction.

### C8 — Brand voice（品牌声音）
One product cannot speak as the other without explicit Acting Context / authorization（行动身份上下文 / 授权）.

### C9 — Legal responsibility（法律责任）
Strategic relationship does not automatically merge legal liability, contractual responsibility or regulatory obligations.

## 5. Key questions for user review（需要用户确认的关键问题）

The next decision is not binary; the user should challenge the boundary design.

Key questions:

1. Should A3（相互适配优先评估） be stronger than “ask first” — for example a real Preferred Partner（优先合作伙伴） relationship — or should it remain non-preferential?
2. Should A5（显式跨产品引用） be a normal product capability whenever a real relationship exists, or only for formally approved projects?
3. Should C6（个人数据独立） be a hard default with later explicit user opt-in（用户明确选择加入） for any cross-product personalization?
4. Are there any items in Bucket C（不可转移边界） that should be absolute hard boundaries rather than merely default-independent?
5. Should the standing relationship itself be publicly disclosed somewhere, or only when materially relevant to a specific page/project/editorial context?
