## 1. User-confirmed tension（用户确认的核心张力）

The user confirms the key concern:

> Cultural Stewardship（文化守护） is promising, but if framed poorly it may feel too charitable / donation-like（过于公益 / 捐赠化） and lose the feel of a modern platform relationship（现代平台关系）.

The goal is therefore **not** to turn Ink & East（墨与东方） into a charity organization（慈善组织）, nor to reduce Membership（会员） to buying perks.

---

## 2. Positioning target（定位目标）

A stronger target is:

> **Support public-interest cultural work（支持公共价值文化工作）**
> + **stay visibly connected to how that work happens（持续看见工作如何发生）**
> + **belong more deeply to the platform as it grows（随着平台成长形成更深的平台关系）**

This preserves public value while remaining a modern digital-platform relationship.

---

## 3. What should make it feel modern（什么让它具有现代平台感）

### A. Ongoing Visibility（持续可见性）
Supporters should not receive only a receipt or thank-you message.

They may see:
- project progress（项目进展）;
- research / recovery milestones（研究 / 寻回里程碑）;
- what is being worked on now（当前正在做什么）;
- what support helped sustain（支持推动了什么）.

This is a live relationship, not a donation certificate（捐赠证明）.

### B. Process Access（过程访问）
Final knowledge remains public where appropriate, but supporters may receive richer access to:
- research process（研究过程）;
- project diaries（项目日志）;
- work-in-progress context（进行中工作背景）;
- restoration / digitization progress（修复 / 数字化进度）;
- editorial methodology（编辑方法）.

The value is proximity to process, not exclusive ownership of knowledge.

### C. Participation Opportunities（参与机会）
As real formats emerge, supporters may gain optional opportunities to participate in:
- feedback sessions（反馈活动）;
- salons / roundtables（沙龙 / 圆桌）;
- project briefings（项目简报）;
- selected cultural/research activities（部分文化 / 研究活动）.

Participation remains non-governance and non-authority.

### D. Evolving Platform Value（随平台成长的价值）
Later benefits may grow naturally from actual platform maturity:
- Ad-free / Reduced Ads（无广告 / 减少广告）;
- advanced personal utility（高级个人工具）;
- partner ecosystem benefits（合作生态权益）;
- event / experience courtesy（活动 / 体验礼遇）.

These are additions, not the spiritual core（精神核心）.

---

## 4. What to avoid（应避免什么）

Avoid making Membership（会员） feel like:
- charity badge（公益勋章）;
- moral superiority status（道德优越身份）;
- donor hierarchy（捐赠等级）;
- paid cultural authority（付费文化权威）;
- locked knowledge club（知识封闭俱乐部）;
- generic Premium（高级订阅） bundle with unrelated perks.

---

## 5. Working identity（工作中的定位表达）

A possible working identity is:

> **Supporter Membership（支持型会员） with a Cultural Stewardship（文化守护） core and a modern platform relationship（现代平台关系）.**

This is **not final naming（非最终命名）**.

The important distinction is:

- Supporter（支持者） explains why the relationship begins;
- Stewardship（守护） explains what kind of public-value work is being sustained;
- Platform Relationship（平台关系） explains why it is more than a donation.
