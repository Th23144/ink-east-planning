## 1. Why this distinction is now necessary（为什么现在必须区分）

Membership Value Discovery（会员价值发现） led to Early Co-builder Relationship（早期共建者关系）, but the two concepts are not necessarily the same product relationship.

If they are collapsed, several problems appear:
- later users could never meaningfully join Membership（会员） because they are not “early”;
- an early co-builder who never pays might be incorrectly excluded from historical recognition;
- stopping payment might appear to erase historical co-building;
- paying Membership（会员） might incorrectly become a way to buy founding status.

Therefore the next question is:

> **How should Membership / Supporter Relationship（会员 / 支持者关系） and Early Co-builder Relationship（早期共建者关系） overlap without becoming the same identity?**

---

## 2. Proposed separation（建议分离）

### A — Early Co-builder Relationship（早期共建者关系）
A historical relationship.

It answers:
> **What did this person materially do during the formative stage of the platform?（这个人在平台形成阶段实际做过什么？）**

Properties（属性）:
- historical;
- evidence/type based;
- not purchasable merely through a plan;
- does not disappear if Membership（会员） later ends;
- naturally becomes closed to new “early” entrants after the formative stage ends, while ordinary contribution remains open.

### B — Membership / Supporter Relationship（会员 / 支持者关系）
An ongoing/current relationship.

It answers:
> **What support/relationship does this user currently maintain with Ink & East（墨与东方）?（这个用户当前与平台维持什么支持 / 会员关系？）**

Properties:
- can exist for early or later users;
- may have evolving benefits;
- does not create Early Co-builder（早期共建者） history by itself;
- may coexist with one-time or recurring payment mechanics without turning cadence into prestige.

---

## 3. Overlap model（重叠模型）

A person may be:
- Early Co-builder + Member/Supporter（早期共建者 + 会员 / 支持者）;
- Early Co-builder but not current Member（早期共建者但当前不是会员）;
- current Member but not Early Co-builder（当前会员但不是早期共建者）;
- neither（两者都不是）.

These combinations should all be valid.

---

## 4. Historical status must survive payment changes（历史关系不随付款变化消失）

If a user genuinely helped build the early platform, that history should not disappear because:
- they stop paying;
- a Membership plan changes;
- the platform later changes pricing;
- they never paid at all.

Likewise:

> **Paying Membership（会员） must not retroactively purchase Early Co-builder（早期共建者） history.**

---

## 5. Shared vs separate benefits（可重叠权益与独立权益）

Some experiences may be available to both relationships:
- project/platform updates（项目 / 平台更新）;
- feedback/research invitations（反馈 / 研究邀请）;
- event access（活动机会）.

But the reason may differ:
- Membership（会员） because it is a current supporter benefit;
- Early Co-builder（早期共建者） because of historical relationship/relevance.

The same surface does not mean the two identities are equivalent.

---

## 6. “Spiritual Shareholder（精神股东）” should not become a Membership tier

Because “Spiritual Shareholder（精神股东）” refers to early co-building/belonging, it should not simply be sold as:
- a higher Membership tier（更高会员档位）;
- a price package（价格套餐）;
- a badge unlocked by payment amount（按付款金额解锁的徽章）.

Public wording remains open, but underlying architecture should keep it on the historical relationship axis.

---

## 7. Current decision questions（当前决策题）

1. Should Early Co-builder Relationship（早期共建者关系） and Membership / Supporter Relationship（会员 / 支持者关系） be formally separate axes?
2. Should genuine Early Co-builder history survive regardless of future Membership/payment status?
3. Should paying Membership alone never be sufficient to buy Early Co-builder status?
4. Should later users be able to become Members/Supporters without being treated as “lesser” merely because they were not early?
5. Should “Spiritual Shareholder（精神股东）” remain outside Membership tier/pricing architecture?
