## 1. Distinguish procedure status from substantive outcome（程序状态与实质结论分离）
Received / Under Review / Closed / Reopened（已受理 / 审查中 / 已结案 / 重新开启） describes the **case process（案件程序）**, not whether a claim is true（主张真假）.

A final outcome should summarize evidence-supported findings and appropriate effects on the affected work.

## 2. Five candidate substantive results（五种候选实质结果）

### A. Confirmed Error / Corrected（确认错误 / 已纠正）
A verifiable date, citation, edition, transcription, attribution or other objectively checkable fact was wrong（可核实的日期、引用、版本、转录、归属等事实确有错误）.

Effects（处理效果）:
- Correct the platform-controlled record where responsible（更正平台负责的记录）.
- For independent author work, seek author revision or attach a properly scoped factual correction note; do not secretly overwrite the author's prose（独立作者作品由作者修订或平台附加适度事实纠错说明，不能暗改正文）.
- Material changes carry an explanation and provenance（重要改动留说明和依据）.

### B. Supported Dispute（确认存在有依据的争议）
More than one source-backed interpretation remains defensible（多种解释各有可成立的来源依据）.

Effects（处理效果）:
- Preserve the author's work and argument（保留原作品与论证）.
- If the content actually carries knowledge responsibility and the dispute is material, optionally add a neutral sourced dispute note（仅在具有实质知识责任且争议重要时附加中立、有依据的争议说明）.
- Do not assert that one camp won without sufficient evidence（证据不足时不得宣判一方胜出）.
- No automatic punishment or withdrawal of Recognition（认可）.

### C. Insufficient Evidence / Undetermined（证据不足 / 暂无法判定）
The current record cannot substantiate a correction or conclusively evaluate a source claim（现有证据不能充分支持纠正或最终判断来源主张）.

Effects（处理效果）:
- Do not manufacture certainty（不得假装确定）.
- Retain relevant content; use limited uncertainty notes only when downstream reliance or archival responsibility makes that meaningful（保留内容；仅在他人依赖 / 档案责任使其必要时标注不确定性）.
- Permit reopening with substantial new evidence（出现实质新证据可重开）.

### D. Unsupported Request / Not Sustained（纠错请求不成立）
Provided evidence does not substantiate the alleged error, or the claim is unsupported opinion（提交证据不足以证明错误或仅为缺乏根据的个人意见）.

Effects（处理效果）:
- Leave work unchanged（作品维持原状）.
- Do not attach a stigmatizing public “disputed（有争议）” badge simply because someone complained（不能因为被人举报就附上污名化公开争议标签）.
- Provide a proportionate private explanation to submitter where practical（可行时向提出者说明不采纳理由）.
- Distinguish lack of evidence from proof the original claim is definitively true（不能把质疑证据不足等同于原观点绝对正确）.

### E. Independently Established Misconduct（经独立程序确认违规）
There is independently sufficient evidence of deliberate fabrication, forgery, deceptive manipulation or other prohibited behavior（有独立充分证据证明故意伪造、欺骗操纵或其他违规）.

Effects（处理效果）:
- Handle via Safety / Conduct Moderation（安全 / 行为审核） separately with proportionate scope, due process and appeal（按范围适度处理，提供程序保障和申诉）.
- The knowledge correction case and misconduct finding may coexist but are separate determinations（知识纠错结论和违规认定可并存，但不是同一判断）.
- Contributor Qualification（贡献者资格） and Recognition（作品认可） undergo their own relevant reviews rather than automatic cascading penalties（资格和认可分别审查，不能自动连坐）.

## 3. Avoid treating outcomes as one mutually exclusive score（不要把结果做成互斥万能分）
A case could confirm an objective citation error **and** preserve a reasonable interpretive dispute（某案件可同时确认引用错误并保留合理解释争议）.

Misconduct is a **separate procedural branch（独立程序分支）**, not an ordinary “knowledge result” or a permanent public badge.

## 4. Public/private display principle（公开 / 私有展示原则）
Only publish what is needed to explain a material correction or meaningful source dispute（仅公开解释重大纠错 / 实质来源争议所必需的信息）.
Keep sensitive evidence and unsupported accusations private（敏感证据与未证实指控不公开）.
Do not force badges/academic adjudication onto ordinary Community / User（社区 / 用户） posts.
