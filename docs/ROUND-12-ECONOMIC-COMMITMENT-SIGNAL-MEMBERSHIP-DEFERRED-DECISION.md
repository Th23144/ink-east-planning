## 1. Confirmed decision / 已确认决定

Project 3 separates the anti-abuse economic-friction mechanism from the consumer Membership product.

### D12-1 — Economic Commitment Signal is confirmed
**Economic Commitment Signal（经济承诺信号）** is retained as an independent architecture concept.

Its purpose is narrow:

> where a specific capability restriction exists to resist disposable / zero-cost / Sybil abuse, a real economic commitment may be relevant evidence that the account is no longer costless to create or replace, and may justify earlier relaxation of that specific restriction.

This continues the Rounds 1–5 rule without treating payment as a universal account weight.

### D12-2 — Membership may be one source, but is not the mechanism itself
A future Membership/payment relationship may provide Economic Commitment Signal evidence.

But:

> **Membership product != Economic Commitment Signal.**

Membership is not the only possible signal source, and the anti-abuse architecture must not depend on a particular subscription product existing.

### D12-3 — Non-paying legitimate path remains
For the already-confirmed publishing-capability family, legitimate non-paying users must retain a path to broader capability through normal account history / behavior / other future approved evidence.

Payment may reduce selected waiting/friction; it does not purchase exclusive ownership of ordinary publishing capability.

### D12-4 — Payment does not become trust or authority
Economic commitment must not directly create:

- Behavioral Trust;
- Contributor Qualification;
- factual/identity verification;
- Expertise;
- Work Recognition;
- Reviewer Trust;
- Formal Nomination authority;
- Governance Authority;
- organic Recommendation advantage;
- epistemic/source authority.

### D12-5 — Standalone recurring Membership product is DEFERRED
Project 3 does **not** currently require a distinct recurring consumer Membership product.

This is not a cancellation.

Status:

> **STANDALONE RECURRING MEMBERSHIP — DEFERRED / PRODUCT EXISTENCE NOT YET JUSTIFIED.**

The product may be re-opened later if genuine recurring user value, recurring operating cost, resource capacity, convenience or another legitimate commercial role emerges naturally from the mature product.

### D12-6 — Do not invent VIP benefits to justify a subscription
Round 12 must not manufacture:

- paid-only ordinary platform functionality;
- member-only normal published knowledge;
- arbitrary workspace gating;
- paid authority/status;
- paid organic distribution;
- artificial free-user pain;

merely to populate a Membership feature list.

### D12-7 — Reading Room remains unresolved
Reading Room（阅读室） remains **UNRESOLVED / NOT REQUIRED**.

No Membership decision in this round revalidates it.

### D12-8 — Future evidence may come from Round 13 and later product development
Services / Monetization / Spatial Flow relationship work may later provide evidence relevant to Membership.

Such evidence may justify reopening Membership, but it does not automatically do so.

## 2. Practical interpretation / 实际解释

Current architecture is:

```text
Account × Capability × Context
        +
risk / abuse evidence
        +
possible Economic Commitment Signal
        ↓
contextual capability decision
```

Not:

```text
Free account < Member < VIP < Trusted user
```

And not:

```text
pay → trust → authority → recommendation → governance
```

---

## 3. What this resolves / 本决定解决什么

Resolved:

- the Rounds 1–5 anti-abuse/economic-cost rule remains valid;
- “account weight” is not revived as a universal scalar;
- Membership is not required merely to carry the economic-friction rule;
- the platform does not need to invent paid benefits now;
- standalone recurring Membership remains available as a future product option without becoming current product truth.

Still deferred:

- exact Economic Commitment Signal sources beyond already-discussed payment/Membership;
- exact thresholds and expiry/calibration;
- exact capability set beyond the source-confirmed publishing family;
- standalone Membership product;
- tiers, pricing, billing cadence and benefits;
- Reading Room.

Round 13 may now proceed to:

**Services / Monetization / Spatial Flow Relationship（服务 / 商业化 / Spatial Flow 关系）**
