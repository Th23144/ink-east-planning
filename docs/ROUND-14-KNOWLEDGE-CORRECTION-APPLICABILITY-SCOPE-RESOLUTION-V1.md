## 1. Scope is not universal（适用范围不是全站一刀切）
The formal Knowledge Correction / Source Dispute / Independent Review（知识纠错 / 来源争议 / 独立复核） framework should **not** be imposed on all user-generated works（不应强加于所有用户作品）.

Basic Community Moderation（基础社区审核） applies across all content, but specialized knowledge correction is principally for content that undertakes an identifiable knowledge, sourcing, archival, editorial, or public-reliance responsibility（专门知识纠错主要适用于承担明确知识、来源、档案、编辑或公共依赖责任的内容）.

## 2. Three proportional processing bands（三层按责任和影响划分的处理强度）

### A. Ordinary Community / User Work（普通社区 / 用户作品）
- Author can normally edit own work（作者通常自行修改）.
- Users can comment, disagree, or report rule violations（用户可讨论、反对或举报违规）.
- Ordinary interpretive differences and informal mistakes **do not** enter a mandatory scholarly review, source-adjudication or independent-review process（普通解释分歧、非重大错误不进入强制学术 / 来源 / 独立复核流程）.
- The platform may act on independently substantiated fraud, harassment, harmful deception or other rule breaches（经独立证实的欺诈、骚扰、有害欺骗或违规仍可处理）.
- Content posing substantial real-world harm or making consequential factual claims can require proportionate escalation **because of actual risk**, not because all ordinary posts are academically certified（严重风险或影响重大的事实主张可按风险升级，而非所有普通帖都需要学术认证）.

### B. Substantive Research / Knowledge-oriented Work（实质研究 / 知识型作品）
- Where specific factual/source claims are presented for reliance, supported corrections may warrant evidence-oriented handling（承载可供依赖的事实 / 来源主张时，有依据的纠错可进入证据导向处理）.
- Depending on impact, source disputes and important revisions may warrant explanatory notes or independent review（依据影响决定是否需要争议说明或独立复核）.
- Interpretive disagreement is not itself misconduct or proof the author is wrong（解释分歧不自动等于违规或作者错误）.

### C. Canonical / Source-backed, Accountable Editorial, and Applicable Recognized Work（规范 / 来源型、承担责任的编辑内容，以及适用的认可作品）
- Maintain stronger source / edition / attribution integrity where the content actually assumes that obligation（实际承担相关责任时保留更严格的来源、版本、归属完整性）.
- Significant corrections and disputes should preserve an explainable record and appropriate decision responsibility（重要纠错和争议保留可解释记录及明确责任）.
- **Recognized Works（认可作品） are not automatically infallible or formally authoritative（认可作品不等于绝对正确或天然权威）.** Recognition status by itself does **not** imply every assertion must be subject to canonical-source review（获得认可不代表每句话都需要接受规范来源型审查）.

These bands guide proportional handling. They are not a hierarchy of user worth or an account rank（不是用户价值等级或账户等级）.

## 3. Governing test（控制性判断标准）
Determine rigor from:
1. the content's actual knowledge/source responsibility（内容实际承担的知识 / 来源责任）;
2. the nature of the claim and the strength of its evidence（主张性质及证据）;
3. potential downstream reliance/impact（他人依赖程度与影响）;
4. actual harm/safety implications where present（存在时的实际伤害 / 安全风险）.

**Do not decide by author prestige, Membership（会员）, Contributor Qualification（贡献者资格）, popularity or Recognition（认可） alone.**

## 4. Distinguish safety baseline from formal epistemic review（区分安全底线与正式知识审查）
Ordinary Community / User（社区 / 用户） works still follow anti-abuse / safety rules（反滥用 / 安全规则）.

A platform moderation response to fraud/harassment does **not** require building a formal historical-source dispute panel for the same post（处理欺诈 / 骚扰不意味着要给该帖子建立古籍来源争议审查组）.

## 5. Effect on ongoing work（对后续讨论的约束）
Future Correction Ownership / Contested Decisions（纠错责任归属 / 争议决定） discussion must apply the rigorous author-response, domain review, dispute annotation and independent-review options **only when the content responsibility and real impact justify them（仅在内容责任与实际影响足以支持时适用）**.

For ordinary works, preserve simple author control, normal reporting and baseline rules; **do not create a universal corrective power to rewrite ordinary users' subjective opinions（不建立全站统一纠错权去改写普通用户的主观看法）**.

## 6. Open matters（待后续讨论）
Exact responsibility thresholds, evidence standards, appeals, UI（用户界面） and implementation remain deferred; the currently active issue is who may propose, respond, revise, decide and annotate **within the properly scoped cases（在正确界定的适用范围内）**.
