## 1. Naming boundary（命名边界）

The word **Contributor（贡献者）** is reserved for the existing Round 4（第四轮）qualification identity system.

The cultural-recovery system must not reuse “Contribution / 贡献” as its umbrella vocabulary, because that creates direct semantic collision with Contributor（贡献者）.

Current umbrella name（当前总名）:

> **Open Cultural Recovery（开放文化寻回）**

This is an architecture working name（架构工作名）, not final public naming（最终前台命名）.

---

## 2. Core model（核心模型）

Open Cultural Recovery（开放文化寻回）supports two directions:

```text
Ink & East → World（墨与东方 → 世界）
Public Request / Source Call / Directed Request
（公开求助 / 资料征集 / 定向求助）

World → Ink & East（世界 → 墨与东方）
Lead / Knowledge / Material / Collaboration
（线索 / 知识 / 资料 / 合作）
```

The common process may include:

```text
Discover（发现）
→ Verify（验证）
→ Document（记录）
→ Preserve / Digitize（保存 / 数字化）
→ Restore / Reconstruct（修复 / 重建）
→ Research / Translate / Annotate（研究 / 翻译 / 注释）
→ Refer / Handoff where appropriate（必要时转介 / 移交）
→ Open Public Access where permitted（在允许范围内向公众开放）
→ Preserve Provenance（保存来源溯源）
```

## 3. Recovery participants and roles（寻回参与者与角色）

A person who helps a Recovery Case（寻回案件）is not automatically a Contributor（贡献者）.

Use actual role names instead:

- Discoverer（发现者）;
- Source Provider（资料提供者）;
- Knowledge Provider（知识提供者）;
- Material Holder（资料持有人）;
- Donor（捐赠者）;
- Depositor（寄存者）;
- Photographer / Imager（摄影 / 影像记录者）;
- Digitization Participant（数字化参与者）;
- Transcriber（转录者）;
- Collator（校勘者）;
- Translator（翻译者）;
- Research Collaborator（研究协作者）;
- Restorer / Reconstruction Participant（修复 / 重建参与者）;
- Institutional Connector（机构联络促成者）;
- Custodian / Holding Institution（保管者 / 收藏机构）.

Umbrella vocabulary（总称）:

- Recovery Participant（寻回参与者）;
- Recovery Role（寻回角色）;
- Recovery Attribution（寻回归因）.

Do not use generic “Contributor（贡献者）”, “Contribution Role（贡献角色）” or “Contribution Attribution（贡献署名）” for these roles.

---

## 4. Contributor（贡献者）remains separate

Round 4（第四轮）Contributor（贡献者）continues to mean:

> an evidence-based, scope-bounded qualification identity（基于证据、限定专业范围的资格身份） for people with sufficient demonstrated competence / sustained creation-research-practice capability（已证明的能力 / 持续创作、研究或实践能力）.

Therefore:

> **Recovery Role（寻回角色） != Contributor Qualification（贡献者资格）**

A Recovery Participant（寻回参与者）may later apply for Contributor Qualification（贡献者资格） through the normal qualification process if relevant evidence exists, but the recovery role itself never grants it automatically.

---

## 5. Physical originals（实体原件）

When a Recovery Case（寻回案件）involves a physical original（实体原件）or potentially culturally significant object（潜在重要文化实体）:

Ink & East（墨与东方）may act as:

> Discovery / Documentation / Research / Digitization / Referral Intermediary（发现 / 记录 / 研究 / 数字化 / 转介中间人）

It is not assumed to be the final permanent repository（永久最终保管机构）.

Where lawful, holder-authorized, appropriate and institution-accepted（合法、持有人授权、适当且机构接受）:

> long-term physical custody（长期实体保管）should normally move to a suitable Qualified Institution（合格机构）with preservation / research / public-service capacity（保存 / 研究 / 公共服务能力）.

Object custody may change; Recovery Provenance（寻回来源链）and Recovery Attribution（寻回归因）should remain reconstructable where lawful and appropriate.

---

## 6. Intake modes（接收方式）

The system may distinguish:

1. Lead（线索）;
2. Knowledge Provision（知识提供）;
3. Digital Material Provision（数字资料提供）;
4. Deposit / Temporary Custody（寄存 / 临时保管）;
5. Donation（捐赠）;
6. Institutional Referral（机构转介）;
7. Research Collaboration（研究合作）.

Donation（捐赠）is one specific relationship mode, not the umbrella system name.

---

## 7. Provenance and rights boundary（来源与权利边界）

The following remain separate where relevant:

- Owner（所有者）;
- Holder（持有人）;
- Custodian（保管者）;
- Rights Holder（权利人）;
- Donor（捐赠者）;
- Depositor（寄存者）;
- Digital Provider（数字提供方）;
- Holding Institution（收藏 / 保管机构）;
- Receiving Institution（接收机构）.

Receiving material does not equal authenticating it.
Donation does not equal public-release permission.
Deposit does not equal ownership transfer.
Institutional referral does not equal institutional acceptance.

---

## 8. Relationship to Round 7（第七轮）

Open Cultural Recovery（开放文化寻回）should reuse the existing Typed Relationship + Provenance（类型化关系 + 来源溯源）model rather than inventing a generic “contribution” layer.

Existing Round 7（第七轮）typed relationships such as:

- holding（保管）;
- digitization（数字化）;
- transcription / collation（转录 / 校勘）;
- photography / illustration（摄影 / 插画）;
- data provision（资料提供）;
- translation（翻译）;
- publishing（出版）;

remain the preferred architecture pattern.
