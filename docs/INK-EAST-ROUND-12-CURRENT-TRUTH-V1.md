## 0. Decision at a glance / 结论一览

Round 12 does **not** define a VIP benefit catalogue.

It establishes:

```text
Open Core
+
Account × Capability × Context
+
independent Economic Commitment Signal where relevant
↓
contextual anti-abuse / capability policy
```

It explicitly rejects:

```text
Membership = universal account weight
Membership = trust
Membership = authority
Membership = normal-content paywall
Membership = required recurring product
```

Standalone recurring Membership remains **DEFERRED / PRODUCT EXISTENCE NOT YET JUSTIFIED**.

# 1. Controlling rules / 控制规则

## R12-1 — Open Core
Ordinary users must be able to use Project 3's normal core product loops without Membership, subject to proportionate safety, abuse, legal, rights and resource controls.

## R12-2 — Normal published content is not a Membership paywall
Normal published knowledge/editorial/community/Issue/archive content remains publicly readable when otherwise legally/policy eligible.

No 30% teaser paywall, VIP Library requirement or “pay to unlock knowledge” model is current truth.

## R12-3 — Economic Commitment Signal is an independent concept
Economic Commitment Signal（经济承诺信号） is a contextual risk/capability input.

It is not a public prestige level, identity class, authority class or universal trust score.

## R12-4 — Payment may supply economic-friction evidence
A real payment/economic commitment may be relevant when a restriction exists specifically to resist disposable, bulk-farmed, zero-cost or Sybil accounts.

It may justify earlier relaxation of **that specific restriction** when the economic commitment genuinely changes the abuse economics.

## R12-5 — Membership may be one signal source, not the architecture itself
A future Membership relationship may provide Economic Commitment Signal evidence.

The anti-abuse/capability architecture must not require Membership to exist and must not hard-code “member” as a universal permission bypass.

## R12-6 — Context-specific, not global account weight
Any effect is evaluated through **Account × Capability × Context**.

No global paid-user level or scalar account weight is created.

## R12-7 — Payment != Behavioral Trust
Payment does not automatically prove good behavior, longevity, integrity or future compliance.

Paid accounts remain subject to ordinary moderation and risk controls.

## R12-8 — Payment != Qualification / Recognition / Authority
Payment/Economic Commitment must not directly create Contributor Qualification, Expertise, factual/identity verification, Work Recognition, Reviewer Trust, Formal Nomination authority, Governance Authority, organic Recommendation advantage or epistemic/source authority.

## R12-9 — Restriction-specific justification is mandatory
Payment may relax a restriction only when payment actually mitigates the reason that restriction exists.

Restrictions based on legal/rights obligations, safety, serious moderation history, expertise, source/provenance requirements or unrelated resource constraints require their own logic.

## R12-10 — Legitimate non-paying path remains
For ordinary durable/long-form community publishing, legitimate non-paying users retain a path to broader capability through account history, behavior or other future approved evidence.

Payment may reduce waiting/friction; it does not make ordinary publishing permanently pay-only.

## R12-11 — Publishing capacity is the currently source-confirmed acceleration family
Current source-backed examples include selected new/low-history publishing controls such as maximum length, publishing frequency/rate limits and access to richer/long-form publishing capability where those limits exist for anti-abuse reasons.

Exact thresholds remain deferred.

## R12-12 — No automatic expansion to other capability families
Recognition voting, Contributor qualification, Identity Verification, governance/moderation authority, organic recommendation reach, editorial acceptance, Ask/Letter priority, service priority, private workspace, storage, export or AI capacity are not automatically Membership/Economic Commitment benefits.

Each needs independent justification if considered later.

## R12-13 — Standalone recurring Membership is deferred
Project 3 does not currently require a distinct recurring consumer Membership product.

Status:

**DEFERRED / PRODUCT EXISTENCE NOT YET JUSTIFIED.**

This is not a cancellation.

## R12-14 — Recurring subscription is not required by the anti-abuse mechanism
The existence of Economic Commitment Signal does not prove that payment must recur monthly/annually, be called Membership or require a member center.

## R12-15 — Do not invent Premium-only core features
The platform must not create artificial free-user deprivation merely to give a subscription something to sell.

## R12-16 — Future Membership must be evidence-led
Membership may be reopened if mature product use reveals genuine recurring value, operating cost, resource capacity, convenience, support/security, creator tooling or another legitimate ongoing commercial role.

Such future evidence must be evaluated then; it is not pre-approved now.

## R12-17 — Reading Room remains unresolved
Reading Room（阅读室） is not a confirmed current product and is not required by Round 12.

## R12-18 — Old benefit catalogues are provenance only
Old Workshop A/B benefit/tier/paywall structures, including the 80-item candidate catalogue, do not control current Membership design.

Individual ideas may only return through fresh need/cost/risk justification.

## R12-19 — Services do not become Membership by default
Round 13 services/monetization work must independently validate services and their commercial model.

A future service may provide evidence for reopening Membership, but service existence does not automatically create a Membership bundle.

## R12-20 — Commerce/payment primitives may remain evolvable
Project architecture may preserve the ability to support payments/subscriptions later without treating a currently unchosen Membership product as required product truth.

This is an architecture-flexibility rule, not implementation authorization.
