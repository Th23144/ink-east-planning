# Round 7 — Knowledge Graph & Provenance — Workshop C

Status: **PROPOSED / NOT YET ACCEPTED**. Product architecture only. No implementation authorization.

Scope: Claims, Evidence, assessment, uncertainty, disagreement, correction, and downstream re-evaluation.

Standing rule: precise boundaries, adaptive circumstances. No universal truth score, source rank, fixed confidence formula, or fixed review count.

## R7-C1 — Claim first-class only where needed
Material Claims may become independently addressable when evidence, disagreement, correction, attribution, or review requires it. Not every sentence must become a separate Claim object.

## R7-C2 — Claim / attribution / platform assessment stay separate
What is asserted, who reports it, which source/version contains or supports it, and any platform/editorial assessment remain distinct.

## R7-C3 — Evidence relation is directional and claim-specific
A source may support one Claim while contradicting, qualifying, contextualizing, or merely citing another. Citation alone is not proof.

## R7-C4 — Source assessment is purpose-scoped
No universal source-quality rank. A source may be strong for one question and weak for another.

## R7-C5 — Primary / secondary / tertiary are descriptive labels
They must not become an automatic authority hierarchy.

## R7-C6 — Absence of evidence != evidence of absence
No recorded support must not automatically become false. Direct contradiction, expected-but-absent evidence, incomplete research, and unknown states remain distinguishable.

## R7-C7 — Uncertainty is multi-dimensional
Identity, date, authorship, incomplete evidence, ambiguity, conflicting witnesses, provenance, and interpretation uncertainty may differ. No universal canonical percentage is required.

## R7-C8 — Scholarly disagreement remains recoverable
Credible competing positions may coexist with their sources/evidence and an optional editorial synthesis. Editorial preference does not erase competing scholarship.

## R7-C9 — Platform assessment is attributable and revisable
Material assessments preserve enough provenance to identify subject, conclusion, basis/evidence, assessment provenance, and revision history appropriate to consequence.

## R7-C10 — Claim state and source state are independent
A source becoming unavailable or questioned does not mechanically make every linked Claim false. Correcting a Claim does not erase the source that historically made it.

## R7-C11 — Material evidence changes support dependency-aware re-evaluation
Affected assessments may be marked or queued for proportionate review. Do not blindly rewrite every downstream object.

## R7-C12 — Correction / withdrawal / supersession / reinterpretation are distinct
These operations have different meanings and downstream effects and must not collapse into one generic state.

## R7-C13 — Material Claim revision preserves semantic history
Minor wording fixes need not create a new Claim. Material proposition/scope/time/evidentiary changes preserve prior state through versioning, supersession, or a new Claim as appropriate.

## R7-C14 — Quote / paraphrase / translation / interpretation / extraction stay distinguishable
Direct source wording must remain distinguishable from derivative or interpretive forms.

## R7-C15 — Derived or inferred Claims preserve derivation provenance
Inference must not later be presented as though the source directly stated the full conclusion.

## R7-C16 — Machine-derived Claims remain derivative/candidate knowledge until appropriately grounded
Material uses support source grounding, transformation provenance, extracted-vs-inferred distinction, and a correction/review path appropriate to consequence.

## R7-C17 — Public explanation and internal evidence may differ in depth
Reader-facing synthesis may be concise while internal/editorial evidence is deeper or restricted. Public certainty must not exceed the maintained assessment.

## R7-C18 — Research-grade semantics must not force research-grade complexity on every user
Support simple reader-facing synthesis plus expandable provenance/specialist/internal layers without flattening the underlying knowledge semantics.

## Cross-check
Workshop C preserves accepted Round 7 A/B boundaries: provenance != truth certification; citation != proof; source provenance / rights basis / epistemic assessment remain separate; unknown/disputed states may remain unresolved; corrections preserve provenance; canonical/source authority != Work Recognition; machine-generated output is not canonical merely because the platform produced it; public and restricted evidence layers may differ.

The user-confirmed Ancient Books Image Rights & Source Policy v0.1 remains the operating source for image-rights/search-clearance rules. Workshop C does not alter those rights rules.
