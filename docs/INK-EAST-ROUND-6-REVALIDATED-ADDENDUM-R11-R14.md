## R6-R11 — Personal Platform Context
A normal platform action may use a Personal Platform Context anchored to the authenticated Actor and Stable Platform Identity without requiring a resolved real-world Person record. Acting for an Organization remains a represented context requiring the applicable relationship, permission, scope and current checks.

## R6-R12 — Knowledge record vs operational Organization
A knowledge record describing an Organization does not itself create authority to operate as that Organization. Any operational binding is explicit and records its provenance. This is a semantic separation and does not require duplicate storage. Operational Organization control does not automatically govern all neutral or historical knowledge facts about that Organization.

## R6-R13 — Linked identities do not share standing automatically
Different Accounts or Stable Platform Identities associated with the same real-world Person do not automatically share Organization roles, staff capacity, contributor qualification use, reviewer/governance capability, or comparable operational standing. Projection or rebinding is explicit. Internal linkage alone is not an account-recovery factor.

## R6-R14 — Organization transitions do not union authority automatically
Knowledge-record reconciliation, real-world merger or succession, Organization split, subject correction and operational control migration are different events. None automatically combines all controllers, roles, grants, delegations or sensitive authority. Current operational authority migration requires explicit scoped reconciliation with provenance; historical attribution remains historically accurate.
