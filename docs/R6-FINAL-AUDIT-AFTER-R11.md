# Round 6 final audit after R11

Status: Round 6 remains reopened. No implementation. Do not enter Round 7.

R6-R11 is accepted. A fresh audit found three remaining proposals.

## R6-R12 — Organization knowledge record and operational control are separate
Type: HARDENING.
Creating or suggesting a knowledge record for an existing Organization does not create control, representation, verification, official status, or permissions. A platform Organization workspace may establish initial control only through an explicit authority-creation flow. Binding an operational Organization subject to an existing knowledge record is an explicit provenance-bearing relationship, not a side effect of page creation.

## R6-R13 — linked accounts do not automatically share authority
Type: NEW SAFEGUARD.
If multiple accounts or Stable Platform Identities relate to the same real-world Person, Organization roles, staff authority, Contributor Qualification, reviewer/governance capability, and similar operational standing do not automatically propagate to every linked account/persona. Authority attaches to an explicitly authorized platform principal/context. Recovery or rebinding requires an explicit controlled process.

## R6-R14 — Organization merge/split does not automatically union permissions
Type: HARDENING.
Duplicate-record merge, real-world merger/succession, or split/correction must not mechanically union or duplicate controllers, roles, grants, or sensitive permissions. Operational authority migration requires explicit scoped reconciliation/transfer with provenance and current security checks. Historical attribution remains separate from current authority migration.

These are proposed, not yet accepted. If accepted, incorporate R6-R11 through R6-R14 together, re-run affected parity checks, and perform one final no-new-finding verification before any replacement seal.
