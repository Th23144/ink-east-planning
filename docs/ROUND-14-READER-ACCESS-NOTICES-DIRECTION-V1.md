# Round 14 — Reader Access Notices Direction V1

Status: USER-CONFIRMED PRINCIPLE; DETAILS DEFERRED.

User-confirmed direction: For eligible published materials, use clear reader notices, sensitive-image preview warnings, and age verification when appropriate and required. Present the notices before exposure to the relevant restricted material.

Warnings and reader acknowledgment do not change existing publication or recommendation eligibility. A simple warning cannot substitute for effective age verification where it is required. Existing legal, safety, rights and prior product limits remain in effect.

Exact category mapping, age threshold, verification method, regional requirements, preview interaction, UI and privacy implementation remain deferred. No blanket account identity requirement is approved.
