# 1. Round 1 — Content & Publishing System（内容与发布系统）

## Current direction / 当前方向

1. Project 3 is **not permanently East-only（不永久局限东方文化）**. Eastern/Chinese culture is the initial wedge; the long-term platform may expand across broader culture, knowledge, place, travel, society, community and discovery.

2. Platform content is **not one generic Article/blog system（不是单一文章/博客系统）**. Distinct lanes include canonical/source-backed material, editorial/teaching publishing, contributor publishing, community/user publishing, discussion and Issue（议题） curation.

3. Ordinary users may eventually publish durable substantive work without first becoming a formally qualified Contributor（贡献者）. Publishing capability is capability-specific and anti-abuse aware; there is no blanket “new account prison” or global level ladder.

4. User publications can be durable knowledge-network assets with stable identity/permalink, search, saving, recommendation, topic/place/knowledge links, archival value and future curation.

5. Promotion/selection of a strong community work is **work-specific（作品级）**. It does not automatically upgrade the author’s identity, trust, qualification or governance standing.

6. Durable formal content should connect to meaningful Topic / Place / Knowledge Entity（主题 / 地点 / 知识实体） structure where useful, but classification must not force false singularity.

7. Canonical/source-backed content and social discussion remain separate but linkable. Ordinary social conversation must not become canonical source truth.

8. Issue（议题） is an editorial curation/publication layer over the broader content system, not the whole platform.

## Superseded Round 1 interpretations / 已淘汰解释

- permanent East-only specialist-network boundary;
- blanket fixed restrictions merely because an account is new;
- one global Account Weight（账户权重） score controlling all publishing capability;
- VIP（会员） status as a general shortcut to trust/authority;
- generic Article/blog-only architecture.

# 2. Round 2–3 — Work Recognition & Governance（作品认可与治理）

## 2.1 Core Recognition semantics / 核心认可语义

1. Work Recognition（作品认可） is **work-level**, not author-level.

2. Popularity / likes / saves / views / follower count / discussion volume are not Recognition by themselves.

3. Identity verification, Contributor Qualification（贡献者资格）, Organization/Partner status, Editorial origin and Membership payment do not automatically create Recognition evidence.

4. Canonical/source authority（原典 / 来源权威） and Work Recognition are different systems.

5. The earlier public name `Authoritative Content（权威内容）` is rejected because it overclaims epistemic authority. A high-quality/recognized destination or set of surfaces may exist, but there is no requirement for one permanent monolithic global Recognition Board（高认可内容板块）.

6. Recognition Framework（作品认可框架） applies only to eligible/evaluable durable work classes. It does not govern every reply, reaction, discussion message or social object.

## 2.2 Core lifecycle / 核心生命周期

Conceptually:

```text
Published Work / 已发布作品
→ Nomination Eligibility（推举资格）
→ Formal Nomination（正式推举）
→ Candidate（候选）
→ Structured Review（结构化评审）
→ Recognized / Not Recognized / Revision / Governance Review
```

Important:

- Nomination eligibility/exposure are workflow/distribution states, not extra prestige tiers.
- Candidate（候选） remains the same underlying Work（作品）, not a duplicate article.
- Formal Nomination（正式推举） asks whether a work deserves Candidate-review resources.
- Candidate Review（候选评审） asks whether it meets the applicable quality standard.

## 2.3 Eligibility & nomination / 资格与推举

1. Eligibility is content-type aware; there is no universal academic format or minimum word count.

2. A travel/local guide, cultural essay, classical-text analysis, tutorial or other durable content can use different evaluative profiles.

3. Formal nomination is a governance capability earned through suitable account/governance evidence; Membership/VIP cannot buy it.

4. Nomination opportunity is multi-source: system exploration, author request, qualified-user discovery, editorial/platform discovery and other legitimate routes.

5. Editorial/platform discovery creates **opportunity only**, not positive Recognition evidence.

6. Semi-blind Nomination Mode（半盲推举模式） should suppress unnecessary prestige/social-proof signals where feasible before judgment:
   - author identity/avatar where feasible;
   - VIP / contributor / institutional prestige markers;
   - followers/views/likes/saves/comments;
   - live nomination totals;
   - prior editorial promotion.

7. Provenance/evidence genuinely needed to judge authenticity or substance may remain visible; this is not full anonymity.

8. Skip / Unable to Judge（跳过 / 无法判断） is neutral.

## 2.4 Review integrity / 评审完整性

1. Reviewer Trust（评审信任） is separate from Account Trust（账户信任） and Contributor Qualification.

2. Reviewer Quality（评审质量） must not collapse into majority agreement.

3. Minority judgment can be high-quality when later evidence validates it.

4. Reviewer motivation must not be based on simple per-click/per-review quotas or raw review-count rewards.

5. Review labor must not directly buy traffic for the reviewer’s own work.

6. Reviewer identity is sensitive governance data:
   - private by default to the public where appropriate;
   - internally least-privilege;
   - public aggregates must resist deanonymization.

7. Conflict of Interest（利益冲突） is a first-class governance concern for consequential nomination/review decisions.

## 2.5 Recognition vs Recommendation / 作品认可与推荐

1. Recognition may be a bounded Recommendation（推荐） signal, but it cannot create a self-validating loop.

2. Exposure caused by Recognition must retain served-context provenance; downstream engagement is not independent proof that Recognition was correct.

3. Fair Exploration（公平探索） must preserve discovery opportunity for legitimate low-traffic/non-Recognized works.

4. Recognized works, famous authors, qualified contributors or institutions must not monopolize discovery capacity.

5. Paid/sponsored/commercial distribution is separate from organic Recognition/Recommendation.

## 2.6 Versioning, correction & reversal / 版本、纠正与撤销

1. Recognition is contestable and reversible, not a lifetime honor.

2. Minor edits may preserve prior evidence.

3. Material revision of the same underlying work may require affected dimensions to be re-reviewed.

4. Replacement-level rewrite / effectively new work restarts the normal Recognition journey; old review history remains provenance but does not confer Candidate continuity.

5. Recognition challenge/revocation/correction preserves historical decision provenance rather than silently rewriting history.

6. Historical Recognition and current availability/distribution/legal state remain separate.

7. Appeal（申诉）, Evidence Reversal（证据纠错） and Re-evaluation（重新评估） are distinct mechanisms.

# 3. Round 4 — Contributor Qualification & Identity（贡献者资格与身份）

## 3.1 Core separation / 核心分离

Current architecture separates:

- Stable Platform Identity（稳定平台身份）;
- Public Display Identity（公开展示身份）;
- factual Identity / Credential / Relationship Claims（身份 / 资历 / 关系主张）;
- Contributor Qualification（贡献者资格）;
- Expertise Scope（专业范围）;
- Account Trust / Capability（账户信任 / 能力）;
- Reviewer Trust / Governance Capability（评审信任 / 治理能力）;
- Work Recognition（作品认可）;
- Membership（会员关系）.

No one badge/score/payment state collapses these axes.

## 3.2 Contributor Qualification / 贡献者资格

1. The canonical term is **Contributor Qualification（贡献者资格）**, not `Verified Contributor`.

2. Qualification is evidence-based and scope-based.

3. Degree, job title, fame, followers, publication count or institutional affiliation cannot be one universal prestige shortcut.

4. Multiple legitimate evidence paths exist, such as academic/institutional, independent research/author, practitioner/craft, field/local knowledge and platform-native contribution.

5. These evidence paths are not public prestige tiers.

6. Platform-native users can qualify through sustained high-quality evidence without pre-existing external fame.

7. Qualification may be partial/scoped; one weak Scope does not automatically erase unrelated approved Scopes.

8. Qualification does not grant Work Recognition, Reviewer Trust or Governance Authority.

## 3.3 Verification, pseudonymity & privacy / 验证、化名与隐私

1. Identity Verification（身份验证） and Contributor Qualification are separate.

2. Public real-name display is not required.

3. Pseudonymous/public pen-name participation remains legitimate.

4. Verification strength should be claim-proportional and use the least-sensitive sufficient evidence.

5. Government ID / biometric/video proof is not a universal trust shortcut.

6. Organization attestation may prove a scoped fact such as affiliation/title/representation; it does not automatically mint Contributor Qualification.

7. Partner Organizations cannot bulk-mint qualified contributors from a roster.

## 3.4 Entry & newcomer handling / 申请与新用户

1. Contributor review may begin through Self Application（自行申请）, Platform Invitation（平台邀请）, Organization/Partner onboarding or future legitimate paths.

2. Entry route creates review opportunity, not qualification.

3. New-account anti-abuse controls must remain capability/risk-specific.

4. A genuinely high-value newcomer may be reviewed early through strong external evidence/invitation/onboarding without pretending they already have platform history.

5. Membership may reduce some zero-cost/Sybil friction for selected capabilities, but cannot prove professional competence or governance reliability.

## 3.5 Contributor value / 贡献者产品价值

Contributor Qualification may legitimately support:

- stronger authoring capability/tools;
- citation/provenance/versioning tooling;
- richer profile/portfolio context;
- direct eligibility for editorial, Issue, collaboration, research/teaching opportunities;
- contributor-oriented browse/filter surfaces.

It does **not** automatically grant:

- Work Recognition;
- stronger Formal Nomination weight;
- Reviewer Trust;
- Governance Authority;
- permanent high ranking.

## 3.6 Distribution correction / 分发纠正

The old Contributor-only baseline launch-support concept is superseded.

Current rule:

> **Low-traffic high-quality potential should have a real path to discovery regardless of author class.**

Contributor Qualification can help context/audience matching and product tooling, but cannot create a guaranteed organic ranking multiplier.

# 4. Round 5 — Account Trust, Capability & Integrity（账户信任、能力与完整性）

## 4.1 No level system / 不建立等级体系

1. `Account Weight（账户权重）` may remain historical shorthand, but production architecture does not use one universal scalar score.

2. No public `Lv1/Lv2/Lv3`, Account Trust number, penalty-point table or social-credit-style progress bar.

3. Account Trust is multidimensional evidence, not a universal rank.

4. Capabilities unlock individually through capability-specific evidence/policy.

Canonical pattern:

```text
Account evidence vector（账户证据向量）
→ capability-specific policy（能力特定策略）
→ allow / bounded allow / friction / review / restrict
```

## 4.2 Evidence model / 证据模型

Possible dimensions include:

- Account Maturity（账户成熟度）;
- Anti-abuse Confidence（反滥用置信）;
- Behavioral Integrity（行为完整性）;
- Participation Reliability（参与可靠性）;
- Contribution History（贡献历史）;
- Account Stability / Security（账户稳定 / 安全）;
- Governance Readiness（治理准备度）.

Rules:

- account age matters only with real normal use;
- raw counts/logins/likes/comments/posts are not the primary growth mechanism;
- popularity is not Account Trust;
- one successful work does not directly unlock high governance power;
- good-faith mistakes differ from deliberate manipulation;
- Identity Verification is only bounded supporting evidence where relevant.

## 4.3 Account Trust vs Reviewer Trust / 账户信任与评审信任

1. Account Trust and Reviewer Trust remain permanently separate.

2. Account Trust may be a prerequisite for some governance capability, but it does not transform into Reviewer Trust.

3. Formal Nomination / Reviewer capability requires governance-specific evidence and cannot be purchased.

## 4.4 Adaptive capability model / 自适应能力模型

1. Normal use should feel normal; controls target abuse-prone capabilities rather than punishing every new user.

2. Capability Risk Profiles（能力风险画像） are multidimensional, e.g.:
   - Abuse Scalability（滥用可扩张性）;
   - Reach Leverage（传播杠杆）;
   - Trust-Claim Consequence（信任主张后果）;
   - Governance Consequence（治理后果）.

3. One capability may have multiple risk dimensions; do not force everything into a single tier.

4. Prefer adaptive limits, anomaly detection, low-intrusion challenge and local restriction over blanket punishment.

5. Publishing Capability != Recommendation Eligibility != Recommendation Ranking（发布能力 ≠ 推荐资格 ≠ 推荐排序）.

## 4.5 Membership boundary / 会员边界

1. Membership/payment can provide real economic friction against mass-created/Sybil accounts.

2. It may reduce selected anti-abuse/time-based friction or unlock legitimate product capabilities.

3. It cannot fabricate:
   - behavioral history;
   - Contribution History;
   - Contributor Qualification;
   - Reviewer Trust;
   - Governance Authority;
   - Work Recognition;
   - organic Recommendation advantage.

4. Membership product packaging/value is a later product decision and must not silently become a trust hierarchy.

## 4.6 Internal mechanics vs user transparency / 内部机制与用户透明度

1. Internal scoring/features/thresholds remain non-public where disclosure would enable gaming.

2. Minor ranking/calibration changes need not trigger per-event warnings.

3. Consequential/durable loss of important capability should normally receive a coarse understandable status/reason where feasible.

4. Exact thresholds and recovery recipes must not be exposed as a farmable checklist.

Canonical principle:

> **Hide the scoring machinery; do not hide material capability loss.**

## 4.7 Sensitive Operations Guard（敏感操作保护闸）

1. Credible account-integrity/evasion/takeover risk may trigger cross-cutting protection for sensitive mutations.

2. It is not a punishment tier.

3. Sensitive operations may include identity/contact/recovery change, high-trust claim mutation, Organization representation/control, privileged credentials/tokens and other high-impact authority changes.

4. Low-confidence anomaly does not justify blanket freeze.

5. Secure Recovery Path（安全恢复路径） must remain available for compromised legitimate users.

6. Controls may be Allow（允许）, Step-up Verification（升级确认）, Protective Hold（防护性冻结）, local restriction or broader action where justified.

## 4.8 Multi-account / Sybil / coordinated abuse（多账户 / 女巫 / 协同滥用）

1. Multiple accounts are not inherently abuse.

2. Core problem is **Deceptive Independence（欺骗性独立伪装）**—non-independent actors/accounts pretending to be independent evidence.

3. IP/device/VPN/shared network is weak contextual evidence, not proof by itself.

4. Legitimate coordination and Coordinated Abuse（协同滥用） are distinct.

5. Prefer Signal Neutralization（信号中和） for suspicious social-proof manipulation before unnecessary content/account punishment where appropriate.

6. Evidence Count != Independent Evidence（证据数量 ≠ 独立证据）.

7. Coordinated reports/nominations/reviews must not mechanically count as independent.

8. VIP/payment, Contributor Qualification or Organization identity is not an independence guarantee.

9. Material false-linkage errors require review/reconsideration and Evidence Reversal where appropriate.

## 4.9 Account integrity / 账户完整性

1. Account Compromise（账号被盗） and Owner Abuse（用户本人滥用） are distinct.

2. Sensitive-operation changes are auditable.

3. Recovery is capability-specific, not one universal all-or-nothing unfreeze event.

4. Cooling/waiting periods are adaptive, not one universal number.

# 5. Cross-cutting corrections that control Rounds 1–5 / 控制第一至第五轮的跨轮纠正

1. Editorial/platform discovery may create Recognition opportunity but zero positive Recognition evidence by itself.

2. Platform-owned / Official / Partner / commercial origin receives no Recognition fast lane.

3. Recognition and Recommendation must preserve a causal firewall.

4. Fair Exploration must prevent prestige/Recognition incumbency from monopolizing discovery.

5. Served-context provenance is required where needed for recommendation audit/learning, but data collection remains privacy-minimized.

6. Reviewer identity is least-privilege sensitive governance data; public aggregates must resist deanonymization.

7. Organization attestation proves scoped facts and cannot bulk-mint Contributor Qualification.

8. Reviewer Trust must not become a self-validating majority-agreement loop.

9. Conflict of Interest is first-class but privacy/purpose bounded.

10. Status/badge stacking must not recreate a visible or algorithmic caste.

11. Appeal is a correction path, not unlimited replay.

12. Filing appeal does not automatically restore risky capability.

13. Appeal / Evidence Reversal / Re-evaluation remain distinct.

14. Account deletion, private-data deletion, Work withdrawal, anonymization, legal removal and public-knowledge retention are separate.

15. Privacy-safe Tombstone（占位记录） may preserve referential integrity where lawful.

16. Canonical/source authority describes source identity/provenance, not blanket factual truth.

17. Canonical/source authority != Work Recognition.

18. Moderation visibility restriction != Recommendation ranking != user preference.

19. Material manual/policy distribution interventions need provenance/reviewability.

20. Account Recovery != Organization Control Recovery.

21. Organization control transfer does not transfer personal trust/qualification/governance history.

22. Legitimate Joint Action（联合行动） is allowed; cross-entity permission fusion is not.

23. Ordinary participation does not require universal real-world Person resolution.

24. Anti-abuse correlation is not automatically a factual/public identity or social relationship.

25. Historical Recognition != current availability/distribution/legal/policy status.

# 8. Safe handoff summary / 安全交接摘要

If a future model remembers only one thing from Rounds 1–5:

> **Separate identity, qualification, work quality, account maturity, reviewer/governance evidence, recommendation exposure and commercial Membership. Use adaptive capability-specific evidence instead of levels. Give every legitimate high-potential work a fair path to discovery. Preserve provenance/history. Do not let prestige, payment, popularity, platform affiliation or institutional status silently become authority.**
