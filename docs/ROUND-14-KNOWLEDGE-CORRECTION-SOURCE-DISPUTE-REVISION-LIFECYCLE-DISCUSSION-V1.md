## 1. Why this follows moderation（为什么下一步转向知识纠错）
Round 14（第十四轮） has resolved moderation action scope and appeal basics. Ink & East（墨与东方） must now give **knowledge correction（知识纠错）** its own lifecycle instead of treating every suspected factual issue as a report of misconduct（违规举报）.

The controlling question:
> **How can users, authors, editors and researchers correct a source-based record, disagree in good faith, and inspect a trustworthy change history without turning every correction into punishment?（如何让用户、作者、编辑与研究者纠正来源型内容、善意持有异议，并查看可信修改历史，而不把每次纠错都变成处罚？）**

## 2. Proposed distinction: error vs uncertainty vs dispute（区分错误、不确定与争议）
Do not force all contested knowledge into “true vs false（真 / 假）”.
- **Correction（纠错）:** verifiable mistake in a date, citation, transcription, attribution, etc.（可核实的日期、引用、转录、归属等错误）。
- **Uncertainty（不确定）:** evidence is incomplete or provenance remains unresolved（证据不足或来源未定）。
- **Good-faith Dispute（善意争议）:** multiple interpretations remain supportable by their respective sources（不同解释均有可成立的证据）。
- **Misconduct（违规）:** separately established deliberate fabrication, forgery or abuse（另有证据支持的故意编造、伪造或滥用）。

Correction does not itself imply misconduct. A dispute must not be quietly erased merely because one interpretation is preferred.

## 3. Candidate correction lifecycle（候选纠错生命周期）
1. **Propose（提出）:** user/editor/researcher identifies claim, affected content and evidence（指出具体主张、对应内容与依据）.
2. **Triage（分流）:** determine content class and issue type; identify who owns the correction decision（按内容类型 / 问题类型分流并识别责任方）.
3. **Evaluate（评估）:** verify evidence, source edition and context; preserve uncertainty when needed（核验证据、版本和上下文；必要时保留不确定性）.
4. **Resolve（处理）:** correct, annotate dispute, update sources, decline with reason or escalate a separate misconduct review（纠正、标注争议、增补来源、有理由不采纳，或另开违规审查）.
5. **Record（留痕）:** record material revisions, reason, evidence and time（保存重要修订、理由、依据与时间）.
6. **Revisit（再审）:** reopen when material new evidence or an earlier assessment error arises（有实质新证据或先前判断错误时复核）.

This is a proposed product-level flow, not a committed interface or staffing plan.

## 4. Content class controls responsibilities（按内容类型区分责任）
- **Canonical / Source-backed（规范 / 来源型）:** strongest provenance and material revision traceability; preserve version/edition distinctions（来源记录与重要修订可追溯；保留不同版本 / 底本差异）.
- **Editorial / Teaching（编辑 / 教学）:** accountable editorial correction with material change notes（由编辑责任体系处理重要纠错并说明修订）.
- **Contributor（贡献者） work:** author participation where appropriate; platform recognition/qualification remains a separate review（适当时由作者参与；认可 / 资格另行审查）.
- **Community / User（社区 / 用户）:** lightweight user-authored edits / corrections, with escalation for substantiated harm（优先轻量自我修订；有事实依据的伤害另行处理）.

Do not collapse these lanes into one generic Article（文章） workflow.

## 5. User-facing transparency without complexity（面向用户透明，但不复杂）
For a materially changed source-based record, show a short correction note and a way to inspect relevant changes（重要来源型记录显示简短修订说明，并提供查看相关变更的入口）. Minor typos should not require prominent public announcements（普通错字无需显著公示）.

Keep confidential/private evidence access-limited（私密证据限制访问）. Disclosure scope is distinct from internal provenance preservation（公开程度与内部留证不同）。

## 6. Initial questions for user decision（初步待确认问题）
1. Do you agree that **Error / Uncertainty / Dispute / Misconduct（错误 / 不确定 / 争议 / 违规）** should be distinct states rather than one true/false verdict（单一真假判决）?
2. Should a correction request identify the **specific claim + evidence（具体主张 + 证据）**, while allowing ordinary users to submit a simpler report first（普通用户可先轻量提交）?
3. Should **Canonical（规范来源型）** and **Editorial / Contributor / Community（编辑 / 贡献者 / 社区）** have different correction responsibilities and historical traceability?
4. Should major corrections be publicly explainable, while trivial edits remain lightweight and sensitive evidence stays private?
5. Should disagreement with a sourced interpretation be eligible for an annotated **Dispute（争议）** state without automatically deleting the work or punishing its author?

## 7. Boundaries（边界）
No editor, reviewer or contributor acquires global authority merely by holding a role. Correction, Recognition（认可）, Conduct Moderation（行为审核） and Qualification（资格） remain distinct decisions. Exact user flows, thresholds, storage format and reviewer permissions remain for later implementation/policy discussion.
