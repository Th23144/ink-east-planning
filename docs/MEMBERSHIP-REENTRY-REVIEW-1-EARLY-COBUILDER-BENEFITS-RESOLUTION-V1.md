## 1. Confirmed treatment families（已确认待遇类型）

Early Co-builder Relationship（早期共建者关系） may legitimately include:

### A — Permanent Historical Recognition（永久历史承认）
- early/founding-stage history（早期 / 创始阶段历史）;
- optional public acknowledgment（可选公开致谢）;
- private archival record of early co-building history（个人早期共建历史档案）;
- milestone / join-era history（里程碑 / 加入阶段历史）.

### B — Closer Platform Communication（更近的平台沟通）
- platform/project briefings（平台 / 项目简报）;
- process / roadmap visibility（过程 / 路线可见性）;
- direct feedback windows（直接反馈窗口）;
- product research invitations（产品研究邀请）.

### C — Priority Access to Limited Opportunities（限量机会优先参与）
- beta access（测试版优先体验）;
- selected events（部分活动）;
- salons / roundtables（沙龙 / 圆桌）;
- research/project participation（研究 / 项目参与）;
- partner ecosystem courtesy（合作生态礼遇）.

### D — Commemorative / Symbolic Value（纪念 / 象征价值）
- founding-stage digital mark（创始阶段数字标识）;
- anniversary acknowledgment（周年纪念）;
- physical commemorative objects（实体纪念物）;
- archival/history-wall treatment（档案 / 历史墙呈现）.

### E — Future Economic Opportunity Access（未来经济机会入口）
- Creator Reward（创作者奖励） opportunities;
- Referral Reward（推荐奖励） opportunities;
- paid project work（有偿项目机会）;
- event/research compensation（活动 / 研究报酬）;
- other actual-value-linked compensation opportunities（其他与真实价值创造挂钩的报酬机会）.

## 2. Common history, typed opportunity（共同历史承认 + 按类型匹配机会）

The platform should not give every Early Co-builder（早期共建者） every special opportunity merely because they share one historical label.

A better rule is:

> **Common historical recognition may be shared; specific opportunities should depend on the person's actual co-building history, relevance and current fit.（共同历史承认可以共享；具体机会应依据个人真实共建历史、相关性与当前适配度决定。）**

Examples（例）:
- product co-builders（产品共建者） may be relevant for future Product Research（产品研究）;
- cultural-recovery participants（文化寻回参与者） may be relevant for future recovery/research work;
- early high-quality publishers（早期优质发布者） may be relevant for creator programs;
- early presence alone（仅早期在场） can be historically acknowledged without automatically receiving every later opportunity.

## 3. Anti-hierarchy boundary（反等级边界）

Special treatment is relationship depth, not universal superiority.

It does not create:
- Governance Authority（治理权）;
- Editorial Authority（编辑权）;
- Work Recognition（作品认可） advantage;
- Contributor Qualification（贡献者资格）;
- source/truth authority（来源 / 真相权威）;
- permanent organic ranking boost（永久自然排序加权）;
- a universal priority across all product surfaces（全产品面统一优先权）.

## 4. Economic opportunity rule（经济机会规则）

Early Co-builder status（早期共建者身份） may help surface relevant opportunities, but compensation remains tied to the qualifying activity/value created.

Historical status itself does not create a standing claim on platform revenue（平台收入）.
