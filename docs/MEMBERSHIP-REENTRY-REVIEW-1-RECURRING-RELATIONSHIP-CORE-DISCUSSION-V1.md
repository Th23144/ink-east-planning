## 1. The missing question（缺失的核心问题）

Supporter-first（支持者优先） sounds plausible, but a recurring Membership（持续会员） still needs a reason to be recurring.

The key question is not:

> “What perks can we add?”

It is:

> **What ongoing relationship is the user paying to maintain?（用户持续付费，究竟是在维持什么长期关系？）**

---

## 2. Three distinct models that must not be confused（三种不能混在一起的模型）

### Model A — Donation / One-time Support（捐赠 / 一次性支持）

User pays because they want to help the project.

No ongoing product relationship is required.

This may be the most honest model if recurring value is weak.

### Model B — Recurring Supporter Relationship（持续支持者关系）

User chooses to continuously sustain Ink & East（墨与东方）.

The value is not access to locked knowledge, but an ongoing relationship:
- regular transparency about what support sustains（持续了解支持资金维持什么）;
- periodic editorial/project updates（定期编辑 / 项目更新）;
- supporter history / continuity（支持历史 / 连续性）;
- selected participation opportunities where capacity exists（有条件时参与部分活动）;
- evolving benefits as the platform matures（平台成熟后逐步增加真实权益）.

The recurring payment is partly mission support and partly relationship continuity.

### Model C — Premium Subscription Product（高级订阅产品）

User pays primarily for a recurring bundle of utilities, convenience, reduced ads, partner benefits, scarce experiences or other measurable value.

This requires much stronger recurring product value than currently exists.

---

## 3. Current architecture hypothesis（当前架构假设）

Ink & East（墨与东方） may not need to jump directly from “free platform” to Model C（高级订阅产品）.

A possible evolution is:

> Donation / One-time Support（一次性支持）
> + Recurring Supporter Relationship（持续支持者关系）
> → later, if enough real recurring value emerges,
> Premium Membership（高级会员） or richer Membership（更完整会员）.

This means Membership（会员） may begin as a relationship product before it becomes a feature-rich subscription product.

---

## 4. What must be proven before approval（批准前必须证明什么）

Before Membership（会员） can be considered sufficiently defined, the project must answer:

1. Why recurring instead of one-time?（为什么持续付费，而不是一次性支持？）
2. What does the user continuously receive or participate in?（用户持续获得或参与什么？）
3. What remains meaningful even if ads are still low?（广告很少时，什么仍然有价值？）
4. What remains meaningful even if partner benefits are still sparse?（合作方很少时，什么仍然有价值？）
5. What can be delivered without creating heavy human-service liability?（什么可以持续交付，又不会制造沉重人工负担？）
6. What is the minimum honest Membership（会员） proposition that is valuable without becoming a paywall?（不做知识付费墙的前提下，最小但诚实的会员价值主张是什么？）

---

## 5. Immediate discussion target（当前讨论目标）

The next discussion should decide whether early Ink & East（墨与东方） should support:

- only one-time support（仅一次性支持）;
- recurring Supporter Membership（持续支持型会员）;
- both simultaneously（两者同时存在）;
- or keep all supporter payment deferred until stronger recurring value appears（继续暂缓所有支持型付费，直到持续价值更强）.

No option is selected yet.
