## 1. Distinct rights（不同权力分离）
A Report / Correction Proposal（反馈 / 纠错建议）, Editing Authority（修改权）, Correction Decision（纠错决定权）, and Recognition Authority（作品认可决定权） are distinct.

Submitting evidence does not confer permission to overwrite another person's work（提交证据不等于可以覆盖他人作品）. Correction findings do not automatically remove Recognition（认可）.

## 2. Accountability follows actual content responsibility（责任由内容类型与实际知识义务决定）
- Canonical / Source-backed（规范 / 来源型） records: source and edition fidelity, explicit provenance and responsible correction（来源 / 版本忠实、可追溯归因和责任纠错）.
- Editorial / Teaching（编辑 / 教学） work: accountable editorial corrections（编辑责任纠错）.
- Independent Research / Contributor（独立研究 / 贡献者） work: preserve authorship and author response, with independent qualification/recognition determinations（保护作者表达与回应；资格 / 认可独立判定）.
- Ordinary Community / User（普通社区 / 用户） work: author-led editing and baseline moderation（作者自行修改和基础社区审核）; no mandatory academic adjudication for ordinary interpretive disagreement（不因普通观点分歧强制学术裁判）.

No universal Reviewer（全局审查者） may automatically adjudicate all content.

## 3. Author's right to respond, not indefinite veto（作者享有回应权，不享有无限否决权）
For substantial in-scope corrections（适用范围内的重大纠错）, give the author a reasonable opportunity to respond or revise.

The author cannot indefinitely prevent an evidence-backed correction to the platform's own accountable records or prevent a properly scoped explanatory note（作者不能无限阻止平台纠正自身负责的记录或附上适当证据说明）.

This is **not** permission to silently rewrite an independent author's opinion（不允许悄悄改写独立作者的观点）.

## 4. Evidence-backed disputes and independent escalation（有证据的争议与必要时独立复核）
Where multiple interpretations are supportable（不同解释均有合理证据）, prefer preserving original interpretation with an accurate, proportionate dispute/source note（保留原解释并酌情附上准确的来源争议说明）.

If consequences are high and a dispute cannot be resolved fairly by one deciding party, provide appropriate independent review（后果重大时采用适当独立复核）.

Neither disagreement nor source correction automatically constitutes Misconduct（违规）, removal, withdrawal of Work Recognition（作品认可） or loss of Contributor Qualification（贡献者资格）.

## 5. Explainable, proportionate, private where necessary（可解释、适度、必要时保护隐私）
For consequential corrections or disputes, keep a clear, proportionate explanation and relevant revision history.

Do not publish confidential research/sensitive evidence merely to display transparency（不得仅为展示透明而公开私密研究资料 / 敏感证据）. Ordinary community editing remains lightweight（普通社区编辑保持轻量）.

## 6. Important limitation（重要限制）
**This resolution confirms rights and decision boundaries, not a complete final-outcome taxonomy（本结论确认权责边界，并不等于完整的结案结果类型已被批准）.**

Potential final outcomes such as Confirmed Error（确认错误）, Supported Dispute（合理争议）, Insufficient Evidence（证据不足）, Unsupported Correction Request（纠错请求不成立） or separately established Misconduct（经独立认定的违规） remain to be discussed as an explicit product outcome framework（仍需作为明确的产品结案结果框架讨论）.
