# 会员重启检查 #1——平台形成阶段边界讨论 V1

## 1. Why this boundary matters（为什么这个边界重要）

Early Co-builder Relationship（早期共建者关系） is now confirmed as a historical relationship, separate from Membership（会员）.

The next unresolved question is:

> **What counts as the platform's formative stage（形成阶段）, and when should new Early Co-builder（早期共建者） history naturally stop being created?**

Without a boundary:
- “early” can expand forever and lose meaning;
- later ordinary users may be unfairly excluded or ambiguously classified;
- the platform may accidentally create a permanent closed caste.

---

## 2. Candidate boundary models（候选边界模型）

### Model A — Fixed Date / Time Window（固定日期 / 时间窗口）
Define a calendar cutoff.

Example:
- users/contributions before a certain date may qualify as formative-stage history.

Strength:
- simple and auditable.

Risk:
- arbitrary if the product is still genuinely formative after that date.

### Model B — Product Milestone Boundary（产品里程碑边界）
The formative stage ends when the platform reaches a defined product milestone.

Possible milestone examples:
- public launch（公开上线）;
- stable V1（稳定第一版）;
- first major growth threshold（首个重大增长阈值）;
- first stable monetization phase（首个稳定商业化阶段）;
- completion of foundational product architecture / core systems（基础产品架构 / 核心系统完成）.

Strength:
- tied to actual product evolution.

Risk:
- milestone definition may be subjective or move over time.

### Model C — Community Maturity Boundary（社区成熟度边界）
The formative stage ends when early users are no longer materially shaping basic platform norms/structure.

Possible signals:
- community self-sustains without founder-stage intervention;
- content/network density reaches stable operation;
- early feedback is no longer structurally shaping core product identity.

Strength:
- conceptually faithful to “co-building”.

Risk:
- hard to measure cleanly.

### Model D — Hybrid Boundary（混合边界）
Use a primary product/community milestone plus a bounded time cap.

Example:
> formative stage ends at the earlier/later of a defined milestone and a maximum time window.

Strength:
- reduces arbitrariness on either side.

Risk:
- more complex.

---

## 3. Qualification should still be activity-based（资格仍应基于真实活动）

Even inside the formative stage, mere account creation should not automatically create full Early Co-builder（早期共建者） history.

The platform may distinguish:
- Early Presence（早期在场）;
- materially meaningful co-building histories（具有实际意义的共建历史）.

This preserves:
> **being early（来得早） != materially co-building（真正共建）**.

---

## 4. Historical recognition after the window closes（形成阶段结束后的历史承认）

Once the formative stage closes:
- existing Early Co-builder（早期共建者） history remains permanently preservable;
- new users may still become important Contributors / Recovery Participants / creators / partners / supporters（贡献者 / 寻回参与者 / 创作者 / 合作方 / 支持者） under their own systems;
- later users are not lower-value people; they simply do not share the same early historical context.

---

## 5. Current decision questions（当前决策题）

1. Should the formative-stage boundary be milestone-based rather than a simple date?
2. Should “mere early registration（只是早注册）” be recorded as Early Presence（早期在场） but not automatically equal full co-building?
3. Should the platform allow multiple typed Early Co-builder histories to remain open during the formative stage rather than one binary label?
4. Should the formative stage close explicitly once the product/community reaches a defined maturity milestone?
5. Should later users remain fully eligible for all non-historical platform roles/opportunities without being treated as lower status?
