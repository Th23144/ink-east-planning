## 0. Purpose / 目的

Before revalidating Custom Reading / Custom Ebook or discussing prices, Round 13 first separates:

- what is already a real commercial domain;
- what is only transaction/support infrastructure;
- what is a legacy service concept needing revalidation;

This avoids treating every place where money appears as the same “business model”.

# 5. Structural commercial boundary / 商业化结构边界

## Naturally commercial / 天然可以商业化

Current evidence supports or permits the following categories:

1. **Physical goods transaction** — confirmed through Spatial Flow commerce.
2. **Separately delivered human/custom service** — possible if the service itself passes revalidation.
3. **Other real-cost transactional products** — only when independently validated later.

## Must remain structurally independent from payment / 必须与付费保持结构独立

Payment must not determine:

- factual/source truth;
- Canonical authority;
- Contributor Qualification;
- Identity/Claim verification truth;
- Work Recognition;
- editorial selection/inclusion outcome;
- Reviewer standing;
- governance/moderation authority;
- organic Recommendation;
- ordinary published-content visibility;
- ordinary legitimate platform participation.

Commercial activity may coexist with these systems, but must not become their evidence source.

# 7. The first genuine Round 13 product decision / 第十三轮第一个真正产品选择

Before revalidating individual services, Round 13 needs to decide the **commercial relationship between Ink & East and Spatial Flow** at the conceptual level.

### Direction A — Shared platform, commercially distinct product domains
**One underlying Project 3 platform / account / infrastructure, but distinct commercial semantics.**

- Spatial Flow = physical product/space-oriented commerce domain;
- Ink & East = editorial/knowledge/community domain;
- service products attach to the domain whose user need they actually serve;
- cross-linking is allowed;
- commerce signals do not contaminate editorial truth/recommendation/Recognition.

This does not require two unrelated websites or two separate account systems.

### Direction B — Blend commerce and editorial into one unified commercial surface
Ink & East content, services and Spatial Flow goods behave as one storefront/business surface.

Risk:
- editorial and commerce semantics become easy to collapse;
- shop behavior can start driving knowledge/discovery architecture;
- unclear whether the current distinct brand/product meanings survive.

### Direction C — Keep Ink & East and Spatial Flow almost operationally separate
Share little beyond company ownership.

Risk:
- duplicates account/search/support/data infrastructure;
- undermines Project 3's long-term shared-platform direction.

---

# 8. Provisional architecture recommendation / 暂定架构建议

**Direction A is the strongest current architecture to test.**

Reason:

- it matches Project 3 as one long-term source-native platform for both domains;
- it preserves Spatial Flow as a real commercial engine;
- it prevents ecommerce incentives from redefining Ink & East editorial/knowledge truth;
- it still allows shared account, infrastructure, search plumbing, support and contextual cross-links where appropriate;
- it does not prematurely decide the final umbrella brand, URL structure or public navigation.

This is a **discussion recommendation**, not Current Truth until user confirmation.
