## 1. Why this is the next necessary layer（为什么这是下一层）

Spatial Flow（空间流）is now confirmed as a Preferred Strategic Partner（优先战略合作伙伴） without platform privilege.

That creates a real Conflict of Interest（利益冲突） surface whenever Ink & East（墨与东方） editorial/research work overlaps with Spatial Flow（空间流） commercial interests.

Existing architecture already controls several points:
- Partner / commercial relationship cannot manufacture Work Recognition（作品认可）;
- paid/sponsored/commercial inclusion cannot masquerade as ordinary editorial curation（普通编辑策展）;
- commercial conversion remains separate from editorial merit（编辑价值）;
- material Conflict of Interest（利益冲突） may require disclosure, recusal, evidence exclusion or independent review;
- partnership does not create ranking privilege（排序特权）.

The unresolved task is to define how these general rules apply specifically to the long-term Ink & East ↔ Spatial Flow（墨与东方 ↔ 空间流） relationship.

## 2. Candidate hard editorial rules（候选编辑硬规则）

### E1 — Independent coverage（独立报道 / 研究）
Ink & East（墨与东方） may cover, research, compare, cite or discuss subjects that overlap Spatial Flow（空间流） commercial interests.

Spatial Flow（空间流） cannot veto an Ink & East（墨与东方） editorial conclusion merely because the result is commercially inconvenient.

### E2 — Real criticism must remain possible（必须保留真实批评能力）
Ink & East（墨与东方） must be able to:
- decline to feature Spatial Flow（空间流）;
- criticize a Spatial Flow（空间流） product/practice where editorially justified;
- conclude that another maker/brand/source is more relevant or better supported for the editorial purpose;
- publish an unfavorable or mixed conclusion without requiring Spatial Flow（空间流） approval.

### E3 — Commercial support cannot buy editorial conclusion（商业支持不能购买编辑结论）
Any future sponsorship, advertising, project funding, affiliate/referral arrangement or other commercial relationship must not purchase:
- favorable editorial conclusions（正面编辑结论）;
- Issue Inclusion（议题收录）;
- Work Recognition（作品认可）;
- source authority（来源权威）;
- organic ranking（自然排序）.

### E4 — Material conflict handling（重大利益冲突处理）
Where a specific Ink & East（墨与东方） editorial/research decision materially affects a Spatial Flow（空间流） commercial interest, the conflict should be recognized as a real governance condition.

Possible proportionate responses may include:
- internal conflict flag（内部利益冲突标记）;
- disclosure（披露）;
- recusal（回避）;
- independent editorial/research review（独立编辑 / 研究复核）;
- separation of commercial and editorial decision-makers（商业与编辑决策者分离）.

No universal recusal rule is assumed for every mention or every overlapping topic.

### E5 — Spatial Flow self-publishing remains self-publishing（空间流自行发布仍是自行发布）
When Spatial Flow（空间流） publishes its own content on Ink & East（墨与东方） platform surfaces:
- Spatial Flow（空间流） is the publisher/speaker;
- the content is not transformed into Ink & East Editorial（墨与东方编辑部） speech;
- publication on the platform does not itself create editorial endorsement.

## 3. Commercial placement remains a separate later product（商业展示仍属独立后续产品）

Paid / Sponsored Discovery（付费 / 赞助发现） is not currently authorized by the sealed Discovery / Recommendation（发现 / 推荐） architecture.

Therefore this discussion should not prematurely invent an advertising product.

If such a system is later authorized, it must remain separately labeled and must not masquerade as organic recommendation or ordinary editorial selection.

The immediate questions are:

2. Should E3（商业支持不能购买编辑结论） be a Hard Boundary（硬边界）?
3. For E4（重大利益冲突）, should the platform use proportional handling（按情境处理） rather than forcing automatic recusal for every overlap?
4. If the same owner/team has interests in both products, should direct commercial responsibility for Spatial Flow（空间流） be treated as a material conflict when that person makes consequential Ink & East（墨与东方） editorial judgments specifically about Spatial Flow（空间流） or its direct commercial interests?
5. Should the detailed public disclosure format（具体公开披露形式） remain deferred while the principle “material conflicts must be handled” is decided now?
