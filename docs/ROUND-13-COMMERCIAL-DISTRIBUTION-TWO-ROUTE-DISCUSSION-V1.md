## 1. Why two routes（为什么需要两条路径）

The user has described both:
- future classic advertising / traffic monetization（传统广告 / 流量商业化）;
- normal substantive content published by commercial organizations, with optional paid heating（商业组织正常发布实质内容 + 可选付费加热）.

These should not be forced into one content type.

A clean model has two distinct commercial-distribution routes.

---

## 2. Route A — Content-first Paid Amplification（内容优先的付费加热）

Flow（流程）:

```text
Organization / User（组织 / 用户）
→ creates a normal content object（创建普通内容对象）
→ passes ordinary content eligibility / moderation（通过普通内容发布资格 / 审核）
→ publishes normally（正常发布）
→ may also purchase Paid Amplification（可另行购买付费加热）
→ paid exposure is explicitly represented（付费曝光显式标记）
```

Properties（属性）:
- the underlying Work（作品） remains the same object;
- it may still receive ordinary eligible organic exposure separately;
- paid exposure is a distinct Served Context（投放情境）;
- paid exposure creates no Recognition（作品认可） or authority benefit;
- paid-generated engagement must not be silently counted as independent organic-quality proof.

This route fits the user's travel-guide example.

---

## 3. Route B — Pure Advertisement / Commercial Creative（纯广告 / 商业素材）

A future advertiser may also want a direct Advertisement（广告） that is not pretending to be an article.

Examples may include:
- display advertising（展示广告）;
- sponsored cards（赞助卡片）;
- commercial campaign units（商业活动单元）;
- other clearly commercial creative（其他明确商业素材）.

This route should remain semantically separate from ordinary publication objects.

A pure Advertisement（广告） should not need to masquerade as Community Publication（社区发布物） or Editorial Work（编辑作品） to receive commercial distribution.

Exact ad formats, inventory, targeting, pricing and auction mechanics remain completely deferred.

---

## 4. Shared integrity rules（共同完整性规则）

Both routes must preserve:
- clear Actor / Sponsor（主体 / 赞助方） attribution;
- explicit paid/commercial exposure context（明确付费 / 商业曝光情境）;
- separation from Work Recognition（作品认可）;
- separation from Editorial Selection（编辑选择）;
- separation from source/truth authority（来源 / 真相权威）;
- no hidden organic-ranking purchase（不得暗中购买自然排序）.

---

## 5. Current decision question（当前决策题）

Should Ink & East（墨与东方） preserve **both** commercial-distribution routes over time:

1. **Content-first Paid Amplification（内容优先的付费加热）** for already-eligible substantive content;
2. **Pure Advertisement / Commercial Creative（纯广告 / 商业素材）** for direct advertising;

instead of forcing advertisers and content publishers into one product path?
