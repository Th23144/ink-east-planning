## 1. User's explicit product vision（用户明确表达的平台愿景）
The user emphasizes: the source text remains the source text; readers can legitimately reach many different conclusions from the same classical material. “A thousand readers have a thousand Hamlets（千人千个哈姆雷特）.” Publishing, exploring and openly discussing one's own understanding of the classics is a core attraction of Ink & East（墨与东方）, not a defect that the platform should adjudicate away.

**User-origin direction（源于用户的方向）:** preservation of source material + open plurality of interpretation and public conversation（保存原典材料 + 多元诠释开放发表和公众交流）.

## 2. Separate documentary fidelity from interpretation（原典忠实与诠释开放分离）
### Source/Witness layer（原始材料 / 文献见证层）
Preserve sourced image/facsimile（影像 / 原貌）, edition/witness（版本 / 底本）, archival metadata（馆藏元数据）, transcription（转录） and correction history（纠错历史） with fidelity and provenance.

Important nuance: different authentic editions/witnesses may contain legitimate variants（真实古籍不同版本可能存在异文）. Source integrity means faithful representation of **each specified witness（每个明确版本 / 底本）**, not imposing one universal “the original text（唯一原文）” across every edition.

Correct objectively demonstrable transcription, cataloguing, citation and attribution mistakes where evidence supports the change（有证据时纠正可核实的转录、编目、引用和归属错误）, preserving historical source truth and change reasons（保留来源事实和修改原因）.

### Interpretation layer（理解 / 诠释层）
Readings, translations-as-interpretations（解释性翻译）, philosophical views, teaching approaches, personal responses and scholarly analyses（哲学观点、教学理解、个人感悟和研究论证） can coexist and evolve.

Different interpretations are **not** automatically:
- Errors（错误）;
- Disputes requiring formal adjudication（需要正式裁决的争议）;
- Moderation incidents（审核违规事件）;
- Negative public badges（负面公开标签）;
- Reasons to revoke recognition（撤销认可的理由）.

Users may voluntarily publish readings and discuss disagreements with evidence, curiosity and respectful dialogue（可以公开发表理解，并依据证据展开讨论）. No automatic mandatory debate panel or consensus verdict（无需默认进入裁决组 / 寻求统一结论）.

## 3. Three independent axes（独立的三个维度）
1. **Source Fidelity（来源忠实）** — whether cited materials, versions and documentary records are represented accurately（引用 / 版本 / 文献记录是否忠实）;
2. **Interpretive Diversity（诠释多元）** — the substantive viewpoints and analyses people present（人们提出的不同理解、分析与观点）;
3. **Participation Conduct（参与行为）** — fraud/forgery/harassment/other harm governed separately（伪造、欺诈、骚扰和其他伤害另行按行为规则处理）.

Work Recognition（作品认可） may evaluate craft, argument clarity, originality, contextual care and source usage（作品质量、论证清晰度、原创性、上下文与来源运用） without granting final truth over interpretation（不能因此授予最终解释真理）.

## 4. Product implications for later exploration（后续可探索的产品含义）
A passage or source witness may serve as a common anchor（同一文献段落作为共同锚点） to discover:
- source text and editions（原文和不同底本）;
- multiple independent interpretations（多种独立解读）;
- attributed translations/commentaries（标明作者与来源的翻译 / 注释）;
- discussion and replies（讨论 / 回复）;
- new interpretations published over time（随时间新增的理解）.

These are conceptual possibilities, **not approved interfaces or implementation requirements（仅概念可能性，非已批准界面或实现需求）**.

No automatic ranking of interpretations as true/false or obligatory warning merely because they differ（不能仅因解释不同就自动排序真假或挂警告）. Quality/discovery/recommendation remain separate from truth/authority（质量 / 发现 / 推荐与事实真相 / 权威分离）.

## 5. Correction outcomes remain only for genuine correction cases（纠错结论仅用于真正的纠错案件）
The earlier five-result Outcome Taxonomy（结案结果分类） incorrectly risked treating ordinary interpretive plurality as a “Supported Dispute（确认争议）” case.

Reframe into two distinct paths:
- **Source / fact maintenance（来源 / 事实维护）**: confirm source-record errors, acknowledge genuine unresolved source provenance, or decline unsupported factual correction requests（纠正文献事实、说明真正未决的来源问题、驳回无依据的事实纠错）;
- **Interpretation / exchange（理解 / 交流）**: publish, compare, extend and discuss interpretations without mandatory case closure, platform truth verdict or public dispute flags（发表、比较、拓展和讨论解释，而无需默认结案、判定解释真理或挂争议标识）.

Only independently demonstrated misconduct enters the distinct Moderation（行为审核） process.

## 6. Next questions, not yet decided（后续需要讨论、尚未决定）

- How to present multiple readings without creating a “platform official answer（平台官方正确答案）”?
- What qualifies as objective source error vs open interpretive disagreement（如何区分客观来源错误和开放理解分歧）?
- Should formally commissioned/platform-written explanatory pieces clearly identify themselves as such without silencing independent interpretations（平台编辑解读如何标注自身身份且不压制民间诠释）?
- How to support respectful disagreement while maintaining baseline safety rules（如何在基础安全规则下保护充分辩论）?
