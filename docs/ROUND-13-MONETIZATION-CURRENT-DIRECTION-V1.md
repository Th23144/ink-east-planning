# Round 13（第十三轮）— Ink & East Monetization Current Direction V1
# 墨与东方商业化当前方向 V1

> **Status（状态）:** CURRENT DIRECTION / PRODUCT ARCHITECTURE ONLY（当前方向 / 仅产品架构）
> **Implementation（实现）:** NOT AUTHORIZED（未授权）
> **Merge（合并）:** NOT AUTHORIZED（未授权）

## 1. Core commercial posture（核心商业姿态）

Ink & East（墨与东方） is not currently defined as a first-party service-selling business（第一方服务销售业务）.

The current long-term commercial direction is:

> **Platform Monetization（平台型商业化）**
> + **Vertical Partner Economy（垂直合作生态）**
> + **optional Project / First-party Monetization（可选项目型 / 第一方商业化）**

Membership（会员） remains a separate product question.

## 2. Platform Monetization（平台型商业化）

Future monetization may include:
- advertising（广告）;
- sponsorship（赞助）;
- Paid Amplification（付费加热）;
- other clearly represented commercial distribution（其他明确标识的商业分发）.

Exact advertising products, inventory, pricing and targeting are not yet defined.

Paid/commercial distribution must remain distinct from:
- Organic Recommendation（自然推荐）;
- Editorial Selection（编辑选择）;
- Work Recognition（作品认可）;
- source/truth authority（来源 / 真相权威）.

## 3. Vertical Partner Economy（垂直合作生态）

Ink & East（墨与东方） may create commercial value because it aggregates contextually concentrated user intent（有明确语境的集中用户意图）.

Potential future verticals may include:
- Travel（旅游）;
- Education / Teaching（教育 / 教学）;
- cultural experiences（文化体验）;
- publishing / events（出版 / 活动）;
- craft / maker ecosystems（工艺 / 制作者生态）;
- other future domains.

These are examples, not committed product verticals.

## 4. Commercial actor is not a separate caste（商业主体不是独立用户阶级）

An Organization（组织） may simultaneously:
- be a normal platform actor;
- publish eligible substantive content;
- participate in Community（社区）;
- have a commercial relationship with Ink & East（墨与东方）;
- buy future Paid Amplification（付费加热） or advertising products.

Commercial Customer（商业客户） is a relationship/capacity, not a separate platform caste.

## 5. Content and distribution remain separate（内容与分发保持分离）

A commercial Organization（商业组织） may publish a normal substantive article if the Work（作品） itself satisfies the applicable content rules.

Limited self-reference or commercial mention does not automatically make the entire Work（作品） a pure Advertisement（广告）.

Pure advertising must not be disguised as ordinary substantive content.

Paid Amplification（付费加热） is a Distribution Treatment（分发处理）, not a quality / authority / recognition status（质量 / 权威 / 认可状态）.

## 6. Commercial conversion depth（商业转化深度）

Different verticals may support different depths:

- Discovery / Outbound（发现 / 外跳）;
- Intent / Lead Capture（意图 / 线索承接）;
- Booking / Transaction Enablement（预订 / 交易承载）.

Level 2–3（第二至第三级） are architecturally possible when independently justified.

Marketplace / Intermediation（市场平台 / 交易中介） is not assumed and requires a separate future decision.

## 7. First-party products/services（第一方产品 / 服务）

Future first-party paid products/services may exist if a real user/platform need emerges.

No service catalogue is currently required.

Rejected legacy products remain rejected:
- Custom Reading（定制解读）;
- Custom Ebook Studio（定制电子书工作室）.

Commission（约稿） remains historical / superseded terminology（历史 / 已取代术语）.

## 8. What remains deferred（仍暂缓）

Not yet defined:
- ad formats;
- ad inventory;
- pricing;
- targeting;
- partner packages;
- lead fees;
- booking/payment flows;
- revenue share;
- transaction fees;
- Marketplace（市场平台）;
- first-party paid service catalogue;
- Membership（会员） product definition.
