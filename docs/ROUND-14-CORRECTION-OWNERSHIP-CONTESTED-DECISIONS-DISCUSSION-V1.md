## Scope guardrail（适用范围约束）
This discussion applies to substantive research/source-backed, accountable editorial or otherwise genuinely consequential knowledge claims（适用于实质研究 / 来源型、承担责任的编辑内容或其他确有重要影响的知识主张）. Ordinary Community / User（普通社区 / 用户） works ordinarily follow author editing, ordinary discussion and baseline conduct reporting（普通作者自行修改、正常讨论、基础违规举报）; they do not require scholarly source adjudication merely because someone disagrees（不会仅因观点不同就要求学术来源裁定）. **Recognition（认可） alone is not authority（认可本身不等于权威）**.

## 1. The next substantive question（下一个实质性问题）
The prior round resolved which knowledge issues exist and the broad correction lifecycle. The remaining problem is not simply “can someone report an error（能不能举报错误）” but:

> **Who may amend the underlying record, who must be consulted, who can contest a change, and what happens when the owner and reviewer disagree?（谁能修改底层记录、谁必须被征询、谁可以提出异议，以及作者与复核者意见不一致时怎么办？）**

This cannot be answered with one global Reviewer（审查者） role because Ink & East（墨与东方） has different content classes and different authority sources.

## 2. Distinguish report, edit, and decision（区分反馈、修改和裁定）
A Report / Correction Proposal（反馈 / 纠错建议） is **not** by itself permission to overwrite another actor's work.
An Author Revision（作者修订） is **not** by itself a final determination of external sources' truth.
An Editorial Decision（编辑决定） is **not** universal governance authority over every community work.

Potential separation:
- **Submitter（提出者）** identifies an issue and supplies evidence where possible;
- **Content Owner / Responsible Publisher（内容所有者 / 责任发布者）** has appropriate opportunity to respond or self-correct;
- **Domain Reviewer（领域复核者）** examines the contested source/evidence where necessary;
- **Platform Safety Reviewer（平台安全复核者）** acts only if distinct misconduct / safety issues are independently present.

Roles describe responsibility within this case, not a permanent cross-platform caste.

## 3. Decision rights by content class（按内容类别配置决定权）
- **Canonical / Source-backed（规范 / 来源型）**: authoritative source metadata, edition evidence and documented scholarly uncertainty should control corrections; attribution/edition histories must not be silently overwritten.
- **Editorial / Teaching（编辑 / 教学）**: editors maintain editorial accountability; the responsible team may correct its publication with a change record and explain declined evidence.
- **Contributor（贡献者）**: the author should normally have a voice in proposed changes to the authored work, while platform qualification/Recognition（认可） remains independently governed.
- **Community / User（社区 / 用户）**: users usually edit their own ordinary posts; the platform may label, limit or remove content under applicable rules without impersonating authors or secretly rewriting their stated opinion.

Exact tools and rights need separate implementation design.

## 4. No self-judge monopoly in consequential contested cases（重要争议避免单方自判）
An author should not be able to suppress supported corrections indefinitely simply by refusing to agree. Equally, a single reviewer should not be able to recast a good-faith interpretive disagreement as proven fabrication without evidence.

For important unresolved contested cases, propose a route to:
- preserving the author's original interpretation where lawful/appropriate（适当时保留原作者解释）;
- adding a sourced dispute / qualification annotation（添加有证据的争议 / 限定说明）;
- obtaining review independent of the original deciding party where impact warrants it（影响较大时由不同责任方复核）;
- revisiting the record on material new evidence（出现实质新证据时重新审视）.

## 5. Transparency and proportion（透明与比例）
The user's public experience should be understandable:
- a specific correction for a verifiable error（可验证错误明确纠正）;
- a dated revision note for meaningful updates（重要更新附时间和修订说明）;
- a bounded disputed/uncertain label where evidence remains contested（证据有争议时适度标注）;
- a clear route to respond or appeal consequential decisions（重大处理可回应 / 申诉）.

Avoid putting every ordinary post through scholarly fact adjudication（避免对每条普通帖子实施学术事实裁判）. Never publish private evidence merely to make the decision appear more transparent（不能为展示透明而公开私密证据）.

## 6. First decision set for in-scope consequential cases（适用范围内重要案件的第一组待确认问题）
1. Separate **Report / Edit / Decision（反馈 / 修改 / 决定）** as distinct capabilities; a report cannot itself overwrite another party's work.
2. Assign correction responsibility **by content class（按内容类别）**, not through one global reviewer role.
3. In genuinely accountable/source-relevant cases（确有知识责任 / 来源责任的案件）, give authors a reasonable chance to respond to substantial corrections, **but not an unlimited veto（不享有无限否决权）**.
4. For consequential interpretive/source disputes, prefer evidence-backed annotations and independent escalation when justified over forced one-sided deletion（有证据的争议标注与必要时独立升级，优于强制单方删除）.
5. Preserve clear public correction/dispute notes where relevant, with privacy and proportionate treatment（必要时公开简明说明，同时保护隐私并按比例处理）.
