## 1. Core principle（核心原则）

The user confirms the following relationship principle:

> **Persistent cooperation, explicit crossover, independent authority.（合作长期存在；跨界行为显式；权威与责任保持独立。）**

This principle governs the Standing Strategic Relationship（持续战略关系） between Ink & East（墨与东方）and Spatial Flow（空间流）.

---

## 2. What the Standing Strategic Relationship（持续战略关系）means

The relationship may remain continuously real even when no joint project is active.

It may support:
- long-term strategic awareness（长期战略互相了解）;
- recurring collaboration channels（持续合作通道）;
- accumulated cooperation history（持续积累合作历史）;
- recurring research / material / craft / object collaboration（持续研究 / 材料 / 工艺 / 物件合作）;
- explicit knowledge ↔ object relationships where justified（有依据时建立知识 ↔ 物件关系）;
- earlier mutual consideration when a relevant project appears（相关项目出现时优先评估彼此是否适合参与）;
- conflict/disclosure awareness because the relationship itself is persistent（因关系长期存在而持续保有利益冲突 / 披露意识）.

This does not create blanket authorization（全面授权）.

---

## 3. Three relationship buckets（三类关系桶）

### A. Persistent standing layer（持续关系层）
Things that may remain active without a new project agreement every time:
- strategic awareness（战略互相了解）;
- collaboration familiarity（合作熟悉度）;
- recurring communication/collaboration channel（持续沟通 / 合作通道）;
- cooperation history（合作历史）;
- explicit relationship records between knowledge and physical-world projects where valid（有效的知识与现实项目关系记录）;
- conflict/disclosure awareness（利益冲突 / 披露意识）.

### B. Project-specific agreement layer（逐项目协议层）
Things that must not be assumed merely from the standing relationship:
- output ownership（成果所有权）;
- copyright / license（著作权 / 授权）;
- revenue sharing（收益分成）;
- procurement / supply（采购 / 供应）;
- pricing（定价）;
- exclusivity（独家 / 排他）;
- co-branding（联合品牌）;
- trademark use（商标使用）;
- commercial promotion（商业推广）;
- non-public research/data access（非公开研究 / 数据访问）;
- physical custody（实体保管）;
- publication rights（发布权）.

### C. Default independence layer（默认独立层）
The following remain independent by default:
- Editorial Judgment（编辑判断）;
- Work Recognition（作品认可）;
- Recommendation（推荐）;
- Knowledge / Source Authority（知识 / 来源权威）;
- Contributor Qualification（贡献者资格）;
- Governance（治理）;
- Membership（会员）;
- Account（账户）;
- Personal Data（个人数据）;
- Payment / Orders（支付 / 订单）;
- Customer Support Responsibility（客服责任）;
- Brand Voice（品牌声音）;
- Legal Responsibility（法律责任）.

Some default-independent systems may later support explicit, bounded interoperability（明确且有限的互操作） if separately designed. Independence is the default; crossover requires explicit architecture.

---

## 4. Hard anti-inference rule（硬性禁止推导规则）

Standing Strategic Relationship（持续战略关系） must never be interpreted as:

- automatic data sharing（自动数据共享）;
- automatic rights sharing（自动权利共享）;
- automatic commercial entitlement（自动商业权益）;
- automatic content preference（自动内容优待）;
- automatic editorial alignment（自动编辑立场一致）;
- automatic account unification（自动账户统一）;
- automatic brand merger（自动品牌合并）;
- automatic legal consolidation（自动法律主体合并）.
