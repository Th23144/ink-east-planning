# Round 4（第四轮）— Recovery Participation / Attribution Amendment V2（寻回参与 / 归因修订 V2）

> **Status（状态）:** USER-CORRECTED TARGETED CROSS-ROUND AMENDMENT（用户纠正后的定向跨轮修订）
> **Supersedes（取代）:** `ROUND-4-CULTURAL-CONTRIBUTION-ROLE-AMENDMENT-V1.md`
> **Applies to（适用于）:** Round 4（第四轮）Contributor / Identity（贡献者 / 身份）architecture
> **Implementation（实现）:** NOT AUTHORIZED（未授权）

## A1 — Reserve Contributor（贡献者）

Contributor（贡献者）keeps its original Round 4（第四轮）meaning:

> a scoped qualification identity（范围化资格身份） based on evidence of competence / sustained creation-research-practice capability（能力 / 持续创作、研究、实践证据）.

Do not use Contributor（贡献者）as a generic label for anyone who helps with cultural recovery.

## A2 — Recovery roles use separate vocabulary（寻回角色使用独立词汇）

Use:
- Recovery Participant（寻回参与者）;
- Recovery Role（寻回角色）;
- Recovery Attribution（寻回归因）.

Examples:
- Discoverer（发现者）;
- Source Provider（资料提供者）;
- Knowledge Provider（知识提供者）;
- Material Holder（资料持有人）;
- Donor（捐赠者）;
- Depositor（寄存者）;
- Translator（翻译者）;
- Research Collaborator（研究协作者）;
- Digitization Participant（数字化参与者）;
- Institutional Connector（机构联络促成者）.

Do not call these “Contribution Role（贡献角色）” or “Contribution Attribution（贡献署名）”.

## A3 — Hard separation（硬分离）

> **Recovery Role / Attribution（寻回角色 / 归因） != Contributor Qualification（贡献者资格）**

A recovery role may later be relevant evidence in a normal Contributor Qualification（贡献者资格）review if it genuinely demonstrates scoped competence, but it never grants qualification automatically.

## A4 — Existing Round 4（第四轮）meaning survives

Contributor Qualification（贡献者资格）, Expertise Scope（专业范围）, Identity Verification（身份验证）, Work Recognition（作品认可）, Reviewer Trust（评审信任）and Governance Authority（治理权）remain separate systems.
