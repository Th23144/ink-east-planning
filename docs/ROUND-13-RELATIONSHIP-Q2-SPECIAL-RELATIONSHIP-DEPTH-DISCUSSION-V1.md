## 1. The core distinction（核心区分）

Two different “ecosystems（生态）” must not be confused.

### Platform Ecosystem（平台生态）
Ink & East（墨与东方）may contain many independent people, organizations, institutions, brands and communities.

Inside this ecosystem, Spatial Flow（空间流）is one legitimate actor and receives no automatic hidden privilege.

### Cross-product Strategic Ecosystem（跨产品战略生态）
Ink & East（墨与东方）and Spatial Flow（空间流）may separately maintain a deeper, persistent strategic relationship because of their complementary missions/capabilities.

This relationship may exist without changing ordinary platform ranking, Recognition（作品认可）, source authority（来源权威）or editorial independence（编辑独立性）.

These two layers must remain separate.

---

## 2. Three relationship depths to discuss（需要讨论的三种关系深度）

These are architecture options（架构选项）, not rankings.

### Model A — Project-only Partnership（纯项目合作关系）

No standing special relationship exists.

Every collaboration is independently created around a specific:
- research project（研究项目）;
- object/craft project（物件 / 工艺项目）;
- event/exhibition（活动 / 展览）;
- publication（出版 / 发布）;
- commercial project（商业项目）.

Between projects, the two products are simply independent actors.

Strength:
- maximum independence（最大独立性）;
- simplest conflict separation（最简单的利益冲突分离）.

Tradeoff:
- does not encode the user's long-term intention that the two products were conceived as naturally complementary（无法表达两个产品长期以来被设想为天然互补关系）.

---

### Model B — Standing Strategic Relationship（持续战略关系）

The two products remain independent, but there is an ongoing relationship even when no specific project is active.

Possible semantics:
- each product understands the other as a recurring strategic collaborator（持续战略协作者）;
- cooperation can be initiated more naturally because shared history/context exists（存在稳定合作历史 / 上下文）;
- selected knowledge ↔ object relations can be explicitly linked（部分知识 ↔ 物件关系可显式连接）;
- recurring research / material / craft / documentation cooperation may exist（可持续开展研究 / 材料 / 工艺 / 记录合作）;
- commercial/legal arrangements remain project-specific unless separately agreed（商业 / 法律安排仍按具体项目处理）.

Hard boundary:
> standing strategy does not create platform privilege（持续战略关系不产生平台特权）.

---

### Model C — Shared Corporate / Brand Ecosystem（共同企业 / 品牌生态）

The two products remain independent at product level but are publicly or legally connected through:
- a common parent brand（共同母品牌）;
- common ownership（共同所有权）;
- a group/company structure（集团 / 公司结构）;
- a formal public sister-brand relationship（正式公开姊妹品牌关系）.

This can coexist with Model B（持续战略关系）, but it is a different axis.

This model is **not decided** merely because the user is considering a future total brand / company name（未来总品牌 / 公司名）.

---

## 3. Preliminary architecture judgment（初步架构判断）

The current discussion supports a strong distinction:

> **Platform treatment（平台待遇）should stay ordinary/equal; cross-product cooperation（跨产品合作）may be unusually deep.**

This suggests that the most important decision is not whether Spatial Flow（空间流）gets special standing *inside* Ink & East（墨与东方）, but whether the two products should have a **Standing Strategic Relationship（持续战略关系） outside ordinary platform participation**.

If yes, that persistent relationship could later govern:
- recurring collaboration channels（持续合作通道）;
- joint-project initiation（联合项目发起）;
- cross-product semantic linking（跨产品语义连接）;
- research/material/craft resource exchange（研究 / 材料 / 工艺资源交换）;
- conflict/disclosure rules（利益冲突 / 披露规则）;
- shared long-term planning where appropriate（适当范围内共享长期规划）.

It still would not determine:
- one account system（统一账户）;
- one database（统一数据库）;
- one brand（统一品牌）;
- one legal entity（统一法律主体）;
- automatic content preference（自动内容优待）.

## 4. Decision question（决策题）

The immediate product-architecture question is:

> **Even when Ink & East（墨与东方）and Spatial Flow（空间流）are not working on a specific joint project, should they still be treated as long-term strategic counterparts with a standing relationship（长期战略对应方 / 持续关系）, or should every relationship exist only project-by-project（完全按项目临时建立）?**
