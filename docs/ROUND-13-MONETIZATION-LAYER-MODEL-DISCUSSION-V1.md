## 1. Reframed problem（重新定义问题）

The current question should no longer be:

> “Which first-party paid service should Ink & East（墨与东方） sell?”

The user's early ideas suggest a broader platform model.

A better architecture question is:

> **Which monetization layers should an audience + community + knowledge platform like Ink & East（墨与东方） be capable of supporting over time?**

This avoids forcing first-party services where platform/partner monetization may be more natural.

---

## 2. Candidate Layer A — Audience / Distribution Monetization（受众 / 分发商业化）

Possible future family:
- advertising（广告）;
- sponsorship（赞助）;
- clearly labeled paid placement（明确标注的付费展示）;
- other commercial distribution surfaces（其他商业分发表面）.

Role:
monetize reach, attention and context.

Hard boundary:
- organic recommendation（自然推荐） remains distinct;
- editorial selection（编辑选择） remains distinct;
- Work Recognition（作品认可） remains distinct;
- source/truth authority（来源 / 真相权威） remains distinct.

This layer is plausible but still deferred as a product.

---

## 3. Candidate Layer B — Vertical Partner Economy（垂直合作生态）

This is potentially more important than generic advertising.

Because Ink & East（墨与东方） may aggregate high-intent audiences around specific cultural domains, Places（地点）, Topics（主题） and practices（实践）, outside commercial/organizational actors may find the audience economically valuable.

Possible verticals may include:
- Travel（旅游）;
- Education / Teaching（教育 / 教学）;
- cultural experiences（文化体验）;
- publishing / events（出版 / 活动）;
- craft / maker ecosystems（工艺 / 制作者生态）;
- other future domain-specific sectors（其他未来垂直行业）.

Possible commercial relationships are **not yet selected** and could later include:
- sponsorship（赞助）;
- promotion（推广）;
- qualified lead generation（高质量线索）;
- booking / transaction enablement（预订 / 交易承载）;
- revenue share（收益分成）;
- commercial organization tools（商业组织工具）;
- other models.

Key principle:
> **The valuable asset is not “traffic” in the abstract; it is contextually concentrated user intent（有明确语境的集中用户意图）.**

---

## 4. Candidate Layer C — Project / First-party Monetization（项目型 / 第一方商业化）

Ink & East（墨与东方） may sometimes monetize:
- joint projects（联合项目）;
- institutional collaborations（机构合作）;
- exhibitions / events / experiences（展览 / 活动 / 体验）;
- licensing（授权）;
- research or production projects（研究 / 制作项目）;
- future first-party paid products/services（未来第一方收费产品 / 服务） if a real need is proven.

This layer is **optional**, not presumed to be the core business model.

No rejected legacy service is revived.

---

## 5. Membership（会员） remains separate

Membership（会员） remains a separate deferred Product Architecture（产品架构） subject.

It should not be used to fill gaps in the commercial model before its own product role is independently justified.

---

## 6. Preliminary architecture judgment（初步架构判断）

The user's ideas currently point toward this pattern:

> **Platform monetization first（平台型商业化优先）**
>
> **+ vertical partner economy（垂直合作生态）**
>
> **+ optional project/first-party revenue（可选项目型 / 第一方收入）**
>
> rather than forcing Ink & East（墨与东方） to become primarily a service-selling business.

This is a Working Model（工作模型）, not yet Current Truth（当前有效真相）.

---

## 7. Next decision question（下一决策题）

The next useful question is simple:

> **Should Ink & East（墨与东方） treat “audience/distribution monetization + vertical partner economy（受众 / 分发商业化 + 垂直合作生态）” as the primary long-term commercial direction, while first-party paid services remain optional and need-based（第一方收费服务保持可选、按真实需求再决定）?**

If confirmed, later discussion can focus on commercial integrity and partner/platform mechanics rather than inventing a service catalogue.
