# Membership Re-entry Review #1（会员重启检查 #1）— “Spiritual Shareholder” Community Concept Discussion V1
# 会员重启检查 #1——“精神股东”社区概念讨论 V1

> **Status（状态）:** ACTIVE EXPLORATION / LEGAL-SEPARATION REQUIRED（正在探索 / 必须保持法律分离）
> **Implementation（实现）:** NOT AUTHORIZED（未授权）
> **Merge（合并）:** NOT AUTHORIZED（未授权）

## 1. User idea（用户想法）

Early Ink & East（墨与东方） should intentionally reduce distance between platform and users.

The user is considering an internet-culture concept similar to “Spiritual Shareholder（精神股东）”:
- early registered users are treated as people who helped the platform exist/grow;
- supporters are especially important early participants / founding supporters（早期参与者 / 元老支持者）;
- the relationship should be concrete rather than empty marketing;
- early supporters may receive special treatment/rights/benefits over time;
- one speculative future idea is participation in advertising revenue（广告收益参与）.

This is an exploration concept, not a legal/economic commitment.

---

## 2. Product value（产品价值）

The concept has strong potential because it can make early users feel:

> “I am not merely an audience/customer; I was here while the platform was being built, and the platform remembers that relationship.”

Potential non-financial value may include:
- permanent early-participant / founding-history record（永久早期参与 / 创始阶段记录）;
- visible platform-history acknowledgment（平台历史致谢）;
- invitations to product research / feedback sessions（产品研究 / 反馈邀请）;
- early access to selected experimental features（部分实验功能优先体验）;
- project briefings / platform progress visibility（项目简报 / 平台进展可见性）;
- selected cultural activities / events（部分文化活动 / 事件）;
- future partner-ecosystem courtesy（未来合作生态礼遇）;
- non-binding advisory participation（非约束性建议参与）.

These should not become:
- Governance Authority（治理权）;
- Editorial Authority（编辑权）;
- Work Recognition（作品认可） advantage;
- Contributor Qualification（贡献者资格）;
- source/truth authority（来源 / 真相权威）;
- organic recommendation priority（自然推荐优先）.

---

## 3. Important identity rule（重要身份规则）

Early registered users and financial supporters should not be converted into a prestige caste（身份阶级）.

Historical recognition is allowed; universal superiority is not.

A useful distinction is:

> **Founding/Early Participation History（创始 / 早期参与历史） != permanent platform privilege（永久平台特权）.**

---

## 4. Financial-return boundary（财务回报边界）

The phrase “Spiritual Shareholder（精神股东）” can remain a cultural/community metaphor only if its legal/economic meaning remains clearly separated from real equity/investment rights.

If supporter payment is linked to:
- a right to share platform advertising revenue（平台广告收益分成权）;
- profit distributions（利润分配）;
- dividends（分红）;
- appreciation/value-return promises（升值 / 回报承诺）;
- transferable financial interests（可转让金融权益）;

the arrangement may no longer be a simple supporter/member benefit and may enter securities / investment / revenue-sharing legal territory（证券 / 投资 / 收益权法律领域） depending on structure and jurisdiction.

Therefore no advertising-revenue-share promise is authorized by Product Architecture（产品架构）.

Any future financial-return model must receive separate legal/tax/securities review before public promise, sale, or marketing.

---

## 5. Safer economic-participation directions to explore（更安全的经济参与方向）

If Ink & East（墨与东方） later wants users to economically participate without treating supporter payments as investments, possible separate models to evaluate include:
- Creator Revenue Share（创作者收益分成） tied to eligible content/performance;
- Referral / Affiliate Reward（推荐 / 联盟奖励） tied to measurable referrals;
- project-specific paid contribution / bounty（项目型有偿贡献 / 奖励）;
- event/project compensation（活动 / 项目报酬）;
- partner benefits / credits（合作方权益 / 额度）.

These are compensation/reward models based on actual contribution or consumption relationships, not automatically a return on supporter payment.

They still require their own tax/contract/policy review.

---

## 6. Current working conclusion（当前工作结论）

“Spiritual Shareholder（精神股东）” is worth exploring as:
- community language（社区语言）;
- early-stage closeness mechanism（前期拉近平台与用户关系的机制）;
- historical belonging / founding participation concept（历史归属 / 创始参与概念）.

It should **not** currently be used as a promise of legal equity, profit share or investment return（法律股权、利润分成或投资回报）.
