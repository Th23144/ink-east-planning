§ 貳The premise

For people who have one book in them, and one only.

一　書

Most of the people who write to us are not professional writers. They are someone with a long and particular life, or a family whose history is about to be lost, or the founder of a small house that has finally earned the right to put its origin in print. They have the material for one book. They do not have the next ten years to learn how to shape it.

A memoir, written in the first person, with the editor sitting in the margins. A brand book or company book — not advertising, but the record of why a place came to exist. A private essay collection, kept for circulation among friends, family, or a small readership only the author knows. Sometimes a single long letter to a child. Once or twice a year, a piece of fiction that needed an outside hand.

The work is slow and the calendar is short. We take three of these commissions a quarter. Beyond that the editor cannot give the attention the form requires, and we would rather decline than thin the writing.

§ 肆What you keep — and what we don't promise

The book, the rights, the things we will not say.

Copyright

Two licences are available. By default — the Standard Licence — the client receives personal use of the final manuscript and any commercial use named in the scope letter; Ink & East retains its baseline authorial rights, editorial method, and right to display the project anonymously as a case.

Source material

Anything you give us — text, photographs, archive, interview audio, family stories — must be yours to give. Ink & East does not carry liability for third-party rights in client-supplied source.

Revisions

Every project includes two reasonable rounds of revision after the manuscript is delivered. Work beyond that scope is re-quoted by the hour or by stage.

Delivery format

PDF and DOCX are the default deliverables. An EPUB-ready file is available as an add-on. Print formatting, ISBN registration, distribution, and marketing are separate work.

What we will not promise

We do not promise publication, sales, an Amazon ranking, an offer from a publisher, a return on investment, or any commercial outcome. The studio is paid to make a well-written private book.

Briefs we don't take

Medical, legal, financial, and academic ghostwriting fall outside the studio's brief and are declined on receipt.

Pricing · placeholder copy

A custom ebook begins at a meaningful starting price — closer to a small new car than to a paperback — and is given as a fixed quote before any work begins.

No public price list, no tiered packages. Each project is quoted in step 叄 after the editor has read the brief.

§ 陸Questions we are usually asked

Eight things worth knowing before you write to us.

壹Who owns the book?

Under the default Standard Licence, the client owns personal use of the final manuscript and any commercial use named in the scope letter. Full Rights Buyout is quoted separately.

貳How many revisions are included?

Two careful rounds, after the first manuscript is delivered. Work beyond two rounds is re-quoted.

叄What format do I get?

PDF and DOCX, always. EPUB-ready is available if named in the scope letter.

肆What if I don't like the first draft?

The outline stage exists to prevent that. If the first draft strays from the agreed outline, the included revision rounds are how we converge.

伍Will I be credited as author?

Authorship and credit are named in the scope letter, not set by a default rule.

陸Do you accept anonymous projects?

Yes. Pseudonyms are fine; so is no public byline at all.

柒What if scope changes mid-way?

Out-of-scope work is re-quoted in writing before it begins.

捌How is payment structured?

By milestone: smaller projects 50/50; larger projects 30/40/30.
