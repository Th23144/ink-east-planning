壹

Before you write — what this is, and what it is not

何 為 · 何 非

Ask the Ancient Text is a reader-letter column inside a journal. It is not a forum, not a chatbox, not a comment section. You write a letter; the editors read it; if it belongs in the journal, they shape it, anonymize it, and publish it — sometimes weeks later, sometimes months.

We reply privately to every letter, even when we cannot publish. But we can carry only a small number into each issue. Please write when something is genuinely catching on you, not as a test of our response time.

— Service boundary · 服務邊界 —

Ask the Ancient Text is not: psychological counselling · medical advice · legal advice · financial advice · religious guidance. It is a reader-letter column for cultural, literary, educational, and editorial purposes. If you need professional help, please seek a qualified provider in your area.

Your letter

來 信

We reply privately to this address. Never shared, never published.

If your letter responds to something specific — an article title, a paragraph, an Issue number — note it here so the editor can find it.

Write as if you were writing to a person, not filling in a form. There is no word limit. The editors read carefully.

— Authorizations · 授權 · all required —
— Acknowledgement · 確認 · required —

By sending, you agree to the authorizations above. We never share your email. Your letter stays between you and the editors until you give permission otherwise.

— A note on pace —

We read every letter. We can carry three letters a month into the issue, sometimes four — we choose them slowly, and we always reply privately, even when we cannot publish. Readers may send up to one letter a month; Patrons up to three; guests are welcome to send a single letter when something is genuinely catching on you.

信

Your letter has arrived.

It is now in the editors' hands — on the desk, beside the tea, waiting for a quiet morning. We will read it carefully.

Expect a private reply within one working week, sometimes sooner. If your question finds its way into an issue, we will write to you again before it is published.

Thank you for writing. The journal is better for it.
