On the way a room remembers the people who used to be in it.

By the editor · 8 min read · 12 May 2026

There is a particular feeling that arrives in the half-hour before guests come — when a room is finished but not yet used, and the air still belongs entirely to whoever lives there. We rarely write about that feeling, and yet it is the one most clearly designed by the objects we choose.
