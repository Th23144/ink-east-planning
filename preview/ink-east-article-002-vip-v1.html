Control, exhaustion, and the ancient art of not forcing life.

控之疲、與不強為之術

The modern self has been taught that effort is the universal answer — that one more push, one more plan, one more revision will eventually unlock the life it deserves. The classical Chinese tradition gently disagrees. It suggests, across two thousand years of patient writing, that some of the most important things in a human life arrive only when we stop forcing them. This essay walks slowly through that suggestion, and what it asks of a person who is already tired.
